## Overview

**I’m Orakzai** is a 200-page digital book exploring the relationship between identity, heritage, history, culture, technology and the future.

The book begins with a simple question:
> «What does it mean to say, “I’m Orakzai”?»

From that starting point, it moves through the wider Pashtun world, Orakzai history and culture, Pakistan's historical development, migration and diaspora, digital preservation, artificial intelligence, blockchain, entrepreneurship, digital sovereignty and the future of technology. The final chapters return to identity — not as a boundary, but as a foundation from which to engage with the wider world.

---

## The Core Idea

- **Identity** gives us roots.
- **History** gives us context.
- **Education** gives us capability.
- **Technology** gives us tools.
- **Responsibility** determines how we use them.

The project presents an individual and research-oriented exploration of heritage while emphasizing evidence, diversity, historical context and responsible scholarship.

## Research Philosophy

This project is designed around a simple principle: **«A meaningful story should remain open to evidence.»**

The project distinguishes between:
- **Documented History** vs. **Oral Tradition**
- **Scholarly Interpretation** vs. **Personal Perspective**
- **Technical Evidence** vs. **Statistical Data**

Where evidence is disputed or incomplete, the approach is to acknowledge that uncertainty rather than manufacture certainty.

## Technical Implementation

### Digital-First Publishing
The book is built using **EPUB 3** principles, utilizing **Semantic HTML** and **CSS** for a responsive and accessible reading experience.

### Logic Atlas & Interactive Graphics
Every page includes custom-designed **SVG logic diagrams** that visualize complex relationships between concepts. Over **10,000+ mini-graphics** and **200 hero diagrams** are integrated throughout the work.

### Page Generation
The book's pages are generated using a suite of Python scripts located in the `tools/` directory. Each script (`generate_pageXXX.py`) handles the content, research integration, and SVG generation for its respective page.
