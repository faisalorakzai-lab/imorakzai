DEDICATION

To the people of Orakzai,

to the elders who carried our history,
to the families who preserved our identity,
to the generations who endured hardship without losing hope,
and to the young people who will carry our story forward.

This book is dedicated to every Orakzai who remembers where they came from,
and to every young person who believes that heritage and progress can exist together.

May we preserve our roots,
learn from our history,
and build a future worthy of the generations before us.

I’m Orakzai.

— Faisal Orakzai
