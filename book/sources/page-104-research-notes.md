# Research Notes: Page 104 — The Future of Pashto

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 104: The Future of Pashto**.

## 1. Linguistic Classification & Vitality
- **Classification:** Pashto is an Eastern Iranian language, part of the Indo-Iranian branch of the Indo-European family.
- **Geographic Scope:** Primarily Afghanistan (national language) and Pakistan (regional language in KP and northern Balochistan), with a significant global diaspora.
- **Vitality:** While Pashto has millions of speakers, it faces challenges in formal education and official domains in Pakistan. However, it remains the dominant home and community language for Pashtuns.

## 2. Computational Linguistics & NLP
- **Terminology:** Pashto is classified as a "low-resource" language in Natural Language Processing (NLP) research.
- **Tools & Resources:**
    - **NLPashto:** An open-source NLP toolkit for Pashto (Haq et al., 2023) covering spell checking, word segmentation, POS tagging, and offensive language detection.
    - **Corpora:** Recent efforts have created POS-annotated corpora and paraphrase detection datasets (Ali et al., 2024).
    - **Challenges:** Dialect variation, orthographic inconsistency (modified Perso-Arabic script), and limited labeled datasets for deep learning.

## 3. Language Policy & Multilingualism
- **Multilingual Context:** Research in Lahore (Khalid et al., 2026) shows that Pashto is the desired language for home and community, while Urdu and English are viewed as languages of utility, social mobility, and opportunity.
- **Family Language Policy (FLP):** The family is the primary site for language maintenance and identity negotiation.
- **Education:** Mother-tongue education is recognized as beneficial for early literacy but faces implementation challenges in Pakistan.

## 4. Digital Future & Media
- **Digital Text:** Unicode support is essential for Pashto digital participation.
- **Social Media:** Platforms like Facebook, TikTok, and WhatsApp are major domains for contemporary Pashto use, creating new writing styles and increasing visibility.
- **AI Ethics:** AI can assist in translation and transcription but must not be used to manufacture historical or linguistic evidence.

## 5. Evidence Boundaries
- **No Statistics Invention:** Avoid inventing speaker numbers or growth rates.
- **Orakzai Specificity:** Distinguish between Orakzai-specific linguistic traits (if documented) and Pashto-wide features.
- **Scenario vs. Prediction:** Present future possibilities as scenarios, not guaranteed forecasts.
