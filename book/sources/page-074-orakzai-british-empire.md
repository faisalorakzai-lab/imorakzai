# Sources: Page 74 — The Orakzai and the British Empire

This document records the historical and academic sources supporting **Page 74: The Orakzai and the British Empire** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Agha, Sameetah.** (2021). "The Tirah Campaign, 1897–1898". *Queen Victoria's Wars: British Military Campaigns, 1857–1902*. Cambridge University Press.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive analysis of the 1897 uprising and the scale of the British response in Orakzai and Afridi territory.
   - **Claim Supported**: Details of the Tirah Expedition, the scale of forces, and the nature of tribal resistance.

2. **Hutchinson, H. D.** (1898). *The Campaign in Tirah, 1897-1898: An Account of the Expedition Against the Orakzais and Afridis*. London: Macmillan.
   - **Details**: Contemporary British military account providing specific dates, locations, and tactical details of engagements.
   - **Claim Supported**: Direct evidence for the 1897 military actions, including the siege of Samana posts and the storming of Dargai.

3. **Moreman, T. R.** (1998). *The Army in India and the Development of Frontier Warfare, 1849–1947*. Macmillan.
   - **Details**: Military history of frontier operations, specifically the 1891 Miranzai campaign against Orakzai clans.
   - **Claim Supported**: Details of the 1891 expedition and the securing of the Samana Range.

4. **Khan, Rashid.** (2020). "Geopolitics and Strategic Interests of the British in the Tribal Region". *Central Asia Journal*.
   - **Details**: Exploration of the strategic motivations (The Great Game) behind British frontier expansion.
   - **Claim Supported**: Strategic importance of the frontier as a buffer against Russian/Afghan influence.

5. **Dawar, S.** (2013). *The Orakzai Tribe: A Historical and Ethnographic Study*. Peshawar.
   - **Details**: Local historical perspective on the Orakzai's interactions with colonial powers.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai-specific tribal responses and the impact of colonial policy on local autonomy.

## Documented Terminology

| Term | Context | Historical Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Great Game** | Geopolitics | The 19th-century rivalry between the British and Russian Empires in Central Asia. |

| **Samana Range** | Geography | The strategic mountain ridge overlooking Orakzai valleys, site of multiple forts. |
| **Lashkar** | Resistance | A tribal militia or armed force organized for defense or attack. |
