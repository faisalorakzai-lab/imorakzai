## Verified Hydrological Data

- **Major Valleys**:
    - **Mastura Valley**: Central fertile tract; primary area for Orakzai settlement.
    - **Khanki Valley**: Bounded by the Samana and Karagh Ghar ranges.
    - **Khurmana Valley**: Western rugged corridor leading to Kurram.
- **Major Watercourses**:
    - **Mastura River**: Perennial stream flowing eastward into the Bara River.
    - **Khanki Toi**: Major watercourse flowing through the Khanki Valley, feeding the Kohat Toi.
    - **Khurmana River**: Tributary of the Kurram River system.
- **Hydrological Origins**: All major systems rise from the **Spin Ghar** (Safed Koh) near **Mount Mitta** (approx. 12,470 ft).
- **Terminology**:
    - **Toi**: Regional term for a stream or river (e.g., Khanki Toi).
    - **Seasonal Streams**: Numerous dry watercourses in the southwest that flow only during rainfall events.
- **Settlement/Agriculture**: Fertile valley floors support 11.5% cultivated land; irrigation is concentrated along perennial streams.
