# Sources: Page 84 — FATA & Its Historical Development

This document records the constitutional, administrative, and academic sources supporting **Page 84: FATA & Its Historical Development** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Constitutional & Legal Primary Sources

1. **Government of Pakistan.** (1956). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan*.
   - **Details**: Provisions for Tribal Areas under the first constitution.
   - **Claim Supported**: 1956 constitutional status of tribal areas.

2. **Government of Pakistan.** (1962). *The Constitution of the Republic of Pakistan*.
   - **Details**: Provisions for Tribal Areas under the second constitution.
   - **Claim Supported**: 1962 constitutional status of tribal areas.

3. **Government of Pakistan.** (1973). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan*.
   - **Articles**: 246, 247.
   - **Claim Supported**: Formal definition of FATA and the President's executive authority.

4. **National Assembly of Pakistan.** (2018). *The Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 2018*.
   - **Details**: Merger of FATA with KP and repeal of Article 247.
   - **Claim Supported**: 2018 constitutional merger.

5. **Government of Pakistan.** (2011). *Political Parties Order (Amendment)*.
   - **Details**: Extension of political party activities to FATA.
   - **Claim Supported**: 2011 political reforms.

## Administrative Records

6. **Government of Pakistan.** (1973). *Gazette of Pakistan (Nov 3, 1973)*.
   - **Details**: Notification of the creation of Orakzai Agency.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai Agency creation date.

7. **Election Commission of Pakistan.** (1996). *Extension of Adult Franchise to FATA*.
   - **Details**: Implementation of universal suffrage in the tribal areas.
   - **Claim Supported**: 1996 adult franchise milestone.

## Academic & Scholarly Research

8. **Gohar, S., Khan, Y., & Sajid, H.** (2024). "Exploring the Reforms Journey of FATA in Pakistan Through Various Regimes (1947-2018)." *Journal of Asian Development Studies*.
   - **Details**: Historical evaluation of FATA reforms.
   - **Claim Supported**: Long-term historical evolution of the region.

9. **Ullah, A., & Hayat, S. U.** (2018). "The merger of FATA with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: An historical analysis." *Journal of Political Studies*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of the 2018 merger and the 31st Amendment Bill (eventually the 25th Amendment).
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical analysis of the merger process.

## Research Gaps
- **Local Agency Gazettes**: Detailed tehsil-level boundary notifications from the 1973 Orakzai Agency creation are not always fully digitized in central archives.
- **Early Voting Records (1997)**: Specific turnout data for Orakzai in the first adult franchise election (1997) compared to other agencies requires more localized research.
