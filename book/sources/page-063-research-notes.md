# Research Notes: Page 63 — Pashto Proverbs

## Core Concept
- **Proverbs as Oral Wisdom**: Pashto proverbs (Mataloona) are compact vehicles for social experience, morality, and cultural memory.
- **Linguistic Context**: Orakzai Pashto belongs to the "Middle Pashto" or "Pakhto" dialect group, sharing phonetic characteristics with Bangash and Dawarwala dialects.

## Key Findings

### 1. Pashto Paremiology (Proverb Study)
- **Primary Source**: *Rohi Mataluna: Pashto Proverbs* by Mohammad Nawaz Tair (2009). This is the definitive collection of 1,350 proverbs.
- **Structure**: Proverbs are typically short, metaphorical, and context-dependent. They are transmitted orally across generations.
- **Function**: They serve as advice, warning, humor, or social commentary.

### 2. Orakzai Linguistic Context
- **Dialect Classification**: Orakzai dialect is classified within the "Middle Pashto" or "Pakhto" group (spoken in Orakzai, Kurram, Bannu, Karak).
- **Phonetic Markers**: Characterized by specific vowel shifts and consonant realizations (e.g., "sh" vs "kh" variations).
- **Regional Grouping**: Research in *Palatana* (2022) groups Orakzai, Bangash, and Dawarwala dialects based on phonetic similarities.

### 3. Thematic Categories of Proverbs
- **Wisdom & Experience**: "Time softens all grieves" (Wakt da har gham dawa day).
- **Honor & Identity**: "The modesty of women is the honor of men" (Da khaze satra da nari ghairat de).
- **Hospitality**: "First food, then talk" (Awal ataam, pase kalaam).
- **Patience**: "Patience is bitter, but its fruit is sweet" (Sabr talkh day kho mewa ye khozha da).
- **Origin & Blood**: "No error in the original, no loyalty in the base" (Asal ta khata nishta, kam asal ta wafa nishta).

### 4. Cultural Transmission
- **Oral History**: Proverbs are carried in family conversations, Hujra gatherings, and everyday speech.
- **Modern Adaptation**: Proverbs are increasingly shared via digital platforms (WhatsApp, TikTok), serving as a contemporary archive.

## Evidence Hierarchy
- **A. Orakzai-Specific**: Linguistic grouping of Orakzai/Bangash dialects; Middle Pashto phonetic markers.
- **B. Pashtun-Wide**: Common proverbs found in *Rohi Mataluna* and used across KP and Afghanistan.
- **C. Regional**: Dialectal variations specific to the southern/central tribal districts.
- **D. Contemporary**: Digital transmission and use of proverbs in modern social media contexts.

## Documented Terminology
- **Mataloona**: Pashto proverbs.
- **Middle Pashto / Pakhto**: The dialect group including Orakzai.
- **Melmastia**: Hospitality (a recurring theme).
- **Nang / Ghairat**: Honor (a recurring theme).
- **Sabr**: Patience.
- **Asal**: Origin/Lineage.
