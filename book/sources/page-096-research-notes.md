# Research Notes: Page 96 — Orakzai Across Pakistan

## Core Concept
- **National Overview**: This page synthesizes the individual urban stories (Karachi, Peshawar, Islamabad, Lahore) into a broader national picture of Orakzai mobility.
- **Mobility Taxonomy**: Distinguishing between voluntary economic migration and forced displacement (IDPs).
- **Identity Maintenance**: Exploring how Orakzai identity is carried and adapted across diverse cultural landscapes in Pakistan.

## Internal Migration in Pakistan (UNFPA & IOM)
- **Drivers**: Internal migration is influenced by employment opportunities, infrastructure, agricultural conditions, and access to services (education/health).
- **Urbanization**: Rural-to-urban migration is a major factor in Pakistan's urban growth, with megacities like Karachi and Lahore being primary destinations.
- **Labour Markets**: Migration redistributes labour staff to urban centers, impacting family structures and regional economies.

## Displacement and Return (UNHCR 2016)
- **Orakzai Agency Return Intention Survey (2016)**:
  - Specifically surveyed displaced populations from Orakzai Agency.
  - **Challenges to Return**: 27% cited destroyed houses as a major obstacle.
  - **Process**: Documentation of return intentions and the role of UNHCR/NGOs in return assistance (CRI kits, cash grants).
- **Distinction**: Forced displacement due to conflict (2009–2011) vs. voluntary migration for better livelihoods.

## Regional Mobility (KP Context)
- **Proximity**: Movement between Orakzai and neighboring districts (Hangu, Kohat, Peshawar, Kurram, Khyber).
- **Hangu & Kohat**: Primary regional destinations for immediate services, administration, and temporary settlement.
- **Peshawar**: The provincial hub for higher education (UoP) and specialized healthcare.

## Major Metropolises (National Context)
- **Karachi**: The vast commercial hub for the Orakzai diaspora, particularly in transport and retail.
- **Islamabad/Rawalpindi**: The national capital region, attracting professionals and federal government employees.
- **Lahore**: Punjab's historic metropolis, a destination for high-tier education and specialized services.

## Identity Synthesis
- **Mobile Identity**: A synthesis of origin (Orakzai), residence (City), family networks, and memory.
- **Trans-localism**: Maintaining property and social ties in the homeland while established in urban Pakistan.

## Research Gaps
- **National Census Breakdown**: Lack of sub-tribe level demographic data for Orakzai residents outside the KP province.
- **Total Diaspora Volume**: No official national count of Orakzai individuals living outside the district.

## Key Sources
