# Sources: Page 47 — Agriculture & Land

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 47: Agriculture & Land** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **King, Lucas White**: *Monograph on the Orakzai Country and Clans*.
   - **Publication**: Punjab Government Press, 1900.
   - **Details**: Historical overview of Orakzai tribal lands, traditional crops, and the relationship between territory and subsistence.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical significance of agriculture; relationship between land and tribal identity.

2. **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Agriculture Department / Government of KP**: *Orakzai District Profile*.
   - **Details**: Identifies Orakzai as a district with abundant walnuts and significant forest area (one-fourth of Merged Areas).
   - **Claim Supported**: Abundance of walnuts; forest area significance; major crops (wheat, maize).

3. **Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS)**: *7th Agricultural Census (2024) / Census 2010*.
   - **Details**: Statistical data on land use, farm sizes, irrigation, and livestock population.
   - **Claim Supported**: Land use statistics; livestock population; cropping patterns.

4. **World Food Programme (WFP)**: *Comprehensive Food Security and Livelihood Assessment (2020)*.
   - **Details**: Reports high food insecurity (>60%) and the prevalence of rain-fed agriculture in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Food insecurity levels; dominance of rain-fed farming; livestock role in livelihood.

5. **Asian Development Bank (ADB)**: *Federally Administered Tribal Areas Water Resources Development Project*.
   - **Details**: Documents the transition from subsistence rain-fed agriculture to irrigated systems in specific project areas.
   - **Claim Supported**: Prevalence of rain-fed agriculture; irrigation potential; land use in hectares.

6. **TNN English / Local News Reports (2023)**: *Orakzai Farmers Equipped with Certified Seeds*.
   - **Details**: Highlights the agricultural significance of wheat, rice, and maize in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Cultivation of rice; significance of certified seeds for food security.

## Verified Agricultural Data

- **Cultivated Land**: Approx. 24,153 hectares (approx. 15.7% of total area).
- **Forest Area**: Significant; Orakzai contains approx. 25% of the total forest area in the Merged Districts.
- **Major Crops**: 
    - **Wheat**: Primary winter crop.
    - **Maize**: Primary summer crop.
    - **Rice**: Significant in specific watered valleys.
    - **Walnuts**: Abundant and economically vital.
- **Livestock**: Mainstay of household economy; includes cattle, sheep, goats, and buffalo.
- **Irrigation**: Predominantly rain-fed (barani); irrigation concentrated along riverbanks and using traditional/modern wells.
- **Social-Economic Link**: Land represents food, inheritance, and tribal belonging (Page 40).
