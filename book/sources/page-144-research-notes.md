# Research Notes: Page 144 — Blockchain & National Infrastructure

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 144: Blockchain & National Infrastructure**.

## 1. National Strategic Context (2026)
- **Virtual Assets Act 2026:** A landmark piece of legislation enacted in early 2026, providing the legal foundation for digital assets and establishing the **Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA)**.
- **Banking Integration:** In **April 2026**, the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) officially lifted the 8-year ban on crypto-related banking, allowing licensed firms to access the traditional financial system.
- **National Bitcoin Reserve:** Strategic reports in May 2026 indicated Pakistan's intent to explore a national Bitcoin reserve as part of its digital sovereignty and reserve diversification strategy.

## 2. Distributed Infrastructure Applications
- **Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC):** The SBP is executing a phased pilot for a **wholesale CBDC**, utilizing distributed-ledger technology (DLT) to modernize interbank settlement and reconciliation.
- **Land Record Digitization:** Building on the **March 2025** launch, Pakistan has integrated blockchain into its land registration systems to ensure the integrity of digitized property records and prevent tampering.
- **National Stablecoin:** Confirmed plans to launch a sovereign stablecoin to collateralize debt and facilitate programmable payments within the national digital stack.

## 3. The Infrastructure Stack & Trust Model
- **Layers:** Users -> Wallets -> Apps -> Smart Contracts -> Network -> Validators -> Consensus -> Ledger.
- **Trust Shift:** Transition from purely institutional trust to a hybrid model incorporating cryptographic verification and protocol-based consensus.
- **Hybrid Architecture:** Emphasis on integrating blockchain with existing national databases (NADRA, SBP) rather than complete replacement.

## 4. Institutional Governance (PVARA)
- **Roadmap 2026:** Led by Chairman Bilal Bin Saqib MBE, PVARA has established standards for licensing, supervision, and the deployment of real-world blockchain infrastructure.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Focus on AML/CFT compliance, secure custody standards, and the legal recognition of smart contracts for enforceability.

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI & Blockchain Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** Advocating for the use of blockchain as a "shared record layer" for national infrastructure.
- **Vision:** Implementing secure, verifiable systems for land records, remittances, and digital credentials to empower the Orakzai community and the wider nation.
