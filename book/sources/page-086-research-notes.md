# Research Notes: Page 86 — Administrative Evolution

## Core Concept
- **Layers of Authority**: Orakzai's administrative history is a sequence of overlapping systems, from pre-modern tribal norms to colonial indirect rule, the FATA Agency model, and modern provincial district governance.
- **Institutional Persistence**: While laws and names changed (FCR to KP laws, Agency to District), the underlying community structures (jirga, kinship) often persisted as informal layers of authority.
- **The 2018 Pivot**: The 25th Amendment was the most significant administrative shift in over a century, marking the end of the "special" frontier status.

## Administrative Timeline & Phases

### 1. Pre-Modern Local Authority (Pre-1849)
- **Decentralized Power**: No central state bureaucracy. Authority was distributed among family heads, clan elders, and the collective jirga.
- **Social Contract**: Based on Pashtunwali and local custom (*Riwaj*).

### 2. Colonial Frontier Administration (1849–1947)
- **Indirect Rule**: The British managed the frontier through Political Agents.
- **FCR 1901**: Provided the legal framework for collective responsibility.
- **Intermediaries**: Formalization of the *Malik* system to bridge the gap between the state and the tribe.

### 3. The Political Agency Era (1947–2018)
- **Constitutional Continuity**: Pakistan inherited the Agency system.
- **Orakzai Agency (1973)**: Established on Dec 1, 1973, from FR Kohat.
- **Executive Primacy**: The Political Agent (PA) held supreme administrative and judicial authority within the Agency.

### 4. Provincial Integration (Post-2018)
- **25th Amendment**: Repealed Article 247; merged FATA with KP.
- **Orakzai District**: Transition from PA to Deputy Commissioner (DC).
- **Extension of State**: KP Police (*Levies/Khasadars* integrated into police) and regular courts replaced the FCR system.

## Key Administrative Shifts

| Feature | Agency Era (Pre-2018) | District Era (Post-2018) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Code** | FCR 1901 | Constitution / KP Laws |
| **Admin Head** | Political Agent (PA) | Deputy Commissioner (DC) |
| **Judiciary** | PA / Jirga (under FCR) | District & Sessions Judge |
| **Police** | Levies / Khasadars | KP Police |
| **Revenue** | No formal land settlement | Ongoing Land Settlement |

## Research Gaps
- **Early PA Records**: Detailed day-to-day administrative correspondence from the first years of Orakzai Agency (1973-1975) is largely archival and not fully digitized.
- **Land Settlement Progress**: Exact percentage of land surveyed in Orakzai District as of 2026 requires current KP Revenue Department data.

## Author's Reflection Themes
- **The Speed of Change**: Laws change in a day; institutions take years; memories last generations.
- **Service vs. Rule**: The shift from "ruling" a frontier to "serving" a district is the core challenge of the merger.
- **Identity Resilience**: Administrative lines do not define the Orakzai people; they only define how the state sees them.
