# Research Notes: Page 84 — FATA & Its Historical Development

## Core Concept
- **Historical Evolution**: FATA was not a static entity; it was a dynamic administrative and constitutional region that evolved from colonial frontier policy into a part of the Pakistani federation, and eventually into integrated districts of KP.
- **Terminology Transition**: Shifting from "Frontier" (British) to "Tribal Areas" (1947-1972) to "FATA" (1973-2018) to "Merged Districts" (Post-2018).
- **Orakzai's Integration**: Orakzai's specific path from FR Kohat to a full Agency in 1973, and finally to a District in 2018.

## Key Historical Milestones

### 1. The Colonial Foundation (1849-1947)
- **Indirect Rule**: The British established a system of "indirect administration" using Political Agents and tribal intermediaries (Maliks).
- **FCR 1901**: The Frontier Crimes Regulation provided the legal framework for "collective responsibility," separating the frontier from the ordinary judicial system of British India.
- **Buffer Zone**: The frontier was treated as a strategic buffer between the British Empire and Afghanistan/Russia.

### 2. Post-Independence Continuity (1947-1972)
- **1947 Inheritance**: Pakistan maintained the colonial administrative structures to ensure stability.
- **1956 & 1962 Constitutions**: Both constitutions recognized the special status of "Tribal Areas," keeping them under the executive authority of the President/Governor.
- **FR Kohat**: Orakzai remained part of the Frontier Region of Kohat during this period.

### 3. The FATA Era (1973-2018)
- **1973 Constitution**: Formally defined "Federally Administered Tribal Areas" (Article 246) and established the President's executive authority (Article 247).
- **Orakzai Agency (1973)**: Created on Nov 3, 1973, by PM Zulfikar Ali Bhutto. It was the only landlocked agency without a border with Afghanistan.
- **Adult Franchise (1996)**: Extended to FATA, allowing all adults to vote for National Assembly representatives for the first time (previously restricted to Maliks).

### 4. Reform and Integration (2011-2018)
- **2011 Reforms**: Extension of the Political Parties Order to FATA and amendments to the FCR to include basic human rights protections (e.g., protection for children and elders from collective responsibility).
- **2016 FATA Reforms Committee**: Recommended the merger with KP to mainstream the region.
- **2018 25th Amendment**: Repealed Article 247, merged FATA with KP, and extended the jurisdiction of the Supreme Court and High Court.

## Author's Reflection Themes
- **Layers of History**: FATA's identity was built through successive layers of policy and law.
- **The Name "FATA"**: It is a modern term for an older institutional reality.
- **Integration**: The 2018 merger is the start of a new chapter, not the end of history.
- **Orakzai's Specificity**: Landlocked geography influenced Orakzai's unique administrative and social development within FATA.
