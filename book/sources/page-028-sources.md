# Sources: Page 28 — Pashto Poetry

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 28: Pashto Poetry** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **D. N. MacKenzie**: *Pashto Verse*.
   - **Author**: David Neil MacKenzie.
   - **Publication**: *Bulletin of the School of Oriental and African Studies*, University of London, Vol. 21, Issue 2, 1958, pp. 319–333.
   - **DOI**: 10.1017/S0041977X00072700
   - **Details**: Foundational academic study of Pashto metrical and poetic structures, including oral folk forms like the lanḍəy.
   - **Claim Supported**: Formal characteristics of Pashto verse and oral-literary transmission.

2. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *Folk Poetry & Khushhal Khan Khattak*.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Columbia University.
   - **Details**: Authoritative entries detailing oral folk poetic traditions and the literary contributions of classical figures.
   - **Claim Supported**: Classification of folk poetic genres and classical poetic themes.

3. **Sergei Andreyev**: *Pashto Literature: The Classical Period*.
   - **Author**: Sergei Andreyev.
   - **Publication**: *Oral Literature of Iranian Languages: Kurdish, Pashto, Balochi, Ossetic, Persian and Tajik*, I.B. Tauris, 2010, pp. 89–113.
   - **Details**: Scholarly examination of classical Pashto poetry, ethics, and Sufi mysticism.
   - **Claim Supported**: Classical Pashto literature, Khushhal Khan Khattak, and Rahman Baba.
