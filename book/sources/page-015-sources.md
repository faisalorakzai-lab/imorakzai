# Sources: Page 15 — Karlani Pashtuns

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 15: Karlani Pashtuns** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Encyclopaedia Iranica & Robert Nichols (*Oxford Research Encyclopedia*)**: *Karlani Tribal Classification*.
   - **Authors**: Robert Nichols and scholarly contributors.
   - **Publisher**: Encyclopædia Iranica Foundation & Oxford University Press.
   - **Details**: Defines the Karlani tribal grouping within traditional Pashtun genealogy and historical scholarship, outlining its regional distribution across eastern Afghanistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
   - **Claim Supported**: Karlani as a traditional genealogical and tribal classification framework.

2. **Charles M. Kieffer, *Encyclopaedia Iranica***: *“Afrīdī”*.
   - **Author**: Charles M. Kieffer.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Vol. I, Fasc. 6, pp. 579–580.
   - **Details**: Detailed examination of the Afridi tribe, its Karlani genealogical tradition, clan organization, and linguistic placement.
   - **Claim Supported**: Afridi tribal organization and Karlani association.

3. **Daniel Balland, *Encyclopaedia Iranica***: *“BangaṦ”*.
   - **Author**: Daniel Balland.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Vol. III, Fasc. 7, pp. 692–693.
   - **Details**: Scholarly analysis of the Bangash tribe, its diverse historical origins, settlement in Kurram and surrounding valleys, and incorporation into the Karlani framework.
   - **Claim Supported**: Bangash tribal history and Karlani integration.

4. **Historical Ethnographies & Linguistic Research**: *Karlani Geography and Dialects*.
   - **Details**: Studies examining the mountainous terrain (Tirah, Khyber) associated with Karlani communities and dialectological research in Pashto.
   - **Claim Supported**: Regional geography and linguistic diversity among Karlani-associated groups.
