# Sources: Page 68 — Youth in Orakzai Society

This document records the academic and contemporary sources supporting **Page 68: Youth in Orakzai Society** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Alam, J., Ayaz, T. U., & Aziz, T.** (2024). "The Role of Local Supporters in Promoting Social Cohesion in District Orakzai Khyber Pakhtunkhwa". *Human Nature Journal of Social Sciences (HNJSS)*.
   - **Details**: Qualitative study on how local supporters, including youth, stabilize the social fabric in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Youth as active participants in promoting community cohesion.

2. **Ullah, R.** (2025). "Countering extremism through the airwaves: The role of FM radio in peacebuilding in Pakistan's conflict zones". *University of Wah Journal of Social Sciences*.
   - **Details**: Research on the use of FM radio for youth engagement and peacebuilding in Orakzai and Waziristan.
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of modern media in youth civic participation.

3. **Social Media & News Reports**. (2024-2026). "Orakzai Spring Mela Documentation".
   - **Details**: Reports on youth sports achievements, including Sajawal Ali's 2026 gold medal in volleyball.
   - **Claim Supported**: Youth participation in sports and cultural festivals.

4. **NIPA Peshawar**. (2024). "Regional Educational and Economic Disparities and Their Impact on Poverty, Unemployment, and Political Instability in Pakistan".
   - **Details**: Analysis of the link between poor education and unemployment for youth in remote districts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Employment challenges and migration motivations for educated youth.

5. **Gallup Pakistan / Pakistan Bureau of Statistics**. (2023). "Literacy Between the 2017 and 2023 Censuses".
   - **Details**: Data showing female literacy in Orakzai remains below 30%.
   - **Claim Supported**: Educational barriers and literacy statistics.
