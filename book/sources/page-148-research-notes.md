# Research Notes: Page 148 — Financial Infrastructure of the Future

## Overview
Page 148 explores the evolution of financial infrastructure, focusing on the transition from traditional centralized stacks to programmable, API-driven, and AI-enhanced systems. It highlights Pakistan's 2026 milestones, including the massive success of Raast and the operationalization of digital banks.

## Key Findings
1. **Raast Dominance**: By early 2026, Raast has handled over Rs 20 trillion in transactions. Its P2M (Person-to-Merchant) and Bulk modules are fully operational, enabling instant salary disbursements and merchant payments across 1.09 million QR-enabled businesses.
2. **Digital Bank Rollout**: As of April 2026, three of the five licensed digital banks—Easypaisa Bank, Mashreq Bank, and HugoBank—are operational, offering full-fledged retail services without physical branches.
3. **Open Banking Era**: The SBP's Regulatory Sandbox, launched in January 2026, has shortlisted its first cohort to test Open Banking and API-based financial services, paving the way for a more competitive fintech ecosystem.
4. **AI-Integrated Risk**: AI has moved from a standalone app to an infrastructure layer, with banks using it for real-time fraud detection and credit scoring, significantly reducing settlement risks.
5. **Embedded Finance**: The 2026 landscape shows a surge in embedded finance, where payment and credit services are integrated directly into e-commerce and logistics platforms.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Stack Comparison**: The page uses a side-by-side comparison of the traditional financial stack vs. the digital financial stack to illustrate the architectural shift.
- **Raast as DPI**: Raast is presented as the "rail" for the national digital economy, connecting identity (NADRA) with value transfer.
- **Regional Inclusion**: The Orakzai context is used to show how this infrastructure removes geographic barriers, allowing the diaspora to send remittances and local businesses to accept payments instantly.

- Digital bank operational status (April 2026) is confirmed via Fintech News Singapore.
