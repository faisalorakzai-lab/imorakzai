# Sources: Page 22 — Ghairat — Dignity

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 22: Ghairat — Dignity** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Lutz Rzehak (Afghanistan Analysts Network)**: *Doing Pashto: Pashtunwali as the Ideal of Honourable Behaviour and Tribal Life among the Pashtuns*.
   - **Author**: Lutz Rzehak.
   - **Publication**: Afghanistan Analysts Network, 2011.
   - **Details**: Examines *ghairat* as one of the most complex tenets of Pashtunwali, encompassing dignity, self-esteem, pride, zeal, and moral resolve.
   - **Claim Supported**: Definition and complexity of ghairat within Pashtunwali.

2. **Noor Sanauddin (University of Glasgow)**: *Proverbs and Patriarchy: Analysis of Linguistic Sexism and Gender Relations among the Pashtuns of Pakistan*.
   - **Author**: Noor Sanauddin.
   - **Publication**: PhD thesis, University of Glasgow, 2015.
   - **Details**: Analyzes linguistic and cultural representations of gender, honour, and dignity in Pashtun society.
   - **Claim Supported**: Cultural vocabulary and gendered dimensions of honour/dignity.

3. **Dr. Anoosh W. Khan (University of Peshawar)**: *Pakhtunwali from a Gender Perspective*.
   - **Author**: Anoosh W. Khan.
   - **Publication**: *The News on Sunday*, 2014.
   - **Details**: Discusses cultural interpretations of Pashtunwali, evolving social norms, and the modern understanding of dignity and rights.
   - **Claim Supported**: Modern interpretations of dignity and social responsibility.
