# Sources: Page 78 — Orakzai & the Wider Frontier

This document records the historical, ethnographic, and administrative sources supporting **Page 78: Orakzai & the Wider Frontier** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Administrative Records

1. **Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.** (2024). *District Orakzai: Historical and Administrative Profile*. KP Board of Investment and Trade.
   - **Details**: Official profile documenting the 1973 establishment of Orakzai Agency and its 2018 transition to a district.
   - **Claim Supported**: Administrative timeline and geographic boundaries.

2. **Mills, H. W.** (1898). *The Tirah Campaign: Being the Sequel to the Pathan Revolt in North-west India*.
   - **Details**: Contemporary account of the 1897 uprising involving Afridi and Orakzai tribes.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical military cooperation and shared territory in Tirah.

3. **Gazetteer of the Kohat District (1883-84)**. *Punjab Government*.
   - **Details**: British colonial record describing the frontier tribes administered through Kohat.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical administration of Orakzai as part of "FR Kohat" and relations with Bangash.

## Academic & Ethnographic Sources

4. **Ahmed, Akbar S.** (1980). *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*. Routledge.
   - **Details**: Ethnographic study documenting Orakzai kinship, migration to urban centers, and economic connectivity.
   - **Claim Supported**: Seasonal migration (transhumance) and urban diaspora ties.

5. **Hussain, S. I.** (2009). "A New Look at Federally Administered Tribal Areas." *Ancient Pakistan*.
   - **Details**: Historical analysis of the FATA agencies' formation and tribal geography.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai as a landlocked agency and its naming tradition.

6. **P Plaza, D.** (2019). "Facebook and WhatsApp as Elements in Transnational Care-Giving." *MDPI*.
   - **Details**: Study on digital diaspora networks (generalized but applicable to the contemporary frontier context).
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of digital platforms in maintaining community ties across distances.
