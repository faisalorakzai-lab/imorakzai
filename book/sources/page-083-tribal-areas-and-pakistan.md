# Sources: Page 83 — Tribal Areas & Pakistan

This document records the constitutional, administrative, and academic sources supporting **Page 83: Tribal Areas & Pakistan** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Constitutional & Legal Primary Sources

1. **Government of Pakistan.** (1973). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan*.
   - **Articles**: 246 (Definition of Tribal Areas), 247 (Administration of Tribal Areas).
   - **Claim Supported**: Legal and constitutional framework of FATA prior to 2018.

2. **National Assembly of Pakistan.** (2018). *The Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 2018*.
   - **Details**: Act to merge FATA with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and PATA with respective provinces.
   - **Claim Supported**: Repeal of Article 247 and the 2018 merger.

3. **Government of Pakistan.** (1901). *The Frontier Crimes Regulation (FCR)*.
   - **Details**: The administrative and judicial code used in tribal areas until 2018.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical legal framework of the agencies.

## Administrative Records

4. **District Courts Orakzai.** (2024). "History of Orakzai." *Official Website*.
   - **Details**: Records the creation of Orakzai Agency on December 1, 1973.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai's transition from FR Kohat to a dedicated Agency.

5. **FATA Reforms Committee.** (2016). *Report of the Committee on FATA Reforms*.
   - **Details**: Recommendations for the merger and administrative integration.
   - **Claim Supported**: The political and reform process leading to the merger.

## Academic & Scholarly Research

6. **Khan, A.** (2011). "FATA: Voice of the unheard—path-dependency and why history matters." *Strategic Studies*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of the constitutional isolation of FATA and the impact of Article 247.
   - **Claim Supported**: Conceptual understanding of the special administrative status.

7. **Begum, I.** (2018). "FATA's merger with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: An historical analysis." *Pakistan Perspectives*.
   - **Details**: Historical overview of the merger process and the 25th Amendment.
   - **Claim Supported**: Chronology and impact of the 2018 constitutional change.

## Research Gaps
- **Local Agency Records**: Detailed internal correspondence of the Orakzai Political Agent during the 1973 transition is not fully accessible in public digital archives.
- **Village-Level Impact**: Quantitative data on the immediate impact of the 2018 merger on specific Orakzai tehsils (e.g., Upper vs. Lower) is still emerging in academic literature.
