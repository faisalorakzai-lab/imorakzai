## Research Notes on Geography & Landscape

- **The Heartland**: The Tirah region, specifically the Mastura and Khanki valleys, forms the historical and cultural heartland of the Orakzai.
- **Rivers**: The Mastura River and Khanki Toi River are the primary waterways, originating in the western hills and flowing eastward.
- **Mountain Ranges**: The region is defined by the Samana Range to the south and the Karagh Ghar mountains. The topography consists of high ridges separated by fertile valleys.
- **Boundaries**: 
    - **North**: Khyber District.
    - **South**: Kohat and Hangu Districts.
    - **East**: Peshawar District.
    - **West**: Kurram District.
- **Settlement Patterns**: Communities historically settled along the fertile banks of the rivers and on the slopes of the valleys where cultivation was possible.
- **Climate**: Varies significantly with elevation, from temperate in the higher valleys to more extreme patterns in the lower regions.
- **Modern Status**: Orakzai District (formerly Orakzai Agency) was merged into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in 2018 as part of the 25th Constitutional Amendment.
