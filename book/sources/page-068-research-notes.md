# Research Notes: Page 68 — Youth in Orakzai Society

## Core Concept
- **Youth as Active Participants**: Orakzai youth are not just passive recipients of tradition but active agents of social cohesion, innovation, and change.
- **Identity Synthesis**: Balancing Pashtun heritage with modern education, digital culture, and global aspirations.
- **Challenges and Opportunities**: Navigating unemployment, migration, and educational gaps while leveraging technology for cultural preservation and community leadership.

## Key Findings

### 1. Social Cohesion and Civic Participation
- **Local Supporters**: Youth play a significant role as "local supporters" in promoting social cohesion within the Orakzai district, stabilizing the social fabric during transitions.
- **Source**: *The Role of Local Supporters in Promoting Social Cohesion in District Orakzai* (J. Alam, 2024).

### 2. Digital Culture and Identity
- **Social Media Influence**: Social media has a multifaceted impact on Pashtun youth, influencing identity formation and providing platforms for cultural expression and learning.
- **Psychosocial Theory**: Studies apply Erikson's theory to understand how digital spaces help youth navigate identity vs. role confusion in a traditional society.
- **Source**: *The Influence of Social Media on Pashtun Youth in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's Digital Era* (2025).

### 3. Sports and Youth Development
- **Orakzai Spring Mela**: The 2024 and 2026 festivals documented high youth participation in traditional and modern sports, including wrestling, kabaddi, and volleyball.
- **Achievements**: Sajawal Ali from Orakzai won a gold medal in the National Junior Volleyball Championship 2026.
- **Source**: Social media reports and local news (Radio Pakistan, Instagram).

### 4. Education and Employment
- **Literacy Gap**: While literacy is rising, Orakzai remains below 30% for females (2023 Census), highlighting a significant educational barrier.
- **Employment Struggles**: Educated youth often struggle to find gainful employment in the stagnant local economy, leading to migration to urban centers like Peshawar, Kohat, and Islamabad.
- **Source**: *Regional Educational and Economic Disparities* (NIPA Peshawar).

### 5. Intergenerational Dialogue
- **Hujra and FM Radio**: Traditional spaces like the Hujra and modern ones like FM radio serve as platforms for youth to engage with elders and participate in community peacebuilding.
- **Source**: *Countering extremism through the airwaves: The role of FM radio in peacebuilding* (R. Ullah, 2025).

## Evidence Hierarchy
- **A. Orakzai-Specific**: Sajawal Ali's volleyball achievement; J. Alam's 2024 study on social cohesion; local festival reports.
- **B. Pashtun-Wide**: General social media impact studies; youth identity research in KP.
- **C. Regional**: Education and employment disparities in the Merged Districts.
- **D. Contemporary**: Digital archiving and AI use for language preservation.

## Documented Terminology
- **Kishar / Kishray**: Younger / Youth (social role in relation to Mashar).
- **Mataloona**: Proverbs (often reinterpreted or digitized by youth).
- **Attan**: Traditional dance (performed by youth at festivals).
- **Hujra**: Community guesthouse (a space for intergenerational learning).
