# Research Notes: Page 87 — Education & Development Challenges

## Core Concept
- **Development as a Multi-dimensional Process**: In Orakzai, development is not just about building roads; it is about the intersection of geography, institutional capacity, and human capital (education and health).
- **Resilience vs. Deficit**: While indicators show significant gaps (low literacy, infrastructure needs), the narrative must emphasize community resilience and the state's post-merger commitment through the AIP.
- **Evidence-Based Policy**: Using the latest 2023 Census and 2023-24 Annual School Census (ASC) data to provide a precise snapshot of the current state.

## Key Data Points (Orakzai District)

### 1. Population & Literacy (2023 Census)
- **Total Population**: 387,561 (100% Rural).
- **Gender Breakdown**: Males: 204,870 | Females: 182,684 | Transgender: 7.
- **Literacy Rate (10+ Years)**: ~33.57% (Total Literate: 83,690 | Non-Literate: 165,606).
- **Regional Context**: Orakzai remains one of the lowest-ranked districts in literacy, but has shown improvement from the 2017 FATA baseline (~23%).

### 2. Education Infrastructure (ASC 2023-24)
- **School Counts (Government)**:
  - Primary: 449
  - Middle: 83
  - High: 37
  - Higher Secondary: 5
- **Enrolment (Primary)**:
  - Govt. Schools: 8,058 (GER 43.01%)
  - Private Schools: 2,682 (GER 15.66%)
- **Student Flow (Primary Level)**:
  - Promotion Rate: 68.1%
  - Repetition Rate: 4.7%
  - Dropout Rate: 27.1%
- **Institutional Gaps**:
  - Significant shortage of female teachers and girls' schools beyond the primary level.
  - Infrastructure repair needs: ~480 rooms require reconstruction or major repair.

### 3. Health & Infrastructure
- **Primary Health Care (PHC)**: Reliance on Basic Health Units (BHUs) and Rural Health Centers (RHCs).
- **Secondary Care**: DHQ Hospital Orakzai (Mishti Mela).
- **Connectivity**: Road infrastructure remains a primary development goal to link remote valleys to markets and schools in Hangu and Kohat.

## Development Frameworks
- **AIP (Accelerated Implementation Program)**: The primary post-merger funding mechanism focusing on education, health, and rural infrastructure.
- **2026-27 Enrolment Campaign**: Provincial KP target to reduce out-of-school children (OOSC).
