## Source 10 — Evidence Rules Applied to Page 98

The page uses the following hierarchy. First, direct Orakzai evidence is reserved for public self-identification, named records, family testimony, or documented activity whose scope is explicit. Second, broader Pashtun evidence is used to explain historical and cultural context, especially migration routes and institutions. Third, Pakistani-wide and international data are used only for national or global demographic context. Fourth, informal digital evidence is described as a potential research site rather than a basis for membership counts. Finally, research gaps are labelled as gaps rather than filled with estimates.

A surname, language, appearance, nationality, or place of residence is never treated as sufficient proof of Orakzai identity. A single family is not automatically called a community; a single event is not a permanent association; and one Orakzai individual’s public leadership is not generalized into a global organization.
