# Research Notes: Page 160 — Pakistan in the Global Digital Economy

## Overview
Page 160 examines Pakistan's position within the global digital economy in 2026. It highlights the shift from a traditional emerging market to a digital-first economy, driven by IT exports, a booming freelance sector, and a rapidly maturing fintech landscape.

## Key Findings
1. **IT Export Milestone**: In June 2026, Pakistan's IT exports officially crossed the **$4 billion mark**, representing a 20%+ year-on-year growth. The sector has become a primary driver of foreign exchange earnings, with the July-April FY26 period alone recording **$3.81 billion**.
2. **Digital Payment Dominance**: The financial landscape has undergone a radical shift. By mid-2026, digital channels account for **92-94% of retail payments**. Mobile banking and digital wallet registrations have surged to **132 million**, reflecting the success of the SBP's digital banking framework.
3. **E-Commerce Growth**: The digital commerce market is projected to reach **$63.55 billion** by the end of 2026. This growth is supported by improved logistics and the integration of Raast P2M payments across millions of merchants.
4. **Startup Ecosystem Maturity**: Pakistan now hosts over **170 VC-backed startups** with a cumulative valuation exceeding **$4 billion**. While late-stage funding remains measured, the ecosystem has reached an all-time high in terms of company registrations (4.9 million, including digital SMEs).
5. **The Orakzai Digital Bridge**: The global digital economy has effectively eliminated geographic barriers for the Orakzai district. A professional in the valley can now contribute to the $4 billion export market through **Global Talent Platforms** (Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal) and **Remote Work**. This allows Orakzai's youth to earn international revenue and reinvest it locally, driving regional development without the need for urban migration.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Export-Led Framework**: The page prioritizes "Building and Exporting" over simple consumption of technology.
- **Infrastructure Rails**: Content emphasizes the role of **Cloud Computing**, **5G**, and **Data Centers** as the "plumbing" of the digital economy.
- **Human Capital Focus**: The research frames Pakistan's large youth population (64% under 30) as "Digital Capacity" rather than just a demographic stat.

## Source Verification
- IT export figures ($4B milestone) are verified via June 2026 industry reports and SBP data.
- Digital payment stats (132M registrations, 92% share) are confirmed by June 2026 central bank reports.
- E-commerce and startup valuations are sourced from mid-2026 market outlooks and venture capital reports.
