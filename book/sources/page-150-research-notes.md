# Research Notes: Page 150 — Building the Digital Nation

## Overview
Page 150 examines the structural shift from physical to digital infrastructure as the foundation of a modern nation. It details Pakistan's 2026 status under the **Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025**, focusing on connectivity, data governance, and universal access.

## Key Findings
1. **The Legal Framework**: The **Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025** is the primary legislative driver for 2026, establishing the mandate for a "fully digital government" and accelerating the digitalization of public administration.
2. **Connectivity Leap**: By August 2026, Pakistan's phased **5G rollout** has reached over 1,000 sites. Domestic internet connections have surged to **5.1 million**, a significant jump from 2024 levels, supported by a new regulatory framework for satellite internet.
3. **Sovereign Data**: The **National Data Governance Policy 2026**, finalized in June 2026, prioritizes data sovereignty and cybersecurity. This policy supports the establishment of geographically distributed **Tier III Data Centers** to ensure national digital resilience.
4. **Universal Access**: The 2026 strategy focuses on closing the **Digital Divide** by providing affordable access and digital literacy training. The goal is to train 1 million AI experts to lead the next phase of the digital economy.
5. **DPI as National Rails**: Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)—including identity, payments, and data exchange—is now viewed as critical national infrastructure, on par with roads and power plants.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Infrastructure Comparison**: The page uses a side-by-side comparison of the physical nation (roads, bridges) vs. the digital nation (connectivity, identity) to illustrate the evolution.
- **Resilience Focus**: The research emphasizes **Zero-Trust Security** and **Digital Resilience**, framing cybersecurity as a national security priority.
- **Regional Impact**: The Orakzai context is used to show how digital infrastructure (specifically satellite internet and 5G) provides the "last mile" connectivity for remote tribal regions.

## Source Verification
- The Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025 and 5G rollout site counts (1,000+) are verified against August 2026 industry and government reports.
- Data connection stats (5.1M) are sourced from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) 2026 updates.
- National Data Governance Policy 2026 status is confirmed via MOITT June 2026 releases.
