# Research Notes: Page 140 — The Future of Web3

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 140: The Future of Web3**.

## 1. Global Market Dynamics (2026)
- **Market Growth:** The global Web3 market is estimated at **$12.61 billion in 2026**, growing from $8.85 billion in 2025 (The Business Research Company). Longer-term projections suggest a CAGR of ~50%, reaching over $176 billion by 2034.
- **Identity Revolution:** The Decentralized Identity (DID) market is projected to reach **$7.4 billion to $7.9 billion in 2026**, driven by the adoption of W3C Verifiable Credentials (VCs) and Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) models.
- **Messaging & Tools:** Web3 messaging tools are emerging as a significant sub-sector, valued at **$1.7 billion in 2026**.

## 2. The Web3 Evolution Paradigm
- **Historical Framework:**
    - **Web1 (1990s):** "Read" — Static information, document-oriented, decentralized hosting.
    - **Web2 (2000s):** "Read + Write" — Interactive platforms, social networks, centralized data control.
    - **Web3 (2020s):** "Read + Write + Own" — Programmable ownership, decentralized coordination, independently verifiable records.
- **Hybrid Architectures:** The future of Web3 is increasingly seen as a hybrid model, combining centralized cloud infrastructure for performance with decentralized identity and blockchain for settlement and ownership.

## 3. Pakistan's Web3 Integration (2026)
- **Policy Shift:** In **April 2026**, Pakistan ended a 7-year crypto banking ban, signaling a major shift toward Web3 innovation and rank among top global adoption hubs.
- **Regulation:** The **Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA)** released the draft *Virtual Asset Services Regulations 2026*, providing a legal framework for Web3 startups and digital asset services.
- **Institutional Focus:** The **Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP)** is actively promoting a robust ecosystem for blockchain and digital finance through its Talk Series and regulatory sandboxes.

## 4. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI & Blockchain Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation exploring the transition to a decentralized internet.
