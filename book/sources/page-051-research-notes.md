# Research Notes: Page 51 — Orakzai Culture

## Core Definition
Culture is a living system connecting memory, language, family, and social institutions. For Orakzai, it is shaped by Pashtun heritage and the specific geography of the tribal districts.

## Key Sources & Findings

### 1. Ethnobotanical Knowledge (Scientific Reports, 2026)
- **Study**: "Tribal traditions and conservation perspectives of wild plants in Orakzai District, Pakistan" by Zamin et al.
- **Scope**: 123 species, 60 families.
- **Key Plants**:
  - *Daphne papyracea* (Traditional use documented)
  - *Olea europaea* subsp. *cuspidata* (Wild olive, culturally significant)
  - *Salix babylonica* (Willow)
  - *Berberis lycium* (Barberry)
  - *Hedera helix* (Ivy)
  - *Pyrola chlorantha*
- **Cultural Link**: Traditional ecological knowledge (TEK) is a core component of Orakzai cultural heritage.

### 2. Jirga System in Orakzai (Pashto Journal, 2025)
- **Study**: "Jirga System in Orakzai" by M. Khalil.
- **Findings**: Highlights unique features of Orakzai jirga compared to other regions while maintaining Pashtun essence.
- **Function**: Conflict resolution, community consultation, collective decision-making.

### 3. Social Cohesion & Elders (Human Nature Journal of Social Sciences, 2024)
- **Study**: "The Role of Local Supporters in Promoting Social Cohesion in District Orakzai" by Jan Alam et al.
- **Key Agents**:
  - *Jirga Council*
  - *Mashran* (Elders)
  - *Ulema-E-Kiram* (Clerics)
  - *Sportsmen*
- **Role**: Bridging social divisions, promoting mutual respect, conflict resolution.

## Evidence Hierarchy
- **Orakzai-Specific**: Ethnobotany (Zamin 2026), Jirga (Khalil 2025), Social Cohesion agents (Alam 2024).
- **Pashtun Regional**: Pashtunwali, Melmastia, Nanawatai, Hujra, Attan, Pashto poetry.
- **Former FATA Regional**: Impact of militancy on traditional structures, administrative transition.

## Conceptual Models
- **Culture Model**: Language + Values + Practices + Memory + Social Relationships.
- **Transmission**: Grandparents → Parents → Children → Youth → Future.
- **Adaptation**: Tradition ↔ Change (Education, Technology, Migration).

## Digital & AI Potential
- Pashto transcription, oral-history archiving, and digital preservation as modern tools for cultural survival.
