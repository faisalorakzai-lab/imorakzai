# Sources: Page 26 — Pashtun Brotherhood

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 26: Pashtun Brotherhood** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Joseph A. Tainter & Donald G. MacGregor**: *Pashtun Social Structure: Cultural Perceptions and Segmentary Lineage Organization*.
   - **Authors**: Joseph A. Tainter and Donald G. MacGregor.
   - **Publication**: Research report / academic study, 2011.
   - **Details**: Examines Pashtun social structure through the anthropological lens of segmentary lineage systems, hierarchy of social groupings, and social solidarity.
   - **Claim Supported**: Segmentary lineage organization and nested social relationships.

2. **Charles Lindholm**: *Kinship Structure and Political Authority: The Middle East and Central Asia*.
   - **Author**: Charles Lindholm.
   - **Publication**: *Comparative Studies in Society and History*, Vol. 28, No. 2, 1986, pp. 334–355.
   - **Publisher**: Cambridge University Press.
   - **Details**: Explores the political and social implications of kinship relations and the coexistence of cooperation and competition in tribal societies.
   - **Claim Supported**: Interplay between kinship structure, solidarity, and internal competition.

3. **Akbar S. Ahmed**: *Millennium and Charisma among Pathans: A Critical Essay in Social Anthropology*.
   - **Author**: Akbar S. Ahmed.
   - **Publication**: Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1976.
   - **Details**: Sophisticated analysis of Pashtun societal structure, balancing ideal cultural codes with real-world political and social dynamics.
   - **Claim Supported**: Social cohesion, tribal dynamics, and cultural identity.
