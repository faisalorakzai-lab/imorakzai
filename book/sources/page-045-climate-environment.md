## Environmental & Climate Data

- **Ecological Classification**: Transitional zone between **Subtropical** and **Temperate**.
- **Temperature & Elevation**: Mountainous terrain creates a gradient where higher western elevations experience cooler conditions and lower eastern/valley areas remain milder.
- **Seasonal Cycle**:
    - **Winter**: Significant snowfall in higher reaches (e.g., Tirah Valley); first snow often recorded in early November.
    - **Summer**: Temperate in upper valleys; peak monsoon influence.
- **Precipitation Mechanisms**:
    - **Western Disturbances**: Primary source of winter precipitation/snow.
    - **Summer Monsoon**: Influences seasonal rainfall patterns.
    - **Orographic Lifting**: Mountains alter airflow, leading to localized condensation and precipitation.
- **Flora & Biodiversity**:
    - **Total Species**: 123 identified wild species (60 families).
    - **Key Taxa**: *Daphne papyracea*, *Olea europaea* subsp. *cuspidata*, *Salix babylonica*, *Berberis lycium*.
    - **Conservation**: High vulnerability (37% of species) due to environmental pressure and over-harvesting.
- **Climate Change Trends**:
    - **Observed**: Increased unpredictability of monsoon/winter rains; regional warming.
    - **Risks**: Rapid runoff causing valley floods; agricultural pressure from rainfall variability.

## Research Limitations
- **Note**: District-level long-term climate observations are limited in the publicly accessible sources reviewed. National and provincial trends are used as indicators where district-specific measurements are unavailable.
