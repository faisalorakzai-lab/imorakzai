# Sources: Page 14 — The Pashtun Tribal Confederations

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 14: The Pashtun Tribal Confederations** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *General Tribal Classification and Structure*.
   - **Authors**: Scholarly contributors to the Encyclopaedia Iranica.
   - **Publisher**: Encyclopædia Iranica Foundation, New York.
   - **Details**: Outlines the structural framework of major Pashtun tribal confederations and their traditional genealogical contexts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Three-part confederational model (Durrani, Ghilzai, Karlani) and broader classifications (Sarbani, Ghurghusht, Bettani).

2. **Daniel Balland, *Encyclopaedia Iranica***: *“Dorrānī”*.
   - **Author**: Daniel Balland.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Vol. VII, Fasc. 5, pp. 513–519.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive scholarly analysis of the Durrani (Abdali) confederation, its historical development under Ahmad Shah Durrani, and its division into Panjpāy and Zīrak leagues comprising ten primary tribes.
   - **Claim Supported**: Durrani confederation history, leagues, and internal tribal components.

3. **M. Jamil Hanifi, *Encyclopaedia Iranica***: *“Ḡilzī”*.
   - **Author**: M. Jamil Hanifi.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Vol. X, Fasc. 6, pp. 670–672.
   - **Details**: Examination of the Ghilzai tribal confederation, historical chronicles, geographic distribution in eastern/southeastern Afghanistan, and scholarly caution regarding speculative origin theories.
   - **Claim Supported**: Ghilzai confederation history and historiography.

4. **Robert Nichols, *Oxford Research Encyclopedia of Asian History***: *“Pashtuns”*.
   - **Author**: Robert Nichols.
   - **Publisher**: Oxford University Press.
   - **Details**: Academic overview of Pashtun history, tribal organization, Karlani lineage groups, and the interaction between local tribal autonomy and state authority.
   - **Claim Supported**: Karlani grouping and regional tribal dynamics (including Afridi, Orakzai, Wazir).

5. **Academic Anthropology & Historiography**: *Genealogy versus Historical Reality*.
   - **Details**: Methodological distinction between traditional genealogical descent narratives and empirical historical-political processes in segmentary lineage societies.
   - **Claim Supported**: Confederations as frameworks of historical memory and alliance rather than centralized states.
