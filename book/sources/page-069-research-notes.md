# Research Notes: Page 69 — Education in Traditional Society

## Core Concept
- **Education Beyond Classrooms**: In traditional Orakzai society, learning was a holistic process integrated into daily life, family, and community spaces.
- **Holistic Transmission**: Knowledge was passed through observation, imitation, oral tradition, and religious instruction.
- **Key Institutions**: The Home (*Kor*), the Mosque (*Masjid*), the Madrasa, and the Hujra served as the primary educational environments.

## Key Findings

### 1. The Hujra as a Learning Hub
- **Social Education**: The Hujra is documented as a "Learning Hub" where young men learn social conduct (*Adab*), tribal history, conflict resolution, and community leadership.
- **Source**: *Preservation and Dissemination of Indigenous Knowledge in Pashtuns' Culture: The Role of Hujra as a Learning Hub* (2026).

### 2. Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK)
- **Wild Plant Knowledge**: Orakzai tribes possess deep traditional knowledge of wild food and medicinal plants, transmitted intergenerationally through practice and observation.
- **Risk of Decline**: Modernization poses a risk to this intergenerational transmission, highlighting the importance of documentation.
- **Source**: *Tribal traditions and conservation perspectives of wild plants in Orakzai District, Pakistan* (Scientific Reports, 2026).

### 3. Religious Education (Mosque & Madrasa)
- **Historical Hubs**: Mosques have historically served as educational hubs for religious literacy and moral guidance.
- **Orakzai Examples**: Specific madrasas like *Madrasa Anwar-ul-Islam* in the Orakzai Agency are documented as centers for Islamic education.
- **Source**: *The Prominent Leftist Leaders of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: An Appraisal* (GSSR Journal); *Jirga System in FATA-Pakistan*.

### 4. Family and Oral Tradition
- **Language and Values**: The home is the first classroom for Pashto language, family genealogy, and the core values of Pashtunwali.
- **Oral Archiving**: Stories, proverbs, and poetry are the primary tools for transmitting history and ethics.
- **Source**: *Pukhtun economy and society* (Akbar S. Ahmed, 1980).

## Evidence Hierarchy
- **A. Orakzai-Specific**: 2026 Scientific Reports study on wild plants; documentation of Orakzai madrasas; Akbar S. Ahmed's political agency records in Orakzai.
- **B. Pashtun-Wide**: Hujra as a learning hub; Pashtunwali transmission; oral tradition structures.
- **C. Islamic**: Mosque and madrasa educational frameworks; memorization as a tool.
- **D. Historical**: British colonial records on tribal literacy and customary law.

## Documented Terminology
- **Kor**: Home/Household (Primary domain for early childhood education).
- **Masjid / Madrasa**: Mosque/School (Centers for religious and moral literacy).
- **Hujra**: Community Guesthouse (Social and political academy for youth).
- **Mashar / Kishar**: Elder/Younger (The pedagogical relationship).
- **Mataloona**: Proverbs (Condensed social wisdom).
