# Sources: Page 90 — Orakzai Diaspora

This document records the primary and academic sources supporting **Page 90: Orakzai Diaspora** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary Official & International Sources

1. **UNHCR.** (2009, December). *Update on internal displacement in north-west Pakistan*.
   - **Details**: Reported 40,000 people (2,400 families) from Orakzai seeking refuge in Hangu.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical conflict-induced displacement figures.

2. **Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre (IDMC).** (2010). *Internal Displacement: Global Overview of Trends and Developments in 2009*.
   - **Details**: Reported 190,000 people displaced from Bajaur, Mohmand, Khyber, and Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Regional scale of displacement during 2009.

3. **UNHCR.** (2011). *Global Report 2011: Pakistan Overview*.
   - **Details**: Reported return of 23,000 households to Orakzai by end of 2010.
   - **Claim Supported**: Return migration and reconstruction timeline.

4. **Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS).** (2023). *7th Population and Housing Census*.
   - **Details**: Data on internal migration and urban settlement patterns (national level).
   - **Claim Supported**: General context of rural-to-urban movement.

## Academic & Research Sources

5. **Ul Hassan, S. M.** (2015). *Pakistani migration to the Gulf and development in the Miranzai valley in Pakistan*. In *Migration and Development in South Asia*.
   - **Details**: Research on Orakzai and Bangash migration to the Gulf and property acquisition in Hangu/Kohat.
   - **Claim Supported**: Regional migration hubs and economic impact of remittances.

6. **Bukhari, A. Q.** (2026). *Layered Continuity in Pakistan's Northwest Borderlands*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of Pashtun diaspora networks in Karachi (Sohrab Goth, Banaras).
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai community hubs in Karachi and identity maintenance.

7. **Mosel, I., & Jackson, A.** (2013). *Sanctuary in the city? Urban displacement and vulnerability in Peshawar, Pakistan*. Overseas Development Institute (ODI).
   - **Details**: Research on IDPs from Orakzai and Kurram settling in Peshawar.
   - **Claim Supported**: Peshawar as a destination for urban displacement.

## Research Gaps & Limitations
- **Current Diaspora Size**: No official census counts "Orakzai" ethnicity in Karachi or the Gulf; figures rely on community estimates and localized research.
- **UK/Europe Diaspora**: While professional presence is documented, the total population of Orakzai people in Western countries remains unquantified.
