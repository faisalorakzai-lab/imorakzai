# Sources: Page 25 — Pashtun Family Structure

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 25: Pashtun Family Structure** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Akbar S. Ahmed**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*.
   - **Author**: Akbar S. Ahmed.
   - **Publication**: Routledge, 1980 / republished 2011.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive anthropological study of Pashtun tribal organization, patrilineal descent systems, and household structures.
   - **Claim Supported**: Kinship organization and tribal social structure.

2. **Fredrik Barth**: *Features of Person and Society in Swat: Collected Essays on Pathans*.
   - **Author**: Fredrik Barth.
   - **Publication**: Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1981.
   - **Details**: Examines political organization, household dynamics, and social groupings among Pashtuns.
   - **Claim Supported**: Dynamic nature of social groupings and household adaptation.

3. **Lutz Rzehak**: *Doing Pashto: Pashtunwali as the Ideal of Honourable Behaviour and Tribal Life among the Pashtuns*.
   - **Author**: Lutz Rzehak.
   - **Publication**: Afghanistan Analysts Network, 2011.
   - **Details**: Discusses cultural values, family roles, and social obligations within Pashtun society.
   - **Claim Supported**: Cultural transmission and family values.
