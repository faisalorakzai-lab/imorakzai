# Sources: Page 27 — Pashtun Language & Literature

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 27: Pashtun Language & Literature** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *Pashto Language and Literature*.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Columbia University.
   - **Details**: Authoritative linguistic and literary overview of Pashto as an Eastern Iranian language with a documented written and oral tradition.
   - **Claim Supported**: Linguistic classification, historical development, and literary corpus.

2. **Sergei Andreyev**: *Pashto Literature: The Classical Period*.
   - **Author**: Sergei Andreyev.
   - **Publication**: *Oral Literature of Iranian Languages*, Kurdish, Pashto, Balochi, Ossetic, Persian and Tajik, I.B. Tauris.
   - **Details**: Scholarly analysis of classical Pashto poets, including Khushhal Khan Khattak and Rahman Baba.
   - **Claim Supported**: Classical Pashto literary traditions, poetic themes, and historical figures.

3. **David Neil MacKenzie**: *Pashto Verse*.
   - **Author**: David Neil MacKenzie.
   - **Publication**: *Bulletin of the School of Oriental and African Studies*, University of London, 1958.
   - **Details**: Academic examination of Pashto metrical and poetic structures.
   - **Claim Supported**: Poetic forms and oral-literary transmission.
