# Research Notes: Page 105 — Digital Preservation of History

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 105: Digital Preservation of History**.

## 1. Digital Preservation Frameworks

### UNESCO/PERSIST Guidelines
- **Source:** *UNESCO/PERSIST Guidelines for the Selection of Digital Heritage for Long-Term Preservation* (2nd Ed, 2021).
- **Key Principles:** Selection based on significance, sustainability of formats, metadata as a core component, and the distinction between a digital surrogate (copy) and a preservation object.
- **Significance:** Cultural heritage institutions must move beyond "scanning" to "curating" and "preserving."

### 3-2-1 Backup Rule
- **Standard:** 3 copies of data, on 2 different media types, with 1 copy stored offsite.
- **Context:** Essential for preventing data loss due to hardware failure, obsolescence, or disasters.

## 2. Pakistan-Specific Archival Context

### Citizens Archive of Pakistan (CAP)
- **Model:** A non-profit organization dedicated to cultural and historic preservation.
- **Achievements:** Documented over 5,000 oral histories and digitized 650,000 documents/photographs since 2007.
- **Methodology:** Focuses on personal narratives and family archives to supplement official national history.

### National Archives of Pakistan
- **Role:** Official repository for government records and historical documents.
- **Limitation:** Often focuses on state-level records; community-level tribal history (like Orakzai) is often found in private family collections or provincial archives.

## 3. Technical Standards & Metadata

### File Formats
- **Master Files:** TIFF (uncompressed) for images, WAV (uncompressed) for audio, PDF/A for documents.
- **Access Copies:** JPEG, PNG, MP3, standard PDF.

### Metadata Standards
- **Dublin Core:** The standard for descriptive metadata (Title, Creator, Subject, Description, Publisher, Contributor, Date, Type, Format, Identifier, Source, Language, Relation, Coverage, Rights).
- **Provenance:** Critical for authenticity—recording the chain of custody from original creator to digital archive.

## 4. Ethical & Legal Considerations

### Informed Consent
- **Requirement:** Participants must understand the purpose, storage, and future use of their recordings.
- **Privacy:** Balancing historical interest with the right to privacy for living individuals and sensitive family information.

### AI in History
- **Assistance:** AI is valuable for OCR (Optical Character Recognition) and transcription assistance.
- **Risk:** Generative AI must not be used to "fill in the gaps" of historical evidence without explicit labeling as reconstruction.

## 5. Evidence Boundaries & Cautions

- **Originality:** A digital scan is a surrogate; the original physical object (if it exists) remains the primary source.
- **Authenticity:** Requires documented provenance. A file without a known source is a "found object" with limited evidentiary value.
- **Orakzai Specificity:** No formal "Orakzai Digital Archive" exists; the proposed architecture is a conceptual framework for future development.
