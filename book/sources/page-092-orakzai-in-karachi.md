# Sources: Page 92 — Orakzai in Karachi

This document records the primary and academic sources supporting **Page 92: Orakzai in Karachi** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary Academic & Research Sources

1. **Rehman, Zia Ur.** (2017). *Karachi: A Pashtun City?* In *Cityscapes of Violence in Karachi: Publics and Counterpublics* (Eds. N. Khan). Oxford University Press.
   - **Details**: Documents the historical and political transformation of Karachi through Pashtun migration.
   - **Claim Supported**: Broad Pashtun migration context and the non-monolithic nature of the community.

2. **International Growth Centre (IGC).** (2024). *Overcoming the exclusion of Karachi's migrant communities*.
   - **Details**: Identifies Pashtuns as Karachi's largest internal migrant community since the 1950s.
   - **Claim Supported**: Status of Pashtuns in Karachi's demographic and economic landscape.

3. **Nichols, Robert.** (2008). *A History of Pashtun Migration, 1775-2006*. Oxford University Press.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive history of Pashtun movement, including settlement in Karachi.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical waves of migration and economic drivers.

4. **Ul Hassan, S. M.** (2015). *Pakistani migration to the Gulf and development in the Miranzai valley in Pakistan*. In *Migration and Development in South Asia*.
   - **Details**: Mentions Orakzai tribesmen migrating to Karachi after establishing bases in Hangu/Kohat.
   - **Claim Supported**: Staged migration and regional connections.

## Research Gaps & Limitations
- **Orakzai-Specific Statistics**: There is currently insufficient publicly available statistical evidence to determine the exact number of Orakzai people living in Karachi.
- **Neighborhood Mapping**: While Pashtun settlement areas are well-documented, the specific street-level concentration of Orakzai families (relative to other tribes) relies largely on oral history and community observation rather than published census tracts.
