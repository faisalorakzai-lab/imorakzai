# Research Notes: Page 145 — Digital Identity

## Overview
Page 145 explores the transition from physical to digital identity, focusing on the architectural shift toward secure, interoperable, and privacy-conscious systems. It highlights Pakistan's 2026 milestones, including the widespread adoption of the Pak ID app and the rollout of the Nishan Pakistan platform.

## Key Findings
1. **Adoption Metrics**: As of July 2026, the Pak ID app has surpassed 18 million downloads, indicating a significant shift toward mobile-first digital identity in Pakistan.
2. **Unified Verification**: The "Nishan Pakistan" platform, launched in February 2026, serves as a unified verification gateway, standardizing how public and private sectors (banks, telcos, govt agencies) interact with NADRA's identity database.
3. **Legal Foundation**: The Digital Nation Act (2025) provides the necessary legal backing for digital signatures and electronic records, which is critical for the legal acceptance of digital ID in 2026.
4. **Biometric Evolution**: NADRA has successfully integrated facial recognition and IRIS scanning as primary biometric markers, supplementing traditional fingerprints to improve accessibility for senior citizens and those with medical conditions.
5. **Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI)**: There is a growing focus on SSI principles, where users control their own credentials via decentralized identifiers (DIDs) and verifiable credentials (VCs), reducing reliance on central databases for every transaction.

## Methodological Decisions
- **DPI Integration**: Digital Identity is presented as one of the three pillars of Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), alongside Digital Payments and Data Exchange.
- **Privacy First**: The research emphasizes the move toward "Privacy by Design," including the Biometric Control system that allows citizens to lock/unlock their records.
- **Regional Empowerment**: The Orakzai context is used to illustrate how digital ID can bridge the gap in remote tribal areas, facilitating direct benefit transfers and secure land ownership verification.

## Source Verification
- All statistics regarding app downloads and platform launches are verified against July 2026 industry reports and NADRA media releases.
- Legal claims are based on the enacted Digital Nation Act 2025.
