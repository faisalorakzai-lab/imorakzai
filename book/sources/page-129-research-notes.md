# Research Notes: Page 129 — AI Governance

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 129: AI Governance**.

## 1. Institutional Landscape (Pakistan 2026)
- **National AI Policy 2025:** Approved by the Federal Cabinet on July 30, 2025. This is Pakistan's foundational roadmap for AI, covering healthcare, education, agriculture, and public administration.
- **Islamabad AI Declaration (February 9, 2026):** A landmark adoption of a sovereign, responsible, and capability-driven approach to AI. It outlines 9 foundational principles for a trusted AI ecosystem.
- **Pakistan Digital Authority (PDA):** Established under the Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025. The PDA is the central regulatory body for digital standards, AI auditing, and the National Data Governance Policy 2026.
- **National Data Governance Policy 2026:** A proposed framework for setting data standards, monitoring compliance, and supervising data audits across the public and private sectors.

## 2. Governance Framework & Principles
- **Risk-Proportionate Oversight:** A hybrid model that categorizes AI applications into Low, Medium, High, and Unacceptable risk, with corresponding levels of governance.
- **Explainability & Auditability:** Principles 5 and 2 of the Islamabad Declaration emphasize that AI systems must be explainable to users and auditable by authorized institutions.
- **Human Accountability:** The core principle that responsibility for AI outcomes remains with human institutions and individuals, not the technology itself.
- **Sovereign AI Infrastructure:** A focus on building indigenous AI capabilities and infrastructure to ensure national data security and technological independence.

## 3. Technical & Ethical Standards
- **AI Lifecycle Governance:** Oversight starting from data collection through model training, testing, deployment, and eventual retirement.
- **Data Quality & Bias:** Stricter standards for data accuracy and representativeness to prevent the reproduction of historical biases in AI models.
- **Red-Teaming & Safety:** Mandatory adversarial testing for high-risk systems to identify vulnerabilities and prevent harmful outputs.
- **Transparency & Disclosure:** Requirements for disclosing AI use, especially in sensitive areas like elections, finance, and healthcare.

## 4. Orakzai & Regional Governance
- **Traditional vs. Digital:** The intersection of tribal customary law (Jirga) with modern digital governance frameworks.
- **Heritage Bridge:** Framing AI governance as a tool to protect local cultural values and oral histories from digital manipulation or bias.

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** His interests in software architecture and digital systems align with the "Governance-by-Design" philosophy required for building trusted AI ecosystems.
- **Philosophy:** "The algorithm provides the speed; the governance provides the direction."
