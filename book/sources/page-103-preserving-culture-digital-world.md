- **Pashto vs. Orakzai:** General Pashto digital resources (dictionaries, keyboards) are used by Orakzai speakers but are not Orakzai-specific unless noted.
- **Privacy:** Private family records are identified as restricted; no specific family data is published without hypothetical consent placeholders.
- **AI Disclaimer:** AI-generated content is strictly excluded from historical evidence.
