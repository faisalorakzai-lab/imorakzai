# Sources: Page 9 — The Pashtun World

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 9: The Pashtun World** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *Paṣ̌tō language* & *Afghānistān vi. Paṣ̌tō*.
   - **Authors**: Multiple contributors / Iranica Foundation.
   - **Publisher**: Encyclopædia Iranica Foundation, New York.
   - **Details**: Documents Pashto as an Eastern Iranian language, its linguistic classification, and historical distribution across Afghanistan, Pakistan, and regional contact zones.
   - **Claim Supported**: Pashto linguistic lineage and regional spread.

2. **Encyclopaedia Iranica & Britannica**: *Ghilzay, Afridi, Orakzai, and Pashtun Tribal Federations*.
   - **Authors**: Academic ethnographic researchers.
   - **Details**: Detailed accounts of Pashtun tribal confederations, social organization, genealogical traditions, and historical migrations.
   - **Claim Supported**: Diversity of Pashtun tribal structures and major tribal groupings.

3. **Encyclopædia Britannica / Historical Records**: *Tirah and Frontier Geography*.
   - **Details**: Historical geography of the Tirah region, mountainous terrain, and interactions between mountain communities and regional plains.
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of geography and Tirah in tribal history.

4. **Academic Ethnographic Studies**: *Pashtunwali Ethical Framework*.
   - **Authors**: Sociologists and anthropologists specializing in Pashtun society.
   - **Details**: Analysis of Pashtunwali principles (Melmastia, Nanawatai, Nang, Badal) as dynamic customary codes rather than rigid universal legal statutes.
   - **Claim Supported**: Nature and flexibility of Pashtun social values.

5. **Modern Migration and Diaspora Studies**: *Global Pashtun Communities*.
   - **Details**: Documentation of modern urban migration and diaspora formations in major global cities (Karachi, London, New York, Dubai).
   - **Claim Supported**: The twenty-first-century global Pashtun diaspora and digital evolution.
