# Research Notes: Page 93 — Orakzai in Peshawar

## Core Concept
- **The Regional Hub**: Peshawar serves as the primary urban center for the Orakzai people, offering services and opportunities unavailable in the highlands.
- **Multi-local Life**: Many Orakzai families maintain a presence in both the homeland and Peshawar, balancing urban opportunity with tribal identity.
- **Historical Continuity**: Migration to Peshawar is not a new phenomenon; it dates back to at least the 19th century (e.g., Akbar Khan Orakzai settlement in the 1880s).

## Peshawar as an Urban Center
- **Education**: The University of Peshawar is a critical destination for Orakzai youth. Documented student organizations and individual academic success stories (e.g., Laloona Orakzai, BS Psychology, 2013).
- **Healthcare**: Peshawar's major hospitals (Khyber Teaching Hospital, Lady Reading Hospital) serve as referral points for Orakzai patients.
- **Business & Employment**: Orakzai entrepreneurs are active in real estate, construction (e.g., Khiali Orakzai), retail, and the transport sector.
- **Administration**: As the provincial capital, Peshawar is where Orakzai residents interact with higher-level government departments and legal institutions.

## Migration Patterns
- **Temporary Mobility**: Students and patients often reside in Peshawar temporarily before returning to the district.
- **Long-term Settlement**: Historical settlement in Peshawar district (NPS) and modern urban neighborhoods (Pathan Colony, Hayatabad, and surrounding areas).
- **Conflict-Induced Displacement**: Significant influx of IDPs from Orakzai to Peshawar during military operations (2009–2011), with some choosing to stay permanently (Mosel & Jackson, 2013).

## Identity and Culture
- **Language**: Pashto remains the primary language, but urban Orakzai speakers often navigate the bilingual (Pashto/Urdu) environment of the city.
- **Social Networks**: Hujras in Peshawar serve as gathering points for tribal elders and youth, maintaining the "nang" and "melmastia" values in an urban setting.
- **Religious Life**: Connection to Peshawar's historic mosques and religious scholars.

## Research Gaps
- **Exact Population**: Lack of official census data breaking down Pashtun sub-tribes in Peshawar city.
- **Economic Share**: While individual businesses are documented, the total economic contribution of the Orakzai community to Peshawar's GDP is not quantified.
