# Sources: Page 62 — Attan & Traditional Dance

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 62: Attan & Traditional Dance** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Dawn / The Express Tribune**: *Reports on Orakzai Spring Mela 2024*. (June 8, 2024).
   - **Details**: Media coverage of the Jashan-e-Baharan in Orakzai District, documenting "traditional dance performances" and "horse and camel dances."
   - **Claim Supported**: Contemporary public dance performances in Orakzai District.

2. **Hussain, K.**: *Consciousness, Gender and Identity: A Critical Study of Pashto Verbal Folkloric Arts*. (2025).
   - **Details**: Academic study of Pashtun identity through folk arts, including Attan.
   - **Claim Supported**: Attan as a visible expression of Pashtun aesthetics and identity.

3. **Kakar, H.**: *Ritual as Resistance and Social Control: Exploring the Contributing Aspects of Attan in the Uprising of Pashtun Youth in Pakistan*. (2025).
   - **Details**: Research on Attan's role in modern Pashtun social movements and its ethnographic history.
   - **Claim Supported**: Attan's function as a social ritual and its broad Pashtun distribution.

4. **Hanifi, M. J.**: *“Afghan” in Afghanistan: Idols in the Land of Idols*. (2023).
   - **Details**: Discussion of Pashtun ethnology and the structure of Attan.
   - **Claim Supported**: Cross-border nature and structural characteristics of Attan.

5. **Ahmed, A. S.**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society*. (1980).
   - **Details**: Ethnographic analysis of tribal social life and communal rituals.
   - **Claim Supported**: Social context of communal gatherings and performance.
