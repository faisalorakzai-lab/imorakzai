# Sources: Page 100 — The Modern Orakzai

This source record documents the evidence used for **Page 100: The Modern Orakzai** in *I’M ORAKZAI*. The page brings the book's first major section to a close by exploring contemporary identity, development, and future possibilities.

## Source 1 — Constitutional and Administrative Reform

**Author:** Government of Pakistan  
**Title:** *Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 2018*  
**Publisher:** Gazette of Pakistan  
**Year:** 2018  
**Claim supported:** The former Federally Administered Tribal Areas (FATA), including Orakzai Agency, were formally merged into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province.  
**Evidence level:** Official administrative record.

## Source 2 — Digital Skills and Development

**Author:** UNDP Pakistan / Government of Japan  
**Title:** *600 young people from Kurram and Orakzai trained in digital skills*  
**Institution:** United Nations Development Programme  
**Year:** 2025  
**URL:** [UNDP Press Release](https://www.undp.org/pakistan/press-releases/600-young-people-kurram-and-orakzai-trained-digital-skills-under-government-japan-undp-initiative)  
**Claim supported:** A 2025 initiative provided digital training to 600 youth from Orakzai and Kurram, with 52% women participation, focusing on freelancing and remote work.  
**Evidence level:** Contemporary development evidence.

## Source 3 — Youth Career Dialogue

**Author:** Directorate of Youth Affairs, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa  
**Title:** *Seminar on Career Counselling at District Orakzai*  
**Institution:** KP Youth Affairs Department  
**Year:** 2025  
**URL:** [KP Youth Affairs Events](https://kpyouthaffairs.gov.pk/events/new/300)  
**Claim supported:** Contemporary youth in Orakzai engage in formal dialogues regarding education, legal employment, and community development.  
**Evidence level:** Local administrative record.

## Source 4 — Sociological Research on Pashtun Youth

**Author:** Naveed Ahmad Shinwari  
**Title:** *Political and social change in Pakistan's tribal areas: Understanding Pashtun youth*  
**Publisher:** Academic Research / Books  
**Year:** 2025  
**Claim supported:** Young people in merged districts navigate between traditional tribal values and modern civic/educational institutions.  
**Evidence level:** Academic scholarship.

## Source 5 — Gender and Development Context

**Author:** UN Women  
**Title:** *Status of Women and Girls in Orakzai District*  
**Institution:** United Nations Women

**Claim supported:** Provides baseline context for education and human development for women in Orakzai district.

| Claim | Date | Evidence | Confidence | Limitations |

## Evidence Boundary

- **No Generalization**: Training statistics from specific programs (e.g., 52% women) are never presented as district-wide averages.
- **No Prediction**: Future economic outcomes are framed as possibilities, not guaranteed certainties.
- **No Stereotyping**: Modernity is presented as a dynamic choice, not a replacement for heritage.
- **Gaps Acknowledged**: Where district-wide data is missing (e.g., total internet penetration), the page explicitly states the need for further documentation.
