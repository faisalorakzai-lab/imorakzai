# Research Notes: Page 141 — Digital Sovereignty

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 141: Digital Sovereignty**.

## 1. National Framework & Policy (2026)
- **Data Governance:** Pakistan launched the **National Data Governance Policy 2026** in June 2026, establishing the first comprehensive framework for managing and securing government data.
- **Data Protection:** The **Personal Data Protection Bill** remains in the legislative pipeline as of mid-2026, highlighting the ongoing effort to codify individual data rights and national jurisdiction.
- **AI Sovereignty:** The **National AI Policy 2025** emphasizes the development of sovereign AI infrastructure, including a preference for local data centers and cloud environments to safeguard national interests.

## 2. Strategic Infrastructure & Hardware
- **Cloud Sovereignty:** A strategic transition is underway from foreign cloud reliance to domestic **Tier-III data centers**, supported by targeted incentives for domestic infrastructure providers.
- **Computing Capacity:** Access to high-performance computing (GPUs/CPUs) is identified as a critical requirement for AI sovereignty, with initiatives to strengthen public-sector AI infrastructure.
- **Semiconductors:** Pakistan rolled out a **National Semiconductor Plan** in late 2025/early 2026, focusing on chip design talent and strategic capability within the global supply chain.

## 3. Telecommunications & Connectivity
- **Physical Foundation:** Digital sovereignty is anchored in fiber-optic networks, Internet Exchange Points (IXPs), and undersea cable resilience.
- **Satellite Integration:** Satellite connectivity is recognized as a vital complement for remote regions (like Orakzai) and disaster-response scenarios.
- **Domestic Peering:** Strengthening domestic IXPs is a priority to reduce latency, improve resilience, and keep local traffic within national borders.

## 4. Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)
- **Core Components:** The sovereign digital ecosystem is built on the integration of **Digital Identity (NADRA)**, **Payments (Raast)**, and secure **Data Exchange** layers.
- **Cybersecurity:** Cybersecurity sovereignty is identified as the most critical dimension, requiring the capability to detect, investigate, and respond to incidents independently.

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI & Blockchain Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** Advocating for "Sovereignty-by-Design"—building systems that prioritize local control over data, infrastructure, and strategic capabilities.
- **Vision:** Leveraging technologies like blockchain and decentralized identity to empower local communities and ensure that Orakzai's digital heritage remains under its own jurisdiction.
