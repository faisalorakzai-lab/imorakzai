# Research Notes: Page 127 — AI & Finance

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 127: AI & Finance**.

## 1. Institutional Landscape (Pakistan 2026)
- **NCAI & NED University:** NED University of Engineering and Technology (Karachi) serves as a primary hub for AI research in Pakistan's financial capital. The **Smart City Lab** and associated **Industry Connect** initiatives focus on the intersection of AI, IoT, and industrial/financial applications.
- **State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) 2026:** The SBP has implemented a "Regulatory Checklist for AI in Financial Services" (2026), providing a framework for banks and fintechs to deploy AI responsibly. Updated **Prudential Regulations for SME Financing** (July 2026) further support digital assessment models.
- **RAAST Integration:** Pakistan's instant payment system, RAAST, has integrated AI-driven monitoring for real-time fraud detection and payment routing, managing millions of transactions across the national grid.

## 2. Technical Applications in Pakistan
- **AI-Driven Fraud Detection:** Implementation of machine learning models for identifying transaction anomalies, location signals, and behavioral patterns, as documented in 2026 research (Nature, 2026).
- **KYC & Digital Onboarding:** Use of AI for automated document processing and identity verification, reducing operational costs for digital banks like Sadapay and NayaPay.
- **Credit Scoring for the Unbanked:** AI models analyzing alternative data (mobile usage, utility payments) to assess creditworthiness for SMEs and rural farmers, supporting national financial inclusion goals.

## 3. Socio-Economic Context
- **Financial Inclusion:** AI is framed as a tool for reaching the unbanked population in Pakistan, particularly in rural and remote districts.
- **Bias & Responsibility:** The 2026 SBP guidelines emphasize the need for testing, auditing, and transparency to prevent automated systems from reproducing existing social inequalities.
- **Personal Finance Assistants:** The rise of AI-powered digital assistants helping individuals manage budgets, savings, and recurring payments within mobile banking apps.

## 4. Orakzai & Regional Finance
- **Traditional Finance:** The transition from cash-based tribal economies to digital wallets and mobile finance.
- **Heritage Preservation:** Framing digital finance as a means to support local economies while preserving traditional communal values of trust and accountability.

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** His interests in software, digital infrastructure, and blockchain align with the "Systems Philosophy" required for building secure, scalable financial data architectures.
- **Philosophy:** "The system provides the data; the human provides the trust."
