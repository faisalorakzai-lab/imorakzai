# Research Notes: Page 131 — Blockchain Technology

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 131: Blockchain Technology**.

## 1. Institutional & Legal Landscape (Pakistan 2026)
- **Virtual Assets Act 2026:** A landmark piece of legislation passed by the Parliament of Pakistan, providing a permanent legal framework for cryptocurrency, tokenization, and decentralized finance.
- **Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA):** Established as the primary regulator for digital assets, ending years of legal ambiguity.
- **National Crypto Council:** Formed to advise the government on blockchain strategy, taxation, and economic integration.
- **Global Adoption Ranking:** By 2026, Pakistan has climbed to 3rd place globally in crypto adoption, with a market estimated at over $300 billion.
- **National Bitcoin Reserve:** Reports indicate that the government is exploring the creation of a national digital asset reserve as part of its long-term financial strategy.

## 2. Technical Research & Education
- **NUST SINES (School of Interdisciplinary Engineering & Science):** Home to advanced research in computational science, distributed systems, and blockchain applications.
- **Blockchain for Governance:** Research initiatives focusing on using distributed ledgers for land registry, supply chain transparency, and digital identity in Pakistan.
- **Interoperability & Scalability:** A shift in focus from basic blockchain protocols to Layer-2 solutions and cross-chain interoperability to handle high transaction volumes.

## 3. Core Blockchain Concepts
- **Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT):** Understanding blockchain as a family of technologies for shared state and decentralized coordination.
- **Consensus Mechanisms:** The transition from energy-intensive Proof of Work (PoW) to more efficient Proof of Stake (PoS) and hybrid consensus models.
- **Smart Contracts:** Programmable digital agreements that automate workflows without central intermediaries.
- **The Blockchain Trilemma:** The ongoing research challenge of balancing decentralization, security, and scalability.

## 4. Orakzai & Regional Context
- **Digital Identity:** Potential for blockchain-based identity systems to provide secure, verifiable credentials for residents in remote districts like Orakzai.
- **Remittances:** The role of blockchain in lowering the cost of international remittances for the Orakzai diaspora, bridging the gap between global finance and local families.

- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** His documented interests in software architecture, blockchain systems, and digital infrastructure align with the "Decentralized & Secure" philosophy of 2026.
