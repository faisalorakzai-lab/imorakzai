# Sources: Page 50 — Orakzai Markets

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 50: Orakzai Markets** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Government**: *Draft Land Use Plan of District Orakzai (2026)*.
   - **Details**: Identifies Kalaya as an urban area and discusses commercial activities in the district.
   - **Claim Supported**: Administrative and commercial role of Kalaya.

2. **Pakistan Almanac**: *Khyber Pakhtunkhwa-Orakzai Profile*.
   - **Details**: Identifies Ghiljo Bazaar as a tehsil headquarters and commercial hub.
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of Ghiljo as a market center.

3. **Radio Pakistan**: *Orakzai Spring Mela 2024 Report*.
   - **Details**: Documents the festival economy, cultural stalls, and support for local employment and tourism.
   - **Claim Supported**: 2024 Mela economy and tourism-market connection.

4. **Dawn / The Express Tribune**: *Reports on Kalaya Bazaar (2018-2021)*.
   - **Details**: Provides historical context on business activity, the Hindu trading community, and market infrastructure (shop checking).
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical and functional importance of Kalaya Bazaar.

5. **Bank Al-Habib**: *Branch Network Locator*.
   - **Details**: Verifies the presence of a formal banking branch in Kalaya.
   - **Claim Supported**: Market infrastructure and transition to cash/banking economy.
