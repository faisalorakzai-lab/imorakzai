## Evidence Boundaries

- **Schematic Labeling:** All cartographic visualizations are labeled as **SCHEMATIC — NOT A HISTORICAL BOUNDARY MAP**.
- **Disputed Boundaries:** Uncertain tribal boundaries are represented as conceptual zones rather than fixed lines.
- **Name Variants:** The archive acknowledges multiple spellings (e.g., Orakzai, Orakzay, Urakzai) across different historical periods.
- **Evidence Hierarchy:** Physical geography (High) > Administrative records (High) > Settlement history (Medium) > Migration routes (Low).
