# Research Notes: Page 50 — Orakzai Markets

## Core Definition
- **Market**: A system of exchange where goods, services, and information meet.
- **Bazaar**: A physical cluster of shops and traders (e.g., Kalaya Bazaar, Ghiljo Bazaar).
- **Economic Sequence**: Land → Agriculture → Livestock → Traditional Trades → Markets → Trade → Regional Economy.

## Documented Markets & Bazaars in Orakzai
1. **Kalaya Bazaar**:
   - **Location**: Lower Orakzai (District Headquarters).
   - **Status**: Major commercial hub; documented shop checking (2021); banking presence (Bank Al-Habib).
   - **Historical Event**: 2018 bomb blast site; historical presence of Hindu trading community.
2. **Ghiljo Bazaar**:
   - **Location**: Upper Orakzai (Tehsil Headquarters).
   - **Status**: Major community market; commercial and administrative center.
3. **Dabori Bazaar**:
   - **Location**: Upper Orakzai.
   - **Status**: Local bazaar/hometown market center.
4. **Mishti Mela (Mushti Mela)**:
   - **Location**: Near Kalaya.
   - **Status**: Local market/settlement hub.

## Infrastructure & Access
- **Roads**: Kalaya-Kurez Road; roads connecting settlements to Kohat, Hangu, and Peshawar.
- **Transport**: Mountainous terrain increases logistical costs; distance affects market access.
- **Electricity & Comms**: TAESCO provides electricity; mobile/internet access growing (conceptual digital pathway).

## Modern & Future Trends
- **2024 Spring Mela**: Cultural festival with stalls supporting tourism and employment (Radio Pakistan report).
- **E-commerce Potential**: Connecting local products (mazri, walnuts, embroidery) to diaspora via digital marketplaces.
- **Remittances**: Significant for household purchasing power (general FATA/KP trend).
- **Conceptual Tech**: AI for market insights; Blockchain for traceable product history.

## Data Limitations
- **Orakzai-Specific Stats**: Limited publicly accessible datasets for market turnover, shop counts, or daily transaction volumes.
- **Price Data**: District-level price indices are not consistently available in national datasets.
