# Research Notes: Page 128 — AI & Government

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 128: AI & Government**.

## 1. Institutional Landscape (Pakistan 2026)
- **Digital Pakistan Masterplan 2026:** A 10-year digital transformation roadmap led by the Ministry of IT & Telecommunication (MoITT) and the Pakistan Digital Authority (PDA).
- **National AI Policy 2025/2026:** A framework aimed at creating 200,000 digital jobs and strengthening e-governance through AI integration.
- **NCAI Smart City Lab (NED UET Karachi):** A research hub focused on intelligent solutions for indigenous problems, including smart irrigation, environmental sustainability, and autonomous vehicle development.
- **NDMA AI Integration:** The National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) has transitioned to an AI-powered foresight paradigm, using data from 400+ satellite constellations for flood monitoring and weather prediction.

## 2. Technical Applications in Public Administration
- **Digital Public Services:** AI-powered citizen services for information retrieval, document processing, and appointment scheduling.
- **Disaster Management:** Real-time flood intelligence systems combining satellite imagery, river data, and ground sensors for early warning.
- **Smart Cities:** Optimization of urban infrastructure (traffic signals, energy, waste) through AI-driven monitoring and analysis.
- **Public Records Digitization:** The transformation of paper archives into searchable, structured data through OCR and AI analysis.

## 3. Socio-Economic Context
- **Accountability & Oversight:** The 2026 policy framework emphasizes that AI should support public institutions rather than remove human accountability.
- **Privacy & Security:** Strong safeguards are required for sensitive data like digital identity and public health records.
- **Climate Resilience:** AI is framed as a critical tool for planning and response in the face of environmental hazards like floods and heatwaves.

## 4. Orakzai & Regional Governance
- **Administrative Evolution:** The transition from traditional tribal governance (Jirga) to digital public services and AI-assisted administration.
- **Heritage Preservation:** Using technology to digitize and preserve tribal archives while ensuring that digital tools reflect local cultural values.

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** His interests in software, digital systems, and blockchain align with the "Systems Philosophy" required for building secure, transparent government data architectures.
- **Philosophy:** "The machine processes the records; the government remains responsible for the people."
