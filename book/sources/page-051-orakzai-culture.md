# Sources: Page 51 — Orakzai Culture

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 51: Orakzai Culture** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Zamin, M., Adnan, M., Hamdard, M.H. et al.**: *Tribal traditions and conservation perspectives of wild plants in Orakzai District, Pakistan*. Scientific Reports (2026).
   - **Details**: Documents 123 wild plant species and their tribal uses in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Traditional ecological knowledge (TEK) as part of Orakzai culture.

2. **Khalil, M.**: *Jirga System in Orakzai*. Pashto Journal, Vol. 54, No. 669 (2025).
   - **Details**: Explores unique features of the Orakzai jirga system.
   - **Claim Supported**: Specificity of Orakzai jirga within the Pashtun framework.

3. **Alam, J., Ayaz, T. U., & Aziz, T.**: *The Role of Local Supporters in Promoting Social Cohesion in District Orakzai: A Qualitative Study*. Human Nature Journal of Social Sciences (2024).
   - **Details**: Identifies jirga councils, mashran (elders), ulema, and sportsmen as key cultural agents of cohesion.
   - **Claim Supported**: Modern role of traditional and civil society actors in Orakzai culture.

4. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *Orakzai*.
   - **Details**: Provides historical and ethnographic context for the tribe.
   - **Claim Supported**: Tribal history and regional Pashtun connection.

5. **Pashto Academy, University of Peshawar**: *General Cultural Archives*.
   - **Details**: Research on Pashtun social life, poetry, and oral traditions.
   - **Claim Supported**: Pashto language and poetic traditions.
