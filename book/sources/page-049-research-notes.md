# Research Notes: Page 49 — Traditional Trades

## Definition & Context
- **Traditional Trade**: Economic activities involving transmitted skills, materials, or occupational practices (e.g., making, repairing, processing, and trading).
- **Orakzai Context**: Beyond agriculture and livestock, livelihoods include small-scale crafts, mining, and elementary occupations.

## Documented Orakzai Handicrafts (Orakzai-Specific Evidence)
1. **Mazri Palm Crafts**:
   - **Material**: Leaves of the Mazri palm (*Nannorrhops ritchiana*), a perennial palm found in hilly areas of Orakzai and surrounding regions.
   - **Products**: Baskets (*Shkor*, *Tokray*), mats (*Musalah*, *Pozakey*), hand fans, hats, cages, hot pots, salt pots, brooms (*Jaru*), ropes, and traditional footwear (*Pezar*).
   - **Scale**: Household-based; often managed by women.
   - **Market**: Locally prepared; used as an eco-friendly alternative to plastic bags (supported by DC Orakzai).
2. **Embroidery on Clothes**:
   - **Description**: Traditional needlework on garments.
   - **Context**: A common household skill preserved across generations.
   - **Source**: *Pakistan Almanac* (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa-Orakzai profile).
3. **Silkworm Raising**:
   - **Historical Context**: Documented as an economic activity in Orakzai.
   - **Chain**: Silkworm → Cocoon → Silk Fibre → Textile → Trade.
   - **Source**: *Pakistan Almanac* (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa-Orakzai profile).

## Other Traditional & Economic Activities
- **Mining**: Coal extraction is a major industrial activity in Orakzai (169 units documented in 2010).
- **Elementary Occupations**: Tailoring, shop keeping, small-scale agribusiness.
- **Remittances**: Immigration to other districts for work is a major economic driver.

## Regional Comparisons (Regional Pashtun/FATA Evidence)
- **Woodwork**: furniture and wood-based products (documented in Kurram, likely present in Orakzai but labeled as regional comparison).
- **Metalwork**: Blacksmithing for agricultural tools (essential in any farming community, but labeled as regional/functional necessity).

## Gender & Livelihood
- **Women's Role**: Women play a critical role in the agricultural economy and handicrafts, including mazri production and embroidery.
