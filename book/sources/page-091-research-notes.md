# Research Notes: Page 91 — Orakzai in Modern Pakistan

## Core Concept
- **Orakzai as a KP District**: The transition from a federally administered agency to a provincial district within the constitutional framework of Pakistan.
- **Integration as a Process**: The shift from the FATA era (Article 247) to the post-merger era (25th Amendment) involves legal, administrative, and political transformations.
- **Modern Civic Identity**: The coexistence of tribal heritage with Pakistani citizenship and formal institutions.

## Key Historical & Constitutional Milestones

### 1. The 25th Constitutional Amendment (2018)
- **Date**: Passed by the National Assembly on May 24, 2018, and by the KP Assembly on May 27, 2018.
- **Impact**: Abolished the special status of FATA (repealed Article 247) and merged the seven tribal agencies and six frontier regions into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
- **Constitutional Change**: Residents of former FATA became full citizens of Pakistan with equal rights and representation in the provincial assembly.

### 2. Administrative Transformation
- **Agency to District**: Orakzai Agency was formally notified as Orakzai District in 2018.
- **Divisional Context**: Part of the **Kohat Division**.
- **District Administration**: Headed by a **Deputy Commissioner (DC)**, supported by Assistant Commissioners (ACs) for sub-divisions.
- **Tehsils**: Subdivided into four tehsils:
  1. **Lower Orakzai** (Headquarters: Kalaya)
  2. **Upper Orakzai** (Headquarters: Ghiljo)
  3. **Central Orakzai**
  4. **Ismailzai**

### 3. Judicial Integration
- **Establishment of Courts**: **District Courts Orakzai** were established in February 2019.
- **Key Date**: The first District & Sessions Judge was posted on **February 25, 2019**.
- **Location**: Operational at **Baber Mela**.
- **Significance**: Marked the transition from the Frontier Crimes Regulation (FCR) to the formal judicial system of Pakistan.

### 4. Political Representation (2024 Delimitation)
- **National Assembly**: **NA-36 Hangu-cum-Orakzai** (a combined constituency with neighboring Hangu district).
- **Provincial Assembly**: **PK-94 Orakzai**.
- **Elections**: Residents now participate in provincial elections for the KP Assembly, which was not the case during the FATA era.

## Modernity vs. Tradition
- **Identity Synthesis**: Being Orakzai in modern Pakistan involves maintaining Pashto language, tribal lineages, and hospitality (Melmastia) while engaging with formal education, digital connectivity, and national politics.
- **Development Framework**: Integration is supported by the **Accelerated Implementation Program (AIP)** and provincial development budgets, though challenges in service delivery remain.

## Author's Reflection Themes
- **The Process of Integration**: Integration is not a single event (the merger) but a continuous effort to build institutions that serve the community.
- **The Civic Anchor**: Citizenship provides the legal framework for rights, but identity provides the cultural anchor for belonging.
- **Modernity without Erasure**: Modern Orakzai is a synthesis of the ancient mountains and the modern state.
