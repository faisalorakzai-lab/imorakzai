# Sources: Page 30 — Preserving Tribal Memory

This document records the academic and institutional sources supporting **Page 30: Preserving Tribal Memory** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Institutional Guidelines

1. **Oral History Association (OHA)**: *Principles and Best Practices*.
   - **Publication**: Oral History Association, 2018.
   - **URL**: [https://oralhistory.org/best-practices/](https://oralhistory.org/best-practices/)
   - **Details**: Established professional standards for interviewing, archiving, and ethical preservation of oral testimonies.
   - **Claim Supported**: Methodological framework for oral recording workflows, consent, and metadata standards.

2. **UNESCO**: *Convention for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage*.
   - **Publication**: UNESCO, 2003.
   - **URL**: [https://ich.unesco.org/en/convention](https://ich.unesco.org/en/convention)
   - **Details**: International legal framework for identifying and protecting intangible heritage, including oral traditions and social practices.
   - **Claim Supported**: Definition of intangible cultural heritage and community-based preservation models.

3. **Digital Preservation Coalition (DPC)**: *Community Archives Digital Preservation Toolkit*.
   - **Publication**: Digital Preservation Coalition.
   - **URL**: [https://www.dpconline.org/digipres/implement-digipres/community-archives-dp-toolkit/](https://www.dpconline.org/digipres/implement-digipres/community-archives-dp-toolkit/)
   - **Details**: Practical guidance for community-led archives on managing digital assets and long-term storage.
   - **Claim Supported**: The 3-2-1 backup principle and digital preservation strategies for cultural heritage.

4. **Sibanda, M.**: *Community-Driven Archiving: Safeguarding Intangible Cultural Heritage*.
   - **Publication**: International Journal of Digital Humanities, 2025.
   - **Details**: Research on the role of community archives in protecting oral traditions and indigenous knowledge.
   - **Claim Supported**: Community archive models and youth engagement in archival processes.

5. **Alessandro Portelli**: *The Death of Luigi Trastulli and Other Stories: Form and Meaning in Oral History*.
   - **Publication**: State University of New York Press, 1991.
   - **Details**: Theoretical foundation for distinguishing between personal experience and inherited memory.
   - **Claim Supported**: Subjective meaning in oral history and the value of "ordinary" voices.
