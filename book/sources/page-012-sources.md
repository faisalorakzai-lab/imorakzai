# Sources: Page 12 — Pashtun Tribal Society

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 12: Pashtun Tribal Society** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Encyclopaedia Iranica & Anthropological Scholarship**: *Kinship and Extended Family Networks*.
   - **Authors**: Social anthropologists specializing in Afghan and South Asian tribal structures.
   - **Publisher**: Encyclopædia Iranica Foundation, New York.
   - **Details**: Examines the role of extended family, household organization, and mutual support systems in traditional Pashtun communities.
   - **Claim Supported**: Family as the foundational unit of tribal society.

2. **Encyclopaedia Britannica**: *Tribal Organization and Social Stratification*.
   - **Authors**: Ethnographic historians.
   - **Details**: Details the multi-tiered structure of Pashtun lineages, clans, and tribal confederations (such as Sarbani, Bettani, Ghurghusht, and Karlani).
   - **Claim Supported**: Lineage, clan, and tribal identity framework.

3. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *The Role of the Malik and Local Leadership*.
   - **Details**: Documents the historical and administrative functions of maliks as local representatives, negotiators, and intermediaries between tribal communities and state authorities.
   - **Claim Supported**: Institutional role of the malik.

4. **Encyclopaedia Iranica & Academic Studies**: *The Jirga Assembly and Dispute Resolution*.
   - **Authors**: Legal anthropologists and historians.
   - **Details**: Analyzes the structure, consensus-based decision-making, and conflict-resolution procedures of the traditional Pashtun jirga.
   - **Claim Supported**: Function and evolution of the jirga.

5. **EHRAF (eHRAF World Cultures / Yale University)**: *Pashtun Social Organization and Pashtunwali*.
   - **Authors**: Ethnographers (e.g., Akbar S. Ahmed and comparative researchers).
   - **Details**: Examines customary codes (Pashtunwali), balance of power, honour (nang), hospitality (melmastia), and social dynamics in tribal areas.
   - **Claim Supported**: Cultural framework of Pashtunwali and honour.

6. **Sociological Research on Contemporary Pashtun Society**: *Urbanization and Changing Gender Roles*.
   - **Details**: Documents the transformation of Pashtun society through migration, urbanization, women's education, and digital connectivity in the twenty-first century.
   - **Claim Supported**: Modern evolution of Pashtun family, gender roles, and digital diaspora networks.
