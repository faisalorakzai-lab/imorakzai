# Sources: Page 97 — Orakzai Overseas

This source record supports **Page 97: Orakzai Overseas** in *I’M ORAKZAI*. The page deliberately separates Pakistan-wide migration data from Orakzai-specific evidence and marks unavailable country-level counts as research gaps.

## Source 1 — Pakistan emigration statistics

**Author:** Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment, Government of Pakistan  
**Title:** *Reports & Statistics — Emigration Statistics of Pakistan*  
**Institution / Journal:** Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment  
**Publisher:** Government of Pakistan  
**Year:** Current reporting page, accessed 2026  
**Volume / Issue:** Not applicable  
**Pages:** Web report index and linked country-, district-, occupation-, province-, and category-wise files  
**DOI / URL:** [https://beoe.gov.pk/reports-and-statistics](https://beoe.gov.pk/reports-and-statistics)  
**Access date:** 12 August 2026  
**Source type:** Official administrative statistics  
**Geographic scope:** Pakistan-wide, with administrative breakdowns  
**Historical period:** Contemporary reporting and linked historical series  
**Claim supported:** Pakistan has a continuing overseas-employment system and official tables distinguish destination, district, occupation, and category.  
**Evidence level:** High for the stated administrative scope  
**Limitations:** The source is not a global census of Orakzai people abroad; administrative origin does not automatically establish tribal identity in every case.

**Claim supported:** Pakistani labour migration can be studied by origin, destination, skills, and occupations; historical agency-level records may provide context for former-FATA origins.

## Claim discipline

The page never converts **Pakistani** into **Orakzai**, **Pashtun** into **Orakzai**, **former-FATA** into **Orakzai**, **national migration** into **Orakzai migration**, **national remittances** into **Orakzai remittances**, or **one Orakzai family** into an entire diaspora. It also does not invent population figures, community organizations, businesses, migration dates, remittance totals, student numbers, professional numbers, or cultural traditions.
