# Research Notes: Page 156 — Diaspora Investment

## Overview
Page 156 examines the transition of the Pakistani diaspora's economic contribution from consumption-based remittances to capital-based investment. It details the 2026 performance of the Roshan Digital Account (RDA), the rise of diaspora-led venture capital, and the role of global networks in national development.

## Key Findings
1. **RDA Milestone**: By August 2026, the **Roshan Digital Account (RDA)** has reached a cumulative inflow of **$13.647 billion**. The month of July 2026 alone saw **$282 million** in inflows, a **52% year-on-year increase**, reflecting sustained trust from the nearly **956,000** overseas account holders.
2. **FDI Recovery**: Net Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) showed a massive recovery in early 2026, with March 2026 recording **$168 million**, up **163%** from the previous year. This growth is driven by diaspora participation in large-scale infrastructure and technology projects.
3. **Startup & SME Flow**: Pakistani startups raised **$36.6 million** in 2025, signaling a recovery in the ecosystem. Diaspora "Angels" and VC funds (like Paklaunch and others highlighted in the 2026 Investor Summit) are providing both capital and mentorship to local founders.
4. **Capital Formation Model**: The page uses the "Income → Remittance → Saving → Capital → Investment → Value" model to show how overseas earnings can be converted into productive national assets.
5. **The Orakzai Context**: Diaspora investment is framed as a bridge for regional development. Overseas Orakzai natives are investing in **Renewable Energy (Solar)** and **AgriTech** in the valley, bypassing traditional geographic barriers to improve local productivity and quality of life.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Investment vs. Remittance**: The page strictly distinguishes between money sent for household support and capital committed for returns. This highlights the "Investment Mindset" required for long-term growth.
- **DDI Framework**: Research incorporates the "Diaspora Direct Investment" (DDI) framework, viewing the diaspora as a distinct asset class that combines local knowledge with international experience.
- **Governance Focus**: Emphasis is placed on **Corporate Governance and Due Diligence**, ensuring that diaspora investors have the tools and transparency needed to invest confidently in the Pakistani market.

## Source Verification
- RDA inflows ($13.6B, $282M July) and account counts (956k) are verified against August 2026 SBP and financial news reports.
- FDI growth (+163%) is confirmed via March/April 2026 economic monitoring data.
- Startup funding recovery ($36.6M) is sourced from 2026 industry summits and monitoring reports.
