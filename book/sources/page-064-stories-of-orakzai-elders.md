# Sources: Page 64 — Stories of Orakzai Elders

This document records the academic and ethnographic sources supporting **Page 64: Stories of Orakzai Elders** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **King, L. W.** (1900). *Monograph on the Orakzai Country and Clans*. Punjab Government Press. (Reprinted with introduction by Akbar Ahmed, 1984).
   - **Details**: The foundational colonial-era monograph documenting Orakzai tribal structure and the Sikandar Shah origin legend.
   - **Claim Supported**: The "lost son" origin legend and historical tribal geography.

2. **Naseer, N.** (2026). "Early Warning by Women for Conflict Prevention in the Erstwhile Federally Administered Tribal Areas (Merged Districts)". *CSQ Journal*.
   - **Details**: Research using oral histories and field interviews with women in Orakzai and Kurram to document social change and conflict experiences.
   - **Claim Supported**: Women's oral history, displacement memories, and community resilience.

3. **IM Sciences**. (2021). "Preservation and Dissemination of Indigenous Knowledge in Pashtuns' Culture: The Role of Hujra as a Learning Hub".
   - **Details**: Study on the intergenerational transmission of traditional knowledge within the Hujra setting.
   - **Claim Supported**: Hujra as a social space for oral history and memory transmission.

4. **Ahmed, A. S.** (1980). *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*. Routledge & Kegan Paul.
   - **Details**: Anthropological study of tribal social structures, focusing on the Orakzai and Mohmand agencies.
   - **Claim Supported**: Social authority of elders and the role of tribal lineage in oral memory.

5. **Edwards, D. B.** (1996). *Heroes of the Age: Moral Fault Lines on the Afghan Frontier*. University of California Press.
   - **Details**: Analysis of how tribal histories and moral codes are constructed through narrative and oral tradition.
   - **Claim Supported**: Methodological distinction between tribal history and documented archive.
