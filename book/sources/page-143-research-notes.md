# Research Notes: Page 143 — National Digital Infrastructure

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 143: National Digital Infrastructure**.

## 1. National Performance Indicators (2026)
- **ICT Development Index (IDI):** Pakistan showed a **20% improvement** in the ITU IDI 2026, with its score rising from 56.4 in 2025 to **67.7 in 2026**. This reflects significant gains in connectivity, digital infrastructure, and access.
- **Broadband Growth:** Total high-capacity connections reached **5.10 million** in 2026, indicating strong growth in the country's fiber and fixed-broadband infrastructure.

## 2. The 5G Era (2026 Rollout)
- **Commercial Launch:** Commercial 5G services are scheduled to begin in **mid-August 2026**.
- **Phased Roadmap:** The rollout is concentrated in major urban centers (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad) initially (2026), with a planned expansion across all provinces and wider districts between **2026–2028**.
- **Operator Milestone:** Jazz has committed to rolling out 5G to **1,000 network sites** by mid-August 2026, with theoretical speeds reaching **1–10 Gbps**.

## 3. Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)
- **Identity Foundation:** NADRA remains the cornerstone of Pakistan's DPI, with CNIC issuance to adults increasing by **72%**.
- **Payment Ecosystem:** The **Raast** interoperable payment system is integrated with government platforms to drive financial inclusion.
- **Economic Impact:** Projections suggest that wholesale adoption of digital payments through DPI could boost Pakistan's GDP by up to **7%**.

## 4. Physical Backbone & Connectivity
- **Submarine Cables:** Pakistan is connected via major international systems including **AAE-1**, **SEA-ME-WE 3/4/5**, and the **Peace Cable**, which provide the essential link to global digital networks.
- **Internet Exchange Points (IXPs):** Strategic IXPs in **Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi** enable domestic peering, reducing latency and improving network efficiency.
- **Rural Connectivity:** Initiatives are focused on bridging the digital divide by extending fiber-optic backbones to remote districts like Orakzai to support education and healthcare.

- **Focus:** Advocating for the modernization of national digital infrastructure as a prerequisite for participating in the global digital economy.
