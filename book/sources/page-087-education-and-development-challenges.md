# Sources: Page 87 — Education & Development Challenges

This document records the primary and academic sources supporting **Page 87: Education & Development Challenges** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary Statistical & Official Sources

1. **Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS).** (2023). *7th Population and Housing Census 2023: District Report Orakzai*.
   - **Data**: Population counts, literacy rates (10+), rural/urban distribution.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai population (387,561) and literacy rate (~33.57%).

2. **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Elementary & Secondary Education Department (KPESED).** (2024). *Annual School Census (ASC) Report 2023-24*.
   - **Data**: School counts (449 Primary, 83 Middle, 37 High), enrolment figures, student flow (promotion/dropout rates).
   - **Claim Supported**: Education infrastructure and enrolment indicators for Orakzai District.

3. **Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.** (2021). *Accelerated Implementation Programme (AIP) Baseline Survey 2020*.
   - **Data**: Socio-economic indicators for merged districts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Post-merger development framework and baseline challenges.

4. **District Health Office (DHO) Orakzai.** (2024). *District Health Profile*.
   - **Data**: Health facility counts (BHUs, RHCs, DHQ).
   - **Claim Supported**: Health infrastructure status in Orakzai.

## Academic & Research Sources

5. **UN Women.** (2020). *Status of Women and Girls in Orakzai District*.
   - **Details**: Gender profile and disparities in education and health.
   - **Claim Supported**: Gender-specific challenges in Orakzai.

6. **Ahmad, Z., Shah, I., Alam, A., & Khan, H.** (2025). "Quality education in post-devolution scenario in Pakistan: evidence from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province." *Development in Practice*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of education quality and access in KP post-devolution.
   - **Claim Supported**: Provincial context for education reforms.

7. **World Bank.** (2026). *Where Are Pakistan's Out-of-School Children?*
   - **Details**: Analysis of OOSC in merged districts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Factors contributing to school dropout and non-enrolment.

## Research Gaps & Limitations
- **Private Sector Data**: While the ASC includes some private school data, the coverage of informal or non-registered educational centers may be incomplete.
- **Real-time Health Outcomes**: Maternal and infant mortality rates specific to Orakzai District (rather than regional FATA estimates) are difficult to verify with 2026 precision without localized health surveys.
