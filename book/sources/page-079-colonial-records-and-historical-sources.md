# Sources: Page 79 — Colonial Records & Historical Sources

This document records the archival, primary, and academic sources supporting **Page 79: Colonial Records & Historical Sources** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary Archival Collections

1. **India Office Records (IOR).** *British Library, London*.
   - **Series L/P&S**: Political and Secret Department Records (Frontier Affairs).
   - **Series V/10**: Administration Reports of the North-West Frontier Province (1901–1947).
   - **Shelfmark IOR/V/10/446-455**: Specific sequence of NWFP Administration Reports.
   - **Claim Supported**: Institutional framework of colonial documentation.

2. **General Staff, Army Headquarters, British India.** (1910). *A Dictionary of the Pathan Tribes on the North-West Frontier of India*.
   - **Archive**: British Library / Internet Archive (Digitized).
   - **Details**: Comprehensive index of tribal subdivisions and locations used for military intelligence.
   - **Claim Supported**: Colonial tribal classification systems and terminology.

3. **Punjab Government.** (1884). *Gazetteer of the Kohat District, 1883-84*.
   - **Archive**: British Library / National Archives of Pakistan.
   - **Details**: Detailed administrative profile of the district and its neighboring tribes (Orakzai, Bangash, Afridi).
   - **Claim Supported**: Methodology and scope of colonial gazetteers.

4. **Parliamentary Papers.** *House of Commons, UK*.
   - **Collection**: "Orakzai and Black Mountain Expeditions" (c. 1891-1898).
   - **Details**: Official reports and correspondence justifying military expeditions and recording outcomes.
   - **Claim Supported**: Military documentation and political reporting.

## Academic & Methodological Sources

5. **Beattie, Hugh.** (1997). *Tribe and State in Waziristan 1849-1883*. (Applicable to NWFP tribal management).
   - **Details**: Analysis of British tribal management strategies based on India Office records.
   - **Claim Supported**: Administrative purpose of colonial records.

6. **Gul Orakzai.** (n.d.). *The Ecstasy and Anarchy of Nonviolence: The Khudai Khidmatgar Resistance*. (ProQuest).
   - **Details**: Research utilizing both historical archives and oral traditions to explore frontier resistance.
   - **Claim Supported**: Value of cross-checking archives with local memory.

| Source Type | Strength | Limitation | Best Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Oral History** | Lived experience | Memory shifts | Internal community dynamics |
