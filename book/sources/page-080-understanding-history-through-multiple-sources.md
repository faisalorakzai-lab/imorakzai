# Sources: Page 80 — Understanding History Through Multiple Sources

This document records the methodological, academic, and case-study sources supporting **Page 80: Understanding History Through Multiple Sources** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Methodological References

1. **Kipping, M., Wadhwani, R. D., & Bucheli, M.** (2014). "Analyzing and Interpreting Historical Sources: A Basic Methodology." *History, Theory, Methods*.
   - **Details**: Academic guide to the use of triangulation to reduce bias and corroborate findings in historical research.
   - **Claim Supported**: Methodological triangulation principles.

2. **Hammersley, M.** (2008). "Troubles with Triangulation." *Advances in Mixed Methods Research*.
   - **Details**: Discussion on the requirement for independent observations before affirming a fact categorically.
   - **Claim Supported**: Independence vs. Multiplicity of sources.

3. **Vikström, L.** (2010). "Identifying Dissonant and Complementary Data through the Triangulation of Historical Sources." *International Journal of Social Research Methodology*.
   - **Details**: Study on how triangulation enables researchers to identify both corroboration and productive contradictions.
   - **Claim Supported**: Source complementarity and conflict analysis.

## Orakzai Case Study Sources

4. **Nature Portfolio.** (2026). "Tribal Traditions and Conservation Perspectives of Wild Plants in Orakzai District, Pakistan." *Scientific Reports*.
   - **Details**: Quantitative and ethnobotanical documentation of traditional food systems.
   - **Claim Supported**: Triangulating scientific data with local food traditions.

5. **EBSCO Host.** (2025). "Pakistan: Early Warning by Women for Conflict Prevention in the Erstwhile FATA."
   - **Details**: Research utilizing oral histories and field interviews with women in Kurram and Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Triangulating official security reports with gendered oral histories.

6. **General Staff, British India.** (1910). *A Dictionary of the Pathan Tribes*.
   - **Details**: Historical record of tribal subdivisions.
   - **Claim Supported**: Triangulating colonial classification with modern genealogical memory.

## Footnote & Citation Standards
- **Independent Corroboration**: Claims must specify if sources are independent or derivative.
- **Uncertainty Reporting**: Where sources disagree without resolution, the book reports the conflict rather than choosing one.
