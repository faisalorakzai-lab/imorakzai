# Sources: Page 89 — Migration & Urbanization

This document records the primary and academic sources supporting **Page 89: Migration & Urbanization** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary Official & Government Sources

1. **Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS).** (2023). *7th Population and Housing Census: Orakzai District Tables*.
   - **Details**: Total population of 387,561 across 52,104 households.
   - **Claim Supported**: 2023 population figures and 100% rural locality classification.

2. **Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.** (2024). *District Orakzai Profile: KP Board of Investment and Trade*.
   - **Details**: Data on urban-rural estimates and administrative centers.
   - **Claim Supported**: Administrative growth of Kalaya and Ghiljo.

3. **UNHCR / IOM.** (2025-2026). *Internal Displacement and Return Monitoring Reports*.
   - **Details**: Statistics on IDP returns to merged districts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Impact of forced displacement and return migration.

4. **Universal Service Fund (USF) Pakistan.** (2025). *Digital Connectivity Report: Merged Districts*.
   - **Details**: Expansion of mobile networks in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Digital connectivity as a bridge for the diaspora.

## Historical & Academic Sources

5. **Lefebvre, A.** (1999). *Kinship, Honour and Money in Rural Pakistan: Subsistence Economy and the Effects of International Migration*.
   - **Details**: Case study of migration from the Miranzai Valley and neighboring Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical migration to the Gulf and property acquisition in Hangu/Kohat.

6. **Bukhari, A. Q.** (2026). *Layered Continuity in Pakistan's Northwest Borderlands*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of Pashtun diaspora networks and urban adaptation.
   - **Claim Supported**: The trans-local nature of Orakzai identity in urban hubs like Karachi.

7. **Wahab, F.** (2024). *The consequences of Pakistan's counterterrorism policies: socio-cultural and political transformation in tribal districts*.
   - **Details**: Research on the settlement of IDPs in cities like Peshawar and Karachi.
   - **Claim Supported**: Conflict-induced migration and urbanization.

## Research Gaps & Limitations
- **Precise Remittance Figures**: Exact annual dollar amounts specifically for Orakzai District are not isolated in national bank data.
- **Urban Classification**: The mismatch between statistical "rural" status and functional "urban" centers (Kalaya) is an administrative artifact.
