# Research Notes: Page 155 — Global Pakistani Talent

## Overview
Page 155 analyzes the Pakistani diaspora as a strategic human capital asset. It highlights the achievements of global Pakistani professionals in 2026 and the transition from a "Brain Drain" narrative to a "Brain Circulation" model that drives innovation and investment back to Pakistan.

## Key Findings
1. **The Diaspora Asset**: Nearly 10 million Pakistanis live abroad (extrapolated for 2026), representing a vast network of knowledge and capital. A June 2026 NFAP report identified **10 Pakistani immigrants** as founders or co-founders of billion-dollar startups in the United States.
2. **Brain Circulation Model**: The policy shift in 2026 emphasizes "Brain Circulation." This involves engaging overseas experts in domestic sectors, such as the **Telemedicine Backup model**, where Pakistani physicians abroad provide remote diagnostic support to local hospitals.
3. **Freelance & Remote Success**: FY26 saw record freelance earnings of **$1.76 billion**, a 78% increase from the previous year. This "Digital Diaspora" allows talent to remain in Pakistan while serving global markets, effectively reversing the brain drain at the source.
4. **Global Digital Rise**: Pakistan's global digital ranking jumped **27 places** in 2026. This rise is attributed to the integration of global Pakistani professionals into the national digital economy through mentorship, investment, and technology transfer.
5. **The Orakzai Connection**: The global Orakzai diaspora, particularly in the Gulf and Europe, is increasingly active in **Virtual Mentorship**. Overseas valley natives use digital platforms to train local youth in AI, coding, and remote business management, creating a direct link between global expertise and regional opportunity.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Remittance-Plus Framework**: The page moves beyond financial remittances to value "Knowledge Remittances"—the transfer of skills, networks, and intellectual property.
- **Ownership Ladder**: The content follows the "Individual → Freelancer → Team → Agency → Global Exporter" progression to show how local talent scales to global value.
- **Nuanced Migration Narrative**: Research frames migration not as a loss, but as the creation of a "Global Pakistani Network" that acts as economic infrastructure.

## Source Verification
- US founder stats (10 billion-dollar cos) are verified via June 2026 NFAP report.
- Freelance earnings ($1.76B) are confirmed by July 2026 SBP and industry data.
- Telemedicine and Brain Circulation policies are sourced from 2026 Ministry of Planning and OPC records.
