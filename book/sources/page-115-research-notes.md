# Research Notes: Page 115 — Entrepreneurship in Pakistan

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 115: Entrepreneurship in Pakistan**.

## 1. Economic Context (2025–2026)
- **GDP Growth:** 3.7% (FY 2025–26), the highest in four years.
- **Per Capita Income:** US$1,901.
- **Inflation:** 6.19%.
- **Source:** Pakistan Economic Survey 2025–26.
- **Implication:** The economic environment is showing signs of recovery, providing a more stable backdrop for entrepreneurial activity.

## 2. SME Regulatory Framework (SBP, July 16, 2026)
- **Micro Enterprise:** Annual sales turnover up to PKR 30 million.
- **Small Enterprise:** Annual sales turnover above PKR 30 million to PKR 400 million.
- **Medium Enterprise:** Annual sales turnover above PKR 400 million to PKR 2 billion.
- **Startup Definition:** A micro, small, or medium enterprise up to five years old.
- **Source:** State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Revised Prudential Regulations for SME Financing.

## 3. Startup Ecosystem and Support
- **Pakistan Startup Fund (PSF):** 
    - Purpose: Catalyze private and foreign investment into Pakistani startups.
    - Mechanism: Non-equity grants covering up to 30% of an investment round.
    - Grant Range: US$50,000 to US$1,000,000.
    - Source: Ministry of IT & Telecom / Ignite.
- **National Incubation Centers (NICs):** Provide mentorship, networking, and business development support for technology-enabled startups.

## 4. SMEDA Strategic Pillars (2026)
- **Formalization:** Moving businesses into the formal economy.
- **Inclusion:** Deepening participation of women and youth.
- **Market Access:** Expanding local and international trade.
- **Value Chains:** Strengthening domestic production links.
- **Competitiveness:** Improving efficiency and quality.
- **Capital:** Unlocking investment and credit.
- **Climate Resilience:** Building sustainable business models.

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai
- **Profile:** Contemporary Pakistani technology entrepreneur and computer scientist.
- **Pathway:** Computer Science → Software → Technology Projects → Digital Products → Entrepreneurship.
- **Projects:** Orakzai Group, OkzByte Hub, Orakzai Bond, Shamim Forever.
- **Technical Focus:** Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence, Fintech, and Digital Infrastructure.
- **Role:** Illustrates the "Builder's Model" where technical expertise is leveraged for organizational creation.

## 6. Orakzai and Rural Entrepreneurship
- **Barriers:** Connectivity, electricity, transport, and financial literacy.
- **Opportunity:** Digital entrepreneurship, e-commerce, and diaspora-led mentorship can bridge geographic gaps.
- **Cultural Enterprise:** Potential for sustainable commercialization of crafts, textiles, and heritage tourism without erasing cultural meaning.

## 7. Evidence Boundaries
- **Statistical Discipline:** National statistics (PES, SBP) are clearly distinguished from individual case studies.
- **No Promotion:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as *one case study*, not as the representative of all Pakistani entrepreneurs.
- **Verification:** Every regulatory and economic claim is cross-referenced with official institutional records (Ministry of Finance, SBP, SMEDA).
