# Research Notes: Page 146 — Digital Governance

## Overview
Page 146 examines the transformation of the Pakistani state into a digital entity, focusing on the legal, institutional, and technological frameworks established under the **Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025**. It highlights the role of the **Pakistan Digital Authority (PDA)** and the implementation of **Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)**.

## Key Findings
1. **Legal Mandate**: The Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025, enacted in January 2025, serves as the federal law that consolidated digital planning and governance under the PDA.
2. **Institutional Shift**: By June 2026, 70% of cabinet operations were digitalized, marking a significant departure from traditional paper-based administration.
3. **DPI Stack**: Pakistan has successfully integrated its foundational digital systems—Identity (NADRA), Payments (Raast), and Data Exchange—into a layered stack that supports all public services.
4. **Citizen-Centricity**: The 2026 governance model emphasizes "Life-Event" services, where birth, education, and business registration are organized around the citizen's journey rather than departmental silos.
5. **National Digital Masterplan**: As of August 2026, the PDA is actively designing the National Digital Masterplan to guide sectoral digital transformation across all federal and provincial agencies.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Definition Distinction**: The text clearly differentiates between "e-government" (electronic delivery) and "digital governance" (institutional transformation and oversight).
- **DPI Framework**: Digital identity and payments are framed as the infrastructure layer, while services like taxation and health are application layers.
- **Inclusion in Orakzai**: The research notes that digital governance is particularly impactful for remote regions like Orakzai, where mobile-first services reduce the need for physical travel to administrative centers.

## Source Verification
- Legislation and institutional roles are verified against PDA official releases (July/August 2026).
- Cabinet digitalization stats are based on Business Recorder reports (June 2026).
- DPI framework is aligned with World Bank and GlobalDev economic analyses (2025-2026).
