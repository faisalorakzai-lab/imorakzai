## Evidence Boundaries

- **No Existing Orakzai Archive:** No single official "Orakzai Oral History Archive" currently exists. All references are proposed frameworks.
- **Testimony vs. Fact:** Oral history is evidence of remembered experience; it is not independently verified historical fact unless corroborated.
- **Privacy Boundary:** Sensitive family or personal information must be protected through granular access levels.
- **AI Disclaimer:** AI tools assist in processing but do not replace human review or historical authority.
