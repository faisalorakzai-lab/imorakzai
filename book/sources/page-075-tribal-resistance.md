# Sources: Page 75 — Tribal Resistance

This document records the historical and academic sources supporting **Page 75: Tribal Resistance** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Tripodi, Christian.** (2013). *Edge of Empire: The British Political Officer and Tribal Administration on the North-West Frontier 1877–1947*. Anthem Press.
   - **Details**: Academic study of the Political Agent system and the center-point of resistance in the Orakzai hills.
   - **Claim Supported**: Conceptual framework for resistance vs. negotiation and the role of the Political Agent.

2. **Hutchinson, H. D.** (1898). *The Campaign in Tirah, 1897-1898*. London: Macmillan.
   - **Details**: Contemporary military account of the 1897 uprising and Orakzai involvement.
   - **Claim Supported**: Specific dates and locations for armed resistance events in 1897.

3. **Arbab, S.** (2019). "The Khudai Khidmatgar Resistance in the North-West Frontier". *University of California*.
   - **Details**: Research on Pashtun resistance movements and the role of oral traditions in preserving memory.
   - **Claim Supported**: Social and political forms of non-cooperation.

4. **Yousaf, F.** (2020). "Pashtun Jirga and prospects of peace and conflict". *JSTOR*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of the Jirga's role in tribal politics and its use as a tool for both resistance and mediation.
   - **Claim Supported**: The Jirga as a deliberative institution for collective resistance.

5. **Shah, S. W. A.** (2012). *Political Reforms in the Federally Administered Tribal Areas*. Heidelberg University.
   - **Details**: Historical overview of tribal administration and the evolution of the Orakzai Agency.
   - **Claim Supported**: Administrative context of the Orakzai district and historical resistance to centralized control.

## Documented Terminology

| Term | Context | Historical Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lashkar** | Armed Action | A tribal volunteer militia organized for a specific campaign or defense. |
| **Chatti** | Punishment | A fine imposed by the colonial administration on a tribe for raids or non-compliance. |
| **Muajib** | Negotiation | A frontier allowance or subsidy paid to a tribe to maintain peace and road safety. |
| **Masharan** | Authority | Tribal elders who represent the community in negotiations and resistance. |
| **Pakhto** | Language/Identity | The Middle Pashto dialect group encompassing Orakzai, Bangash, and Dawarwala. |
