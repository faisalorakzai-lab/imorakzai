# Sources: Page 58 — Funerals & Community Traditions

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 58: Funerals & Community Traditions** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Ahmed, A. S.**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*. (1980).
   - **Details**: Classic ethnography discussing the social significance of death in tribal structure and the role of the community in funeral rites.
   - **Claim Supported**: Tribal and community involvement in funerals; social role of mourning.

2. **Shinwari, N. A.**: *Political and social change in Pakistan's tribal areas: Understanding Pashtun youth*. (2025).
   - **Details**: Analysis of how younger generations in former FATA, including Orakzai, are adapting traditional practices to modern and urban contexts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Impact of urbanization and conflict on funeral practices; digital memory.

3. **Akbar, S. A.**: *Religious Presence And Symbolism In Pukhtun Society*. (2013).
   - **Details**: Discusses the growth of saints and religious symbolism among Orakzai Shia communities and the role of ritual in social life.
   - **Claim Supported**: Religious symbolism in Orakzai; sectarian variations in ritual.

4. **Aadil, M. & Shahzaib, R.**: *Contextual representation of mourning rituals: A case study of rural Punjab (Pakistan)*. (2024).
   - **Details**: Comparative ethnographic study on mourning rituals in Pakistan, highlighting regional variations and Islamic frameworks.
   - **Claim Supported**: Islamic funeral sequence (Ghusl, Kafan, Janazah); regional mourning customs.

5. **Historical Records of the North-West Frontier**: *Ethnographic accounts of Tirah tribes*.
   - **Details**: British-era records describing the burial grounds and communal support systems of the Orakzai and Afridi tribes.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical burial practices; role of Hujra and elders.
