# Research Notes: Page 52 — Orakzai Traditions

## Core Concept
- **Tradition**: Practice, custom, knowledge, or expression transmitted across generations.
- **Cycle**: Memory → Practice → Transmission → Adaptation → Continuity.

## Key Traditions & Documented Evidence

### 1. Jirga (Orakzai-Specific)
- **Source**: Muhammad Khalil (2025), "Jirga System in Orakzai," *Pashto* Journal.
- **Key Features**: Consultation, elder representation, dispute resolution, regional structural variations.
- **Model**: Dispute → Consultation → Jirga → Discussion → Reconciliation.

### 2. Folk Songs & Oral Tradition
- **Source**: Dr. Shakir Orakzai (2025/26), "The Orakzai Folk Songs," *Pashto* Quarterly.
- **Types**: Tappa, Charbeta, Landay (adapted to Orakzai context).
- **Transmission**: Oral performance during gatherings, weddings, and communal work.

### 3. Ethnobotanical Knowledge (TEK)
- **Source**: Zamin et al. (2026), *Scientific Reports*.
- **Plants**:
  - *Olea europaea* subsp. *cuspidata* (Khowa): Wild olive, culturally significant.
  - *Berberis lycium* (Ziar larki): Medicinal use.
  - *Daphne papyracea*: Traditional bark use.
  - *Salix babylonica* (Wala): Willow for crafts/ecology.

### 4. Life-Cycle Traditions
- **Weddings**: Nikah (contract), Walima (feast), Attan (dance), family-led negotiations.
- **Birth**: Naming, community support.
- **Funerals**: Communal mourning and support.

### 5. Material & Social Traditions
- **Food**: Meat-based specialties, regional spices, communal eating (social bonding).
- **Clothing**: Shalwar Kameez, headwear (Lungee/Pakol), regional embroidery.
- **Mela**: 2024 Spring Mela as a contemporary example of cultural gathering.

## Digital Preservation
- **Tools**: Mobile recording, social media archiving, AI transcription for Pashto oral history.
- **Model**: Human Knowledge + Digital Tools → Digital Preservation.
