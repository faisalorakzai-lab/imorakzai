# Sources: Page 63 — Pashto Proverbs

This document records the academic and linguistic sources supporting **Page 63: Pashto Proverbs** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Tair, M. N. & Edwards, L.** (2009). *Rohi Mataluna: Pashto Proverbs*. Resource Publications.
   - **Details**: A comprehensive collection of 1,350 Pashto proverbs with English translations and cultural annotations.
   - **Claim Supported**: Major corpus of Pashto proverbs, thematic classifications, and cultural meanings.

2. **Nasir, A. H.** (2022). "Place of Dawarwala, Orakzai and Bangash Dialects in the Classification of Pashto's Dialects Groups". *Palatana*, Vol. 1, Issue 1.
   - **Details**: Linguistic analysis grouping Orakzai dialect with Bangash and Dawarwala based on phonetic characteristics.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai-specific linguistic context and dialect classification.

3. **Ali, K., Saleem, A., & Ullah, I.** (2023). "Paremiological Analysis of Proverbs in Pashto and English: A Cross-cultural Pragmatic Study". *NUML Journal of Critical Inquiry*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of Pashto proverbs related to bravery, honor, and social values.
   - **Claim Supported**: Cultural themes of honor (Nang, Ghairat) and social interaction in proverbs.

4. **Sanauddin, N.** (2015). *Proverbs and Patriarchy: Analysis of Linguistic Sexism and Gender Relations among the Pashtuns of Pakistan*. University of Glasgow.
   - **Details**: Ethnographic study of how proverbs encode social values and gender relations.
   - **Claim Supported**: Social function of proverbs in Pashtun society.

5. **Orakzai, A.** (2025). "Globalization's Influence on Pashtun Cultural Values: A Study of Proverbs about Women across Different Age Groups". *PJSEL*.
   - **Details**: Contemporary research on the relevance of traditional proverbs in modern Pashtun contexts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Contemporary transmission and adaptation of proverbs.
