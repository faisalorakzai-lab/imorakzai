# Research Notes: Page 44 — Valleys & Rivers

## Major Valleys
1. **Mastura Valley**:
   - **Location**: Central Orakzai.
   - **Watercourse**: Mastura River.
   - **Landscape**: Fertile, sheltered by mountains, significant cultivated land.
   - **Historical Significance**: Heart of the Orakzai territory, historical settlements.
2. **Khanki Valley**:
   - **Location**: Situated between the Samana Range and Karagh Ghar.
   - **Watercourse**: Khanki Toi.
   - **Landscape**: Narrower in parts, fertile valley floor, agricultural potential.
3. **Khurmana Valley**:
   - **Location**: Western part of the territory, near the Kurram border.
   - **Watercourse**: Khurmana River (tributary of Kurram River).
   - **Landscape**: Rugged, historically significant for western tribal sections.

## Major Watercourses
1. **Mastura River**:
   - **Origin**: Rises near Mount Mitta (Mittughar, 12,470 ft) in the Safed Koh (Spin Ghar).
   - **Flow**: Flows eastward through the Mastura Valley.
   - **Confluence**: Tributary of the Bara River.
2. **Khanki Toi**:
   - **Origin**: Rises near Mount Mitta in the Safed Koh.
   - **Flow**: Flows through the Khanki Valley.
   - **Confluence**: Source river of the Kohat Toi.
   - **Terminology**: "Toi" is a regional term for a stream or river.
3. **Khurmana River**:
   - **Origin**: Rises near Mount Mitta.
   - **Flow**: Flows into the Kurram River.

## Hydrological System
- **Conceptual Flow**: Mountain Runoff → Seasonal Streams/Tributaries → Main Rivers (Mastura, Khanki Toi) → Valleys → Settlement/Agriculture.
- **Seasonal Nature**: Numerous dry watercourses in the southwest; flow is dependent on rainfall events.
- **Valley Morphology**: Steep mountain ridges descending to slopes and fertile valley floors where water accumulates.

## Settlement & Agriculture
- **Relationship**: Water availability + flat valley floor land = primary settlement zones.
- **Cultivation**: Primarily in Mastura and Khanki valleys; wheat, orchards (walnut, apricot).
- **Soils**: Valley-floor soils (often alluvial in lower reaches) support irrigation.
