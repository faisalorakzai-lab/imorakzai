# Sources: Page 52 — Orakzai Traditions

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 52: Orakzai Traditions** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Khalil, Muhammad (Shakir Orakzai)**: *Jirga System in Orakzai*. Pashto Journal, Vol. 54, No. 669 (December 2025).
   - **Details**: Academic analysis of the structural and functional characteristics of the Jirga in Orakzai District.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai-specific Jirga dynamics and reconciliation models.

2. **Orakzai, Dr. Shakir**: *The Orakzai Folk Songs*. Pashto Quarterly Journal, No. 112-113 (2025/26).
   - **Details**: Documentation of local folk song types, performance contexts, and oral transmission in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai folk music and oral tradition.

3. **Zamin, M., Adnan, M., Hamdard, M.H. et al.**: *Tribal traditions and conservation perspectives of wild plants in Orakzai District, Pakistan*. Scientific Reports (2026).
   - **Details**: Ethnobotanical study documenting 123 plant species and their traditional tribal uses.
   - **Claim Supported**: Traditional ecological knowledge (TEK) and specific plant uses (Khowa, Ziar larki, Wala).

4. **Ahmed, Akbar S.**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*. Routledge.
   - **Details**: Ethnographic context of Pashtun tribal structures, including the Orakzai Agency.
   - **Claim Supported**: General Pashtun cultural framework and social reciprocity.

5. **Radio Pakistan / KP Government**: *Orakzai Spring Mela 2024 Reports*.
   - **Details**: Contemporary documentation of cultural festivals, tourism, and community commerce in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: 2024 Spring Mela as a contemporary cultural practice.
