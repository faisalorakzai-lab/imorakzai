# Research Notes: Page 147 — Digital Assets & Regulation

## Overview
Page 147 examines the development of the regulatory framework for digital assets in Pakistan, focusing on the shift from the 2018 restrictive environment to the comprehensive **Virtual Assets Act 2026**. It highlights the role of the **Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA)** and the integration of digital value into the formal financial system.

## Key Findings
1. **Legislative Milestone**: The Virtual Assets Act 2026, passed in March 2026, provides the first comprehensive legal framework for virtual assets (VAs) and virtual asset service providers (VASPs) in Pakistan.
2. **Regulatory Pivot**: In April 2026, the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) issued a circular ending the 8-year crypto banking ban, allowing licensed firms to access formal banking channels for the first time since 2018.
3. **Institutional Stack**: PVARA serves as the primary regulator, working alongside SBP (payments/banking) and SECP (security tokens/RWA) to ensure market integrity and consumer protection.
4. **Licensing Framework**: The 2026 draft regulations introduce mandatory licensing for VASPs, including requirements for capital adequacy, cybersecurity standards, and AML/CFT compliance (FATF Travel Rule).
5. **Economic Function**: The framework follows the principle of "Regulation by Function," treating digital assets based on their actual economic use (payment, utility, security, or commodity) rather than just the underlying technology.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Definition Hierarchy**: Digital Assets are framed as the broad category, with cryptocurrencies, stablecoins, and security tokens as specific subsets.
- **Innovation vs. Risk**: The research emphasizes that effective regulation distinguishes between legitimate technological innovation and illicit financial activities.
- **Regional Context**: The Orakzai context is used to illustrate how a regulated environment provides "safe rails" for regional entrepreneurs and diaspora communities to participate in the digital economy.
