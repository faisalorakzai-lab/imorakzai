# Sources: Page 88 — Infrastructure & Connectivity

This document records the primary and academic sources supporting **Page 88: Infrastructure & Connectivity** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary Official & Government Sources

1. **Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.** (2026, June 22). *Regional Information Office Handout No. 940*.
   - **Details**: Allocation of Rs 18 billion development package for Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Rs 9.45 billion for roads and Rs 7.4 billion for Khan Mir Khel Dam.

2. **Communication & Works (C&W) Department KP.** (2024). *Orakzai Road Schemes Portfolio*.
   - **Details**: Listing of road construction, blacktopping, and rehabilitation projects in Lower and Upper Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Ongoing road infrastructure programming.

3. **Planning & Development (P&D) Department KP.** (2026). *Annual Development Programme (ADP) 2026-27*.
   - **Details**: Strategic investment targets for merged districts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Infrastructure prioritization in post-merger development.

4. **World Bank.** (2025, September 10). *Procurement Plan: KP Merged Districts Education & Infrastructure Projects*.
   - **Details**: Package-1 for improvement and upgradation of rural access roads in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: International support for rural connectivity and flood-affected road rehabilitation.

## Historical & Academic Sources

5. **ReliefWeb / F.P. Report.** (2009). *Flood protection walls completed in Orakzai*.
   - **Details**: Completion of Sabzi Khel flood protection wall benefiting 1,500 residents.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical evidence of water and flood management infrastructure.

6. **Bukhari, A. Q.** (2026). *Layered Continuity in Pakistan's Northwest Borderlands*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of infrastructure contracts and development packages in the frontier.
   - **Claim Supported**: Social and political context of infrastructure development.

7. **Universal Service Fund (USF) Pakistan.** (2025). *Annual Report on Digital Connectivity in Merged Districts*.
   - **Details**: Expansion of mobile and broadband networks in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Growth of digital connectivity layers.

## Research Gaps & Limitations
- **Project Completion Status**: While allocations (June 2026) are documented, the exact percentage of physical completion for the Khan Mir Khel Dam as of late 2026 requires field verification.
- **Private Transport Stats**: Number of private vehicles registered in Orakzai District (vs. Hangu/Kohat) is difficult to isolate from provincial data.
