## Evidence Boundaries

- **Internet vs. Web:** The text strictly distinguishes between the infrastructure (Internet) and the service (Web).
- **Invention Myth:** No single individual is credited with "inventing" the internet; it is presented as a collaborative research achievement.
- **Regional Data:** Internet access in Orakzai is contextualized within the post-2018 administrative changes and mobile broadband growth.
- **First Message:** The "LO" message is accurately described as an interrupted "LOGIN" command.
