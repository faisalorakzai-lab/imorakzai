# Research Notes: Page 130 — The Future of Artificial Intelligence

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 130: The Future of Artificial Intelligence**.

## 1. Technological Paradigms (2026)
- **Agentic AI:** A shift from "assistive AI" (chatbots) to "agentic AI" (systems that act). In 2026, AI agents are characterized by multi-step planning, tool use, and autonomous execution within defined boundaries.
- **Multi-Agent Systems (MAS):** Breakthrough year for MAS where specialized agents (Research, Analysis, Coding, Security) collaborate under central orchestration.
- **Small Language Models (SLMs) & Edge AI:** Adoption of efficient, locally-deployed models on personal devices to enhance privacy, speed, and cost-effectiveness.
- **Multimodal Convergence:** Integration of Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) capable of reasoning across text, image, audio, and video simultaneously.

## 2. Institutional Landscape (Pakistan 2026)
- **Islamabad AI Declaration (Feb 2026):** Pakistan's formal commitment to sovereign, responsible, and capability-driven AI. It emphasizes sovereign compute capacity and resilient digital infrastructure.
- **National AI Policy 2025:** Foundational roadmap aiming to create 3 million digital jobs by 2030 and boost GDP through AI-driven transformation.
- **Sovereign AI Infrastructure:** Initiatives by Ignite National Technology Fund and MoITT to develop indigenous high-performance computing (HPC) clusters for national AI research.

## 3. Language & Heritage Preservation
- **Pashto Language Dataset of Speech and Text (PLDST):** A 2026 research-led corpus created to facilitate Pashto voice processing and text-to-speech synthesis.
- **PsOCR Research (2025/2026):** Benchmarking Large Multimodal Models for Optical Character Recognition in low-resource languages like Pashto, enabling the digitization of tribal archives.
- **Cultural Alignment:** Framing the future of AI as a "Heritage Bridge" that uses advanced technology to amplify traditional Pashtun wisdom and oral histories.

## 4. Socio-Economic Context
- **Sovereignty vs. Global Participation:** The dual need for national technological independence (sovereign AI) and active participation in the global AI ecosystem.
- **Education & Mentorship:** AI tutors as complements to human teachers, providing personalized learning while maintaining human oversight.
- **Ethics & Values:** The transition from "What can AI do?" to "What kind of civilization will we build with it?"

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** His documented interests in systems architecture, blockchain, and digital infrastructure align with the "Agentic & Sovereign" philosophy of 2026.
- **Philosophy:** "The algorithm provides the speed; the human provides the purpose."
