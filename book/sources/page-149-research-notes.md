# Research Notes: Page 149 — The Internet of Value

## Overview
Page 149 explores the transition from the "Internet of Information" to the "Internet of Value" (IoV). It details how digital networks are evolving to move economic value—money, ownership, and credentials—with the same ease as information.

## Key Findings
1. **The Architectural Shift**: The IoV is not just a single technology but a convergence of **Blockchain**, **AI**, **Digital Identity**, and **Tokenization**. In 2026, this convergence is enabling real-time liquidity and programmable settlement.
2. **AI-Driven Value**: A major trend in 2026 is the rise of **AI Agents** capable of executing on-chain transactions. These agents manage liquidity flows and automate complex financial tasks under predefined permissions.
3. **Pakistan's Digital Rise**: Pakistan's **ITU ID score** has risen to **67.7** in 2026, reflecting a significant jump in digital maturity. The **National Digital Masterplan** coordinates this journey, aiming for full digitalization of state payments by the end of the year.
4. **Interoperable Rails**: The success of **Raast** (Rs 20T+ milestone) serves as the foundational rail for the IoV in Pakistan, enabling instant P2P, P2M, and Bulk disbursements.
5. **Programmable Economy**: The concept of "Money as Software" is becoming a reality, where payments are triggered by smart contracts when specific conditions are met, reducing manual friction.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Conceptual Progression**: The page follows the "Web 1.0 (Read) → Web 2.0 (Read/Write) → Web3 (Read/Write/Own)" model to frame the IoV.
- **Scarcity vs. Abundance**: The research highlights the "Double Spend" problem—explaining why value requires security and scarcity, unlike information which thrives on abundance.
- **Regional Context**: The Orakzai context is used to show how the IoV empowers the diaspora, allowing them to participate in the local economy through seamless cross-border value transfer.

## Source Verification
- ITU ID score (67.7) and Digital Masterplan goals are verified against 2026 government and industry reports.
- Global IoV trends (AI agents, stablecoin integration) are based on J.P. Morgan and Arthur D. Little's 2026 outlooks.
- Raast operational data is confirmed via SBP's 2026 milestones.
