## Geographic & Topographical Data

- **Mountain Ranges**:
    - **Karagh Ghar Range** (Kahu Ghar): A significant spur of the **Safed Koh** mountains, dominating the central and upper regions.
    - **Samana Range**: A high-altitude ridge (6,000–7,000 ft) forming the southern boundary between Orakzai and Hangu.
- **Principal Valleys**:
    - **Mastura Valley**: Known for its fertile tracts and the Mastura River.
    - **Khanki Valley**: Situated between the Samana and Karagh Ghar ranges.
    - **Kharmana & Bara Valleys**: Extending into the northern and western parts of the territory.
- **Rivers & Hydrology**:
    - **Mastura River**: Flows eastward through the central valley.
    - **Khanki Toi**: A major stream originating in the western hills.
- **Climate**:
    - **Winter**: Characterized by significant snowfall in higher elevations (Tirah).
    - **Summer**: Temperate in the upper valleys, with isolated heavy rainfall (up to 75mm in extremes as per PMD).
- **Land Use**: Predominantly mountainous; agriculture is concentrated in the 11.5% of cultivable land along river banks and valley floors.
- **Vegetation**: Mixed forest (Pinus wallichiana, etc.) and fruit orchards (walnut, apricot, apple) in the fertile valleys.
