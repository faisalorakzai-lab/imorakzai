# Sources: Page 86 — Administrative Evolution

This document records the primary and academic sources supporting **Page 86: Administrative Evolution** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary Legal & Constitutional Sources

1. **Government of Pakistan.** (1973). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan*.
   - **Articles**: 246, 247.
   - **Claim Supported**: Constitutional status of FATA and Orakzai Agency.

2. **National Assembly of Pakistan.** (2018). *The Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 2018*.
   - **Details**: Repeal of Article 247 and merger of FATA with KP.
   - **Claim Supported**: 2018 administrative merger.

3. **Frontier Crimes Regulation (FCR).** (1901).
   - **Details**: Colonial legal code governing the frontier.
   - **Claim Supported**: Legal framework of the Political Agency system.

## Administrative Records

4. **Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.** (2018). *Notification: Integration of FATA Secretariat into KP*.
   - **Details**: Administrative restructuring post-merger.
   - **Claim Supported**: Transition from FATA Secretariat to provincial departments.

5. **District Courts Orakzai.** (2024). *Historical Overview of Orakzai*.
   - **Details**: Transition from FR Kohat to Agency in 1973.
   - **Claim Supported**: 1973 establishment date.

6. **Revenue & Estate Department KP.** (2025). *Land Settlement in Merged Districts*.
   - **Details**: Ongoing process of land revenue settlement.
   - **Claim Supported**: Shift from informal land tenure to formal settlement.

## Academic & Research Sources

7. **Ullah, A.** (2009). "The Governance of Federally Administered Tribal Areas under FCR." *National Institute of Historical and Cultural Research*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of the Political Agent system.
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of the PA and administrative hierarchy.

8. **Khan, MI., Noorani, GM., & Khan, HU.** (2025). "Merger of Federally Administered Tribal Areas: Mapping the Implementation of Administrative and Judicial Reforms." *International Journal of Social Sciences*.
   - **Details**: Evaluation of the post-merger transition.
   - **Claim Supported**: Ongoing challenges in administrative integration.

9. **Ahmed, Akbar S.** (1980). *Pukhtun Economy and Society*. Routledge.
   - **Details**: Study of tribal authority and early Agency administration.
   - **Claim Supported**: Pre-modern authority and Malik system.
