# Research Notes: Page 59 — Festivals & Celebrations

## Core Concept
- **Celebration as Social Cohesion**: Festivals in Orakzai serve to reinforce family ties, religious identity, and community belonging.
- **Faith and Culture**: The intersection of Islamic requirements and Pashtun cultural expressions (e.g., Eid in the Hujra).
- **Modern Programming**: The emergence of organized district festivals (Jashan-e-Baharan) as tools for tourism and peace-building.

## Key Findings

### 1. Religious Festivals (Islamic)
- **Eid-ul-Fitr**: Marks the end of Ramadan. Focus on prayer, charity (Fitrana), family visits, and new clothing.
- **Eid-ul-Adha**: Commemorates Prophet Ibrahim's obedience. Focus on sacrifice (Qurbani), sharing meat, and community hospitality.
- **Hujra Role**: In Pashtun communities, the Hujra becomes a central greeting point for men after Eid prayers.

### 2. Orakzai Spring Festival (Jashan-e-Baharan 2024)
- **Context**: A modern organized event (June 2024) at Kharasha-Khwa Storikhel, Orakzai.
- **Activities**:
    - **Traditional Sports**: Wrestling, Kabaddi, Stone Lifting, Tug of War.
    - **Modern Sports**: Jeep races, motorcycle races, paragliding, cricket, football, volleyball.
    - **Performances**: Horse and camel shows, cultural stalls, folk music.
- **Evidence Level**: Contemporary / Modern Organized Event.

### 3. Traditional Sports (Regional / Pashtun-Wide)
- **Kabaddi**: A popular contact sport involving raiding and blocking.
- **Wrestling (Pehlwani)**: Traditional grappling focused on strength and technique.
- **Stone Lifting**: A test of raw physical power, common in rural Pakistan.
- **Arrow Shooting / Archery**: Historical martial skill now practiced as a festive sport.

### 4. Cultural Elements
- **Attan**: The traditional Pashtun circular dance, performed at festive gatherings.
- **Hospitality (Melmastia)**: Festive occasions are prime times for the practice of extreme hospitality toward guests and travellers.
- **Clothing**: New *Khet Partug* and *Pakol* are common for festive days.

## Evidence Hierarchy
- **A. Orakzai-Specific**: Jashan-e-Baharan 2024 (Modern); specific local mela traditions (Oral History).
- **B. Pashtun-Wide**: Hujra Eid traditions; Attan dance; Melmastia during festivals.
- **C. Regional**: Kabaddi; stone lifting; wrestling (common across KP/Punjab).
- **D. Islamic**: Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha (Religious foundation).
- **E. Contemporary**: Paragliding; jeep races; digital festival announcements; cricket/football.
- **F. Seasonal**: Spring festivals (Jashan-e-Baharan).

## Documented Terminology
- **Jashan-e-Baharan**: Spring Festival (Persian/Urdu influence).
- **Mela**: Fair/Festival (Regional).
- **Eid-ul-Fitr / Eid-ul-Adha**: Islamic festivals.
- **Hujra**: Pashtun community space.
- **Attan**: Pashtun traditional dance.
