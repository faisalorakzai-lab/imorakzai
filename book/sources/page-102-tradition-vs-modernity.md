# Sources: Page 102 — Tradition vs Modernity

This source record documents the evidence used for **Page 102: Tradition vs Modernity** in *I’M ORAKZAI*. The page explores the relationship between ancestral heritage and modern institutional life.

## Source 1 — Sociological Foundation

**Author:** W. Asghar  
**Title:** *FATA Merger: Impacts on Status of Tribal Women*  
**Institution / Journal:** *Journal of Development and Social Sciences*  
**Year:** 2024  
**URL:** [JDSS Article](https://ojs.jdss.org.pk/journal/article/view/1067)  
**Claim supported:** Analysis of the 2018 FATA merger's impact on social status, human rights, and the transition from tribal norms to constitutional governance.  
**Evidence level:** Academic scholarship.

## Source 2 — Institutional Change

**Author:** Government of Pakistan  
**Title:** *Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 2018*  
**Publisher:** Gazette of Pakistan  
**Year:** 2018  
**Claim supported:** The formal institutional shift from the Frontier Crimes Regulation (FCR) to the integration of former FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.  
**Evidence level:** Official constitutional record.

## Source 3 — Cultural Adaptation

**Institution:** International Hindu University (IHU) / ResearchGate  
**Title:** *Impact of Modernization on Pashtun Culture and the Role of Institutions*  
**Year:** 2024  
**URL:** [ResearchGate Link](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404958184_Tradition_vs_Modernity)  
**Claim supported:** Analysis of how Pashtun institutions like Hujra and Jirga adapt to modern environments rather than disappearing.

## Evidence Boundary

- **No Westernization Equivalence**: Modernization (education, technology) is not presented as synonymous with Westernization.
- **No Backwardness Framing**: Traditional institutions (Jirga, Hujra) are described as social structures, not as signs of backwardness.
- **No Invention**: Festival origins, songs, or genealogies are not invented. The 2024 Spring Mela is correctly labeled as a contemporary event.
- **Gaps Acknowledged**: Explicitly states that research on village-level cultural change and digital Hujra social impact is limited.
