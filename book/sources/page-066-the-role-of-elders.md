# Sources: Page 66 — The Role of Elders

This document records the academic and ethnographic sources supporting **Page 66: The Role of Elders** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Alam, J.** (2024). "The Role of Local Supporters in Promoting Social Cohesion in District Orakzai Khyber Pakhtunkhwa". *Human Nature Journal of Social Sciences (HNJSS)*.
   - **Details**: Qualitative study exploring how local elders and supporters stabilize the social fabric in the Orakzai district.
   - **Claim Supported**: Elders as primary actors in promoting social cohesion and stability.

2. **Naseer, N.** (2026). "Early Warning by Women for Conflict Prevention in the Erstwhile Federally Administered Tribal Areas (Merged Districts)". *CSQ Journal*.
   - **Details**: Research on the influence of elder women in conflict prevention and family guidance in Orakzai and Kurram.
   - **Claim Supported**: Elder women's role in community safety and family authority.

3. **Infinity Press**. (2023). "Role of Malik in Pukhtoon Tribal Areas: A Case Study of Village Mirbak (Orakzai Agency)".
   - **Details**: Ethnographic study of traditional leadership structures in an Orakzai village.
   - **Claim Supported**: The Malik institution as a representative and administrative role for elders.

4. **Ahmed, A. S.** (1980). *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*. Routledge.
   - **Details**: Anthropological analysis of tribal leadership, seniority, and the social organization of Pashtun tribes.
   - **Claim Supported**: Distinction between natural leadership (seniority) and formal state-appointed roles.

5. **Jany, J.** (2020). "Pashtun Jirga and Prospects of Peace and Conflict Resolution in Pakistan's 'Tribal' Frontier". *JSTOR / Third World Quarterly*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of the Jirga as a collective decision-making body led by elders.
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of elders in mediation and customary law.
