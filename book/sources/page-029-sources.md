# Sources: Page 29 — Pashtun Oral History

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 29: Pashtun Oral History** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Oral History Association**: *Principles and Best Practices*.
   - **Publication**: Oral History Association (OHA) Guidelines.
   - **Details**: Professional standards for conducting, preserving, and ethically handling oral history interviews and oral traditions.
   - **Claim Supported**: Methodological framework for oral testimony, informed consent, and documentation.

2. **Alessandro Portelli**: *What Makes Oral History Different*.
   - **Author**: Alessandro Portelli.
   - **Publication**: *The Death of Luigi Trastulli and Other Stories: Form and Meaning in Oral History*, State University of New York Press.
   - **Details**: Seminal text in oral history theory addressing subjectivity, memory, and the relationship between oral narrative and documentary fact.
   - **Claim Supported**: Oral history as subjective meaning, memory analysis, and historical context.

3. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *Oral Literature in Iran & Folklore of Afghanistan*.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Columbia University.
   - **Details**: Scholarly overview of traditional oral transmission, tribal genealogists, and historiography in Iranian and Pashtun cultures.
   - **Claim Supported**: Transmission of genealogical traditions, oral narratives, and collective memory.
