# Sources: Page 77 — The Durand Line

This document records the historical, diplomatic, and academic sources supporting **Page 77: The Durand Line** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Diplomatic Treaties

1. **Anglo-Afghan Agreement (1893)**. 
   - **Details**: Signed on November 12, 1893, in Kabul by Sir Henry Mortimer Durand and Amir Abdur Rahman Khan.
   - **Claim Supported**: Establishment of the frontier and spheres of influence between British India and Afghanistan.
   - **Source Type**: Primary Treaty.

2. **Treaty of Peace between the British and Afghan Governments (1919)**. 
   - **Details**: Also known as the Treaty of Rawalpindi, signed on August 8, 1919.
   - **Article 5**: Reaffirmed the frontier accepted by the late Amir (Abdur Rahman).
   - **Source Type**: Primary Treaty.

3. **Anglo-Afghan Treaty of Friendship (1921)**.
   - **Details**: Signed on November 22, 1921, in Kabul.
   - **Article 2**: Mutually accepted the Indo-Afghan frontier as defined in the 1919 treaty.
   - **Source Type**: Primary Treaty.

4. **Durand Commission Reports (1894–1896)**.
   - **Details**: Records of the joint survey and demarcation commissions, including the placement of 258 boundary markers.
   - **Claim Supported**: Physical demarcation of the line on the ground.
   - **Source Type**: Administrative Record.

## Academic & Ethnographic Sources

5. **Ahmed, Akbar S.** (1980). *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*. Routledge.
   - **Details**: Ethnographic study by a former Political Agent of Orakzai, documenting tribal kinship and cross-border dynamics.
   - **Claim Supported**: Smuggling, kinship interplay, and the Orakzai tribe's relationship with the frontier.

6. **Sykes, Percy.** (1940). *A History of Afghanistan*. Macmillan.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive historical account containing the full text of the 1893 agreement in Appendix C.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical context and text of the 1893 negotiations.

7. **Khan, I.** (2025). "Borderland struggles: the consequences of the Afghan-Pakistan border fencing." *The Round Table*.
   - **Details**: Recent analysis of the impact of the 2017 border fencing on tribal communities.
   - **Claim Supported**: Contemporary border management and its socio-economic impact.
