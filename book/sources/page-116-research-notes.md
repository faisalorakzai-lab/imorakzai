# Research Notes: Page 116 — From Local Markets to Global Markets

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 116: From Local Markets to Global Markets**.

## 1. Market Evolution Framework
- **Local Market:** Characterized by physical proximity, interpersonal trust, and community reputation (e.g., village bazaars in Orakzai).
- **Regional/National Market:** Expanded through urbanization, improved logistics, and provincial trade networks (e.g., Peshawar, Karachi).
- **Diaspora Market:** Transnational networks connecting the homeland with overseas communities (UK, Gulf, etc.).
- **Global Market:** International trade governed by quality standards, international law, digital facilitation, and global competition.

## 2. Current Trade Context (2025–2026)
- **National Tariff Policy (2025–30):** Focuses on export-led growth, rationalizing tariffs, and reducing anti-export bias.
- **Pakistan Single Window (PSW):** An integrated digital platform for trade facilitation, mandated by SBP for export declarations, aiming to reduce fragmentation in trade processes.
- **IT Exports Milestone:** Pakistan's IT and ITeS exports reached a record **US$4.6 billion** in FY 2025–26, driven by software services and freelancing.
- **E-Commerce Policy:** The Second National E-Commerce Policy (2026 draft) aims to improve global market access, digital payments, and cross-border logistics.

## 3. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — Building Beyond Borders
- **Profile:** Technology entrepreneur oriented toward international digital markets.
- **Pathway:** Leverages computer science and software systems to build digital products (e.g., Orakzai Group, OkzByte Hub) for a global audience.
- **Significance:** Illustrates the "Global Mindset" where technical curiosity and Orakzai identity are combined to solve global problems.
- **Verification:** Case study is labeled as an individual pathway, not representative of all Orakzai entrepreneurs.

## 4. Strategic Framework for Global Entry
- **Process:** Research → Pilot → Compliance → Branding → Scale.
- **Barriers:** Logistics, currency volatility, regulatory standards (SPS/TBT), and cultural localization.
- **Enablers:** Technology (AI, e-commerce platforms), diaspora networks, and digital trade facilitation (PSW).

## 5. Evidence Boundaries
- **Orakzai Context:** Local market descriptions are connected to Page 50; trade routes are symbolic unless documented.
- **No Promotion:** Avoids claiming global leadership; focuses on documented initiatives and educational frameworks.
- **Statistical Discipline:** Distinguishes between current strengths (textiles, IT) and future opportunities (AI, specialized manufacturing).
