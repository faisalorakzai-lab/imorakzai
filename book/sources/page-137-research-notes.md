# Research Notes: Page 137 — Real Estate Tokenization

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 137: Real Estate Tokenization**.

## 1. Global Market Context (2026)
- **Market Valuation:** The global real estate tokenization market is valued at approximately **$4.81 billion in 2026**, projected to reach over **$24 billion by 2035** (Precedence Research).
- **Growth Drivers:** Increasing demand for fractional ownership, faster settlement, and improved transparency in traditionally illiquid markets.
- **Institutional Shift:** Moving from conceptual pilots to regulated platforms that connect physical property rights with programmable digital infrastructure.

## 2. Technical & Legal Framework
- **Tokenization Stack:** Requires a multi-layered approach: Legal Structure (SPVs), Custody/Registry, Verification (Title/Liens), Token Layer (Smart Contracts), and Compliance (KYC/AML).
- **Direct vs. Indirect Ownership:** Indirect ownership via Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) is the dominant model for maintaining legal compliance while enabling fractional digital interests.
- **The "Title Gap":** A blockchain record is not an automatic land title. Legal recognition by government land registries is the critical bridge for digital sovereignty.

## 3. Pakistan's PropTech Landscape (2026)
- **PropTech Convention 2026:** Industry leaders in Pakistan are advocating for blockchain-enabled land records and e-governance to build digital trust.
- **PIDE Research:** The Pakistan Institute of Development Economics (PIDE) published an agent-based simulation study (2025/2026) on transforming Pakistan's real estate through blockchain, focusing on market efficiency and inclusion.
- **Active Players:** Platforms like **DAO PropTech** and **PDAV PropTech** are simplifying property investment through secure digital ownership units.
- **Regional Application:** Potential for tokenization to bring transparency to land records in districts like Orakzai, bridging traditional ownership with modern digital registries.

## 4. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI & Blockchain Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** Approaching real estate not just as land, but as a "system" where legal rights, physical assets, and digital infrastructure intersect.
