# Research Notes: Page 125 — AI & Healthcare

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 125: AI & Healthcare**.

## 1. Institutional Landscape (Pakistan 2026)
- **NCAI Medical Imaging & Diagnostics Lab (MIDL):** Based at COMSATS University Islamabad, this lab is a cornerstone of medical AI research in Pakistan.
- **Dx2D Tool:** A significant 2026 development from MIDL—an AI-powered tool designed for early cancer detection and diagnosis from mammograms, X-rays, and CT scans.
- **National Digital Health Framework 2022–2030:** Pakistan's strategic roadmap for integrating technology into healthcare, emphasizing E-Health, data interoperability, and AI-assisted diagnostics.
- **Prime Minister's Health Card Initiative:** As of 2026, there are plans to deploy AI-powered diagnostic systems across 1,110 public and private hospitals to improve triage and early detection.

## 2. Economic & Startup Context
- **HealthTech Funding:** 2025/2026 data shows a surge in funding for AI-driven health startups, with AI companies capturing over 55% of total health tech investment.
- **Startup Map:** Pakistan's 2025/2026 healthcare startup ecosystem includes over 70 ventures, with major players like Sehat Kahani leading in telemedicine and AI-assisted virtual care.
- **Indus AI 2026:** A national convening that showcased Pakistani AI solutions for inclusive and cost-effective healthcare.

## 3. Technical & Ethical Frameworks
- **Workflow Principle:** The "Clinician-in-the-loop" model: Medical Image → AI Analysis → Possible Finding → Clinician Review → Clinical Decision.
- **Data Sovereignty:** Emphasis on validating AI models for Pakistani populations to avoid demographic bias inherent in global datasets.
- **Privacy & Security:** Focus on Electronic Health Records (EHR) and the need for authorized, secure access to sensitive patient data.

## 4. Language & Regional Healthcare
- **Pashto Healthcare AI:** Concept of reducing language barriers in remote regions like Orakzai through Pashto-specific speech recognition and medical translation tools.
- **Rural Access:** Framing AI as a support tool for community health workers in remote districts, providing pre-screening and medical information retrieval where specialist access is limited.

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** His documented interests in digital infrastructure and software align with the "Builder's Model" of creating integrated, secure, and scalable digital health systems.
- **Philosophy:** "The machine can process the data; the human must remain responsible for the care."
