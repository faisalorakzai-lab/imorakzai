# Sources: Page 20 — Jirga — Traditional Governance

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 20: Jirga — Traditional Governance** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **M. Jamil Hanifi (Encyclopaedia Iranica)**: *JERGA*.
   - **Author**: M. Jamil Hanifi.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Vol. XIV, Fasc. 6, pp. 630–632, 2008 (updated 2012).
   - **Publisher**: Encyclopædia Iranica Foundation, New York.
   - **Details**: Defines the jirga as an assembly or council of local adult men sitting in a circular formation for conflict resolution and community deliberation. Notes that historical claims characterizing the jirga as a formal judicial, legislative, and executive government are not fully supported by historical records.
   - **Claim Supported**: Definition, non-permanent assembly nature, and deliberative mechanics of the jirga.

2. **Akbar S. Ahmed**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*.
   - **Author**: Akbar S. Ahmed.
   - **Publication**: Routledge, 1980.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive ethnographic analysis of tribal structure, leadership, and customary governance in Pashtun society.
   - **Claim Supported**: Tribal organization, leadership roles, and customary mediation.

3. **Fredrik Barth**: *Political Leadership Among Swat Pathans*.
   - **Author**: Fredrik Barth.
   - **Publication**: London: Athlone Press, 1959.
   - **Details**: Classic anthropological study examining political organization, factionalism, and councils in North-West Frontier communities.
   - **Claim Supported**: Political deliberation and social dynamics in tribal regions.

4. **L. White King**: *Monograph on the Orakzai Country and Clans*.
   - **Author**: Sir Lucas White King.
   - **Publication**: Punjab Government Press, Lahore, 1900.
   - **Details**: Historical monograph detailing Orakzai tribal structure, clans, geography, and customary administration.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical Orakzai regional context and tribal organization.
