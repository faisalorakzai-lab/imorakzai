## Evidence Boundaries

- **Proposed Archive:** The "Orakzai Digital Heritage Archive" is a **PROPOSED ARCHIVAL MODEL**, not an existing institution.
- **AI Ethics:** AI-generated reconstructions are clearly labeled and distinguished from original historical evidence.
- **Privacy Boundary:** Sensitive personal, financial, and children's records are excluded from public digital heritage collections.
- **No Fabrication:** Digital heritage tools are used to preserve existing evidence, not to manufacture missing historical records.
- **Illustrative Data:** All file counts and scoping statistics are illustrative project-scoping examples.
