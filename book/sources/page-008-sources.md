### Factual Integrity and Methodology

The discussion of the Orakzai diaspora in major urban centers like Karachi and international hubs such as Dubai and London is grounded in documented migration patterns. These trends are observed in ethnographic scholarship and economic reports from the *Pakistan Bureau of Statistics* and the *World Bank*, which track the movement of Pashtun communities from rural tribal regions to urban and overseas environments.

Furthermore, the distinction between oral tradition and historical evidence follows established historical methodology. This includes the principles of **provenance**, **chronology**, and **corroboration**, ensuring that personal and community memories are respected while maintaining a commitment to verifiable historical truth.
