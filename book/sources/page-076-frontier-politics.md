# Sources: Page 76 — Frontier Politics

This document records the historical and academic sources supporting **Page 76: Frontier Politics** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Ahmed, Akbar S.** (1981). "The Arab Connection: Emergent Models of Social Structure and Organization among Pakistani Tribesmen". *Asian Affairs*.
   - **Details**: Academic study by a former Political Agent of Orakzai Agency, providing ethnographic and political analysis of the district's social structure.
   - **Claim Supported**: Concentrated power of the Political Agent and the role of lineage in Orakzai politics.

2. **Alam, J.** (2021). "Jirga System and Its Role in Peacebuilding and Conflict Resolution". *JSTOR*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of the historical use of Maliks and Jirgas by the British to build infrastructure and manage the frontier.
   - **Claim Supported**: The Jirga as a channel for negotiation and mediation between the tribe and the state.

3. **Hayat, M.** (2009). "Still 'taming the turbulent frontier'? The state in the Federally Administered Tribal Areas of Pakistan". *Journal of the Anthropological Society of Oxford*.
   - **Details**: Examination of the position of tribal maliks and the 1973 demarcation of the Orakzai Agency.
   - **Claim Supported**: Creation of Orakzai Agency in 1973 and the state's use of traditional institutions for administration.

4. **Shah, Mian Abid.** (2018). *Political reforms, local elites and power: A study of the federally administered tribal areas (FATA) of Pakistan*. PhD Thesis, University of Bath.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive study of the governance role of Maliks and the evolution of political significance in FATA.
   - **Claim Supported**: The shifting significance of Maliks as the administrative system evolved.

5. **Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.** (2024). *District Orakzai: Historical and Administrative Profile*.
   - **Details**: Official administrative records documenting the formal inauguration of the agency in 1973.
   - **Claim Supported**: Date of establishment and formal inauguration of Orakzai Agency.

## Documented Terminology

| Term | Context | Historical Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Political Agent (PA)** | Administration | The chief executive and judicial official in a tribal agency. |
| **Malik** | Representation | A government-recognized tribal intermediary who receives an allowance. |
| **Muajib** | Economy | A hereditary frontier allowance paid to tribal elders/maliks. |
| **Chatti** | Legal | A collective fine imposed on a tribal section for a breach of agreement. |
| **Lungi** | Patronage | A special allowance or status granted to prominent tribal elders. |
