# Research Notes: Page 133 — Bitcoin & Digital Money

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 133: Bitcoin & Digital Money**.

## 1. Institutional & Legal Landscape (Pakistan 2026)
- **Virtual Assets Act 2026:** Formally passed by the Parliament of Pakistan in March 2026. It provides a comprehensive legal framework for digital assets, ending years of regulatory uncertainty.
- **Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA):** Established as the primary regulator. It is responsible for licensing exchanges, digital wallets, and virtual asset service providers (VASPs).
- **Penalties for Non-Compliance:** Unlicensed operations face criminal penalties, including fines of up to PKR 50 million and imprisonment.
- **Bitcoin Mining Initiative:** The government has allocated **2,000 MW of electricity** for Bitcoin mining and AI data centers, framing mining as a tool for industrialization and energy grid balancing.

## 2. Global & National Adoption
- **Market Dynamics:** In 2026, Bitcoin's global market capitalization remains between $1 trillion and $2 trillion.
- **Pakistan's Global Rank:** Pakistan has climbed to **3rd place globally** in crypto adoption, reflecting high retail and emerging institutional interest.
- **Institutional Shift:** Over 60% of surveyed institutional investors in 2026 express significant interest in integrating tokenized assets and digital financial infrastructure.

## 3. Core Bitcoin Concepts
- **Digital Scarcity:** Solving the double-spending problem through a distributed ledger (blockchain) and Proof of Work consensus.
- **Satoshi Nakamoto:** The 2008 whitepaper and the 2009 genesis block as the starting point for decentralized digital money.
- **Monetary Schedule:** Predictable issuance through block production and halving events, contrasting with discretionary fiat monetary policy.
- **Unit of Account:** While Bitcoin serves as a store of value and medium of exchange, its role as a unit of account remains an area of ongoing transition.

## 4. Orakzai & Regional Financial Access
- **Remittances:** Bitcoin and digital money are analyzed as tools for lowering the cost of international remittances for the Orakzai diaspora.
- **Financial Inclusion:** Digital wallets provide access to global financial markets for residents in remote districts like Orakzai, bypassing traditional banking barriers.

## 5. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI & Blockchain Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation.
- **Focus:** His documented interests in software architecture, blockchain systems, and digital infrastructure align with the "Secure & Decentralized" philosophy of 2026.
- **Philosophy:** "The private key is the ultimate proof of digital ownership."
