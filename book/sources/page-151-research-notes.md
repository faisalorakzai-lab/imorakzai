# Research Notes: Page 151 — Pakistan’s Economic Future

## Overview
Page 151 explores the transformation of Pakistan's economy, focusing on the shift from a consumption-based traditional model to a digital, productive, and global one. It highlights 2026 milestones in IT exports, manufacturing growth, and national modernization policies.

## Key Findings
1. **The IT Export Surge**: In FY26, Pakistan achieved a record **$4.6 billion** in IT and telecom exports, marking a 21% surge. This growth is a core pillar of the country's strategy to bridge the balance-of-payments gap through services exports.
2. **Industrial Modernization**: The **Mobile & Electronics Policy 2026–33** serves as a strategic blueprint for high-value manufacturing. This policy, combined with a **5.77% growth in LSM**, indicates a shift toward value-added production.
3. **The 2026 Pivot**: Designated as the **'Year of Reform and Modernization'**, 2026 marks a systemic overhaul of governance and economic structures aimed at sustainable productivity.
4. **Digital Economy Potential**: Strategic projects like the **Digital Economy Enhancement Project (DEEP)** aim to add **$60 billion** to the national GDP by 2030, leveraging Pakistan's young, tech-savvy population.
5. **Productivity over Consumption**: The economic model is pivoting toward a sustainable productive base, moving from raw material exports to branded, global products through value addition.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Productivity Model**: The page uses the "Consumption → Productivity → Production → Value Addition → Exports" model to frame the economic transition.
- **Human Capital Focus**: Emphasis is placed on digital skills (AI, Software, Cybersecurity) as the primary driver for high-value employment and services exports.
- **Regional Inclusion**: The Orakzai context is used to show how digital platforms enable local businesses and the diaspora to participate in the global economy, removing geographic constraints.

## Source Verification
- IT export figures ($4.6B) and LSM growth (5.77%) are verified against July 2026 reports from Dawn and PBS.
- Policy milestones (Mobile & Electronics Policy 2026-33) are sourced from official MOITT and Ministry of Industries releases.
- Projections for 2030 ($60B add) are based on Planning Commission and Ignite strategic reports.
