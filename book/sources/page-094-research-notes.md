# Research Notes: Page 94 — Orakzai in Islamabad

## Core Concept
- **The National Capital**: Islamabad represents the planned center of the Pakistani state, attracting Orakzai individuals for reasons distinct from the regional hubs of Peshawar or Kohat.
- **Identity Synthesis**: Life in the capital often involves a synthesis of Orakzai tribal identity with a modern, national Pakistani identity.
- **Evidence Distinction**: High confidence in Islamabad's urban growth data; limited but verified evidence for specific Orakzai professional and student presence.

## Islamabad as an Urban Center
- **Capital Role**: National administrative, federal government, diplomatic, and research hub.
- **Urban Expansion**:
  - **1979–2019**: Built-up area expanded by **41.7%** (Shah et al., 2021).
  - **1990–2021**: Built-up increased from **5.7% (52 km²)** to **25.7% (233 km²)** (Khan et al., 2023).
- **Metropolitan Network**: Strong link between Islamabad and Rawalpindi for housing, transport, and commerce.

## Orakzai Presence in Islamabad
- **Professional Class**: Orakzai individuals are documented in civil service, human rights (e.g., Malik Habib Orakzai), and professional sectors.
- **Education**: Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU), NUST, and IIUI are key destinations for Orakzai students.
- **Migration Drivers**:
  - **Government Posting**: Federal employees and military personnel.
  - **Education**: Access to top-tier national universities.
  - **Professional Career**: Medicine, engineering, and technology.
  - **Business**: Real estate and services.

## Identity and Adaptation
- **National Citizenship**: Navigating the formal, planned environment of the capital while maintaining Pashto language and tribal memory.
- **Social Networks**: Tribal connections often facilitate initial settlement and professional networking in the city.
- **Multi-local Life**: Maintaining property and family ties in the Orakzai homeland while building a career in Islamabad.

## Research Gaps
- **Population Statistics**: No official census breakdown for Orakzai sub-tribes within the Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT).
- **Neighborhood Concentration**: Unlike Karachi, Orakzai presence in Islamabad is more dispersed across sectors and housing societies, making specific "Orakzai sectors" non-existent.
