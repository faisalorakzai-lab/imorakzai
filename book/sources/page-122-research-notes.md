# Research Notes: Page 122 — AI & Human Civilization

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 122: AI & Human Civilization**.

## 1. Civilizational Impact
- **Knowledge Production:** AI is transitioning from a tool for calculation to a participant in knowledge synthesis. The emerging model is "Human → AI → Information + Synthesis → Human," which multiplies cognitive power but introduces risks of overreliance and "hallucination."
- **Labor Markets:** 2026 reports (Stanford AI Index) show a positive expert outlook on AI's impact on work (73%), though public perception remains cautious. Impact is uneven, affecting younger workers and entry-level roles more significantly.
- **Education:** The shift is from mass curricula to adaptive, personalized learning pathways (pace, language, difficulty).

## 2. Culture & Language (Pashto Focus)
- **Language Technology:** 2026 saw the emergence of 'Qehwa AI' and 'Katib', developed by Peshawar-based students, providing Pashto-specific LLM and speech-to-text capabilities.
- **Datasets:** The release of the Pashto Language Dataset of Speech and Text (PLDST/PashtoCoST) in early 2026 marks a milestone in addressing the "low-resource" status of Pashto in NLP.
- **Preservation:** Academic models in Pakistan are proposing AI-driven frameworks for folk music and religious heritage preservation, essential for tribal regions like Orakzai.

## 3. Pakistan's Economic Context (FY 2025–26)
- **Exports:** IT and IT-enabled services reached $3.39 billion in the first nine months of FY 2026.
- **Freelance Economy:** Pakistani freelancers earned a record $1.76 billion in FY 2026, illustrating the democratization of digital work.
- **Constraints:** Compute access, electricity reliability, and "brain drain" remain significant structural barriers to full AI adoption.

## 4. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — Civilizational View
- **Perspective:** Faisal Orakzai views technology as a means to change the scale of individual creation and coordination.
- **Orakzai Opportunity:** For remote communities, AI offers a tool to preserve oral knowledge and participate in the global digital economy without losing local identity.
- **Philosophy:** "To become more technologically capable without becoming less human."

## 5. Ethics & Governance
- **UNESCO Standards:** Following the 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of AI and 2026 guidelines on intangible cultural heritage.
- **Trust Stack:** Trust in the AI era is built on Capability + Transparency + Verification + Accountability.
- **Automation Bias:** The risk of humans assuming automated outputs are inherently correct, leading to real-world errors.
