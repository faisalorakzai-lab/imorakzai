# Sources: Page 43 — Mountains of Orakzai

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 43: Mountains of Orakzai** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **King, Lucas White**: *Monograph on the Orakzai Country and Clans*.
   - **Publication**: Punjab Government Press, 1900.
   - **Details**: Provides foundational descriptions of the mountain systems, ridges, and passes (including the Lozaka Pass) in the Orakzai heartland.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical significance of mountains and passes; descriptions of Karagh Ghar and Tirah.

2. **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Board of Investment and Trade (KP-BOIT)**: *Orakzai District Profile*.
   - **URL**: https://kpboit.gov.pk/orakzai-district/
   - **Details**: Modern geographic data confirming elevation ranges and important peaks.
   - **Claim Supported**: Elevation varies from over 10,000 ft (west) to less than 6,650 ft (east); peaks like Sangla and Chara Kandao.

3. **Pakistan Almanac**: *Khyber Pakhtunkhwa - Orakzai*.
   - **URL**: https://pakistanalmanac.com/kp-orakzai/
   - **Details**: Geographic overview identifying Sangla and Chara Kandao as important peaks.
   - **Claim Supported**: Sangla (1,928 m) and Chara Kandao (1,720 m) elevations.

4. **ResearchGate / Academic Papers**:
   - **Study**: *Impact Of Population Growth On Forest Cover In District Orakzai Between 1985 To 2020* (2025).
   - **Details**: Discusses the dominance of the Karagh Ghar Range and the importance of the Lozaka Pass.
   - **Claim Supported**: Dominance of Karagh Ghar; Lozaka Pass as a critical route.

5. **Historical Survey Records**: *Gazetteer of the Kohat District*.
   - **Details**: Records the Samana Range (6,000–7,000 ft) as the southern boundary of the Tirah region.
   - **Claim Supported**: Samana Range elevation and regional boundary role.

## Verified Mountain Data

- **Elevation Ranges**:
    - **Western Hills**: Over 10,000 ft (3,000 m).
    - **Eastern Hills**: Less than 6,650 ft (2,030 m).
    - **Plains/Lower Areas**: 5,200–5,500 ft (1,700 m).
- **Major Peaks**:
    - **Sangla**: 1,928 m (approx. 6,325 ft).
    - **Chara Kandao**: 1,720 m (approx. 5,643 ft).
- **Mountain Ranges**:
    - **Karagh Ghar (Kahu Ghar)**: A spur of the Safed Koh, central to Orakzai geography.
    - **Samana Range**: Forms the southern boundary, separating Orakzai from Hangu.
    - **Safed Koh (Spin Ghar)**: The broader regional system to which Orakzai spurs belong.
- **Passes**:
    - **Lozaka Pass**: Crosses the Karagh Ghar, connecting Shaonkanri and Maidan, leading into the Kurram valley.
