# Sources: Page 91 — Orakzai in Modern Pakistan

This document records the primary and academic sources supporting **Page 91: Orakzai in Modern Pakistan** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary Official & Constitutional Sources

1. **Constitution of Pakistan.** (1973). *25th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2018*.
   - **Details**: The legal instrument that merged FATA with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and repealed Article 247.
   - **Claim Supported**: Constitutional merger and transition to provincial district status.

2. **District Courts Orakzai.** (2019). *Official Establishment Records*.
   - **Details**: Confirms the establishment of courts in February 2019 at Baber Mela, with the first judge posted on February 25, 2019.
   - **Claim Supported**: Judicial integration and operational timeline of formal courts.

3. **Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS).** (2023). *7th Population and Housing Census (Digital Census)*.
   - **Details**: Population: 387,561; Male: 204,870; Female: 182,684; Urban: 0; Rural: 387,561; HH Size: 7.4.
   - **Claim Supported**: Latest demographic and census data for Orakzai District.

4. **Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP).** (2023). *Final Delimitation of Constituencies for General Elections 2024*.
   - **Details**: National Assembly: NA-36 Hangu-cum-Orakzai; Provincial Assembly: PK-94 Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Political representation and electoral framework.

## Academic & Research Sources

5. **Khan, M. I., Noorani, G. M., & Khan, H. U.** (2025). *Merger of Federally Administered Tribal Areas: Mapping the implementation of administrative and judicial reforms*. *International Journal of Social Sciences*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of the administrative and judicial transition in Orakzai and other merged districts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Post-merger administrative reforms and challenges.

6. **Ali, G.** (2020). *FATA Merger to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Problems and Prospects for Federalism in Pakistan*. *Global Political Review*.
   - **Details**: Discussion on the 25th Amendment and the mainstreaming of the tribal frontier.
   - **Claim Supported**: Political and federal implications of the merger.

7. **Bukhari, A. Q.** (2026). *Layered Continuity in Pakistan's Northwest Borderlands*.
   - **Details**: Analysis of the persistence of tribal identity alongside state integration.
   - **Claim Supported**: The concept of modern identity as a synthesis of tradition and statehood.

## Research Gaps & Limitations
- **Local Government Functionality**: While the framework exists, the actual level of service delivery and funding at the village council level remains variable and less documented in official central reports.
- **Urbanization Classification**: The census "Urban = 0" classification is a legal/municipal designation and does not capture the functional urbanization of towns like Kalaya.
