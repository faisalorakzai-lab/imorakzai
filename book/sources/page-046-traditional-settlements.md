# Sources: Page 46 — Traditional Settlements

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 46: Traditional Settlements** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **King, Lucas White**: *Monograph on the Orakzai Country and Clans*.
   - **Publication**: Punjab Government Press, 1900.
   - **Details**: Foundational descriptions of Orakzai tribal lands, village organization, and the relationship between geography and settlement.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical significance of settlement patterns; relationship between terrain and community location.

2. **UN-Habitat**: *Rapid Technical Assessment (Tirah Assessment)*.
   - **URL**: https://unhabitat.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Tieerah-Assesment.pdf
   - **Details**: Statistical data on housing materials in the Tirah region, identifying 49% stone and mud construction.
   - **Claim Supported**: Use of stone and mud as dominant vernacular materials; housing statistics.

3. **Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa**: *Draft Land Use Plan of District Orakzai*.
   - **URL**: https://urbanpolicyunit.gkp.pk/
   - **Details**: Modern administrative records documenting settlement distribution and the presence of boundary walls (99%) in institutional buildings, reflecting regional architectural norms.
   - **Claim Supported**: Settlement density variations; importance of boundary walls/privacy.

4. **Scientific Reports (2026)**: *Tribal traditions and conservation perspectives of wild plants in Orakzai District, Pakistan*.
   - **Details**: Ecological context identifying Orakzai as a transitional zone, which influences local building adaptation to climate.
   - **Claim Supported**: Environmental system context (Mountain → Climate → Valley → Life).

5. **eHRAF World Cultures**: *Pashtun Ethnographic Summaries*.
   - **Details**: Anthropological data on Pashtun settlement types (clustered vs. dispersed) and the social role of the Hujra and Mosque.
   - **Claim Supported**: Clustered settlement drivers (kinship, shared resources); social-spatial roles of communal spaces.

## Verified Architectural Data

- **Materials**: 
    - Walls: Stone masonry and mud (49%).
    - Modern: Brick masonry (45%).
    - Structure: Timber used for doors and ceilings.
- **Settlement Types**:
    - **Clustered**: Common in higher elevations for protection and kinship bonds.
    - **Dispersed**: Occurs on wider slopes or lower basins where land allows.
- **The Compound**: A walled unit organizing living space, storage, and livestock; central courtyard for privacy and ventilation.
- **Communal Spaces**:
    - **Hujra**: Male guest house and social center.
    - **Mosque**: Spiritual and community hub.
- **Geographic Logic**: Proximity to water (Toi) and arable land is the primary determinant of site selection.
