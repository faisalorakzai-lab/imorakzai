# Sources: Page 60 — Traditional Food

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 60: Traditional Food** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **FAO in Pakistan**: *Maize seeds provided to farmers in Orakzai agency*. (2018).
   - **Details**: Official report on the distribution of certified maize seed packages to 3,750 farmer families in Orakzai Agency of FATA.
   - **Claim Supported**: Maize as a primary crop and staple in Orakzai; FAO support for food security.

2. **Historical Records of the North-West Frontier**: *Ethnographic accounts of Orakzai Agency*.
   - **Details**: British-era and early post-independence records identifying maize, wheat bread, and meat as the staple foods of the Orakzai population.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai staple foods (maize, wheat, meat).

3. **Tapper, R. & Tapper, N.**: *“Eat this, it’ll do you a power of good”: food and commensality among Durrani Pashtuns*. (1986).
   - **Details**: Anthropological study on the social meaning of food and shared eating in Pashtun society.
   - **Claim Supported**: Pashtun-wide principles of commensality and food-based hospitality.

4. **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Agriculture Department**: *District Agriculture Profile: Orakzai*. (2025).
   - **Details**: Documentation of current agricultural production in Orakzai, including wheat, maize, vegetables, and fruits.
   - **Claim Supported**: Current agricultural diversity and crop types in Orakzai.

5. **Ahmed, A. S.**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society*. (1980).
   - **Details**: Classic ethnography discussing the relationship between land, livestock, and food in tribal structures.
   - **Claim Supported**: Tribal food systems and the role of hospitality.
