# Sources: Page 56 — Orakzai Weddings

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 56: Orakzai Weddings** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Ahmed, A. S.**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*. (1980).
   - **Details**: Classic ethnography discussing the social significance of marriage in tribal structure, the role of elders, and the distinction between Islamic law and Pashtun custom.
   - **Claim Supported**: Tribal and family involvement in marriage negotiations; social role of weddings.

2. **Asghar, W.**: *FATA Merger: Impacts on Status of Tribal Women*. (2024). *Journal of Development and Social Sciences (JDSS)*.
   - **Details**: Academic study analyzing the changing status of women in former FATA, including Orakzai, and the economic aspects of marriage (Mahr vs. bride price).
   - **Claim Supported**: Economic aspects of marriage; impact of modernization and legal changes.

3. **Jany, J.**: *Legal Traditions in Asia: History, Concepts and Laws*. (2020). *Springer*.
   - **Details**: Chapter on "Concurrent Customary Laws" discussing how Islamic law and Pashtun customs merge, particularly regarding Mahr and consent.
   - **Claim Supported**: Islamic foundation of marriage (Nikah, Mahr) and its intersection with Pashtunwali.

4. **Shinwari, N. A.**: *Political and social change in Pakistan's tribal areas: Understanding Pashtun youth*. (2025).
   - **Details**: Analysis of how younger generations in former FATA are adapting traditional practices, including weddings, to urban and digital contexts.
   - **Claim Supported**: Generational changes; urban and digital wedding adaptations.

5. **Historical Records of the North-West Frontier**: *Ethnographic accounts of Tirah tribes*.
   - **Details**: British-era records describing the ceremonies, music, and communal celebrations of the Orakzai and Afridi tribes.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical wedding ceremonies; role of music and Hujra.
