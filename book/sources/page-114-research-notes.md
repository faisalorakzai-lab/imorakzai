# Research Notes: Page 114 — Young Pakistan & Technology

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 114: Young Pakistan & Technology**.

## 1. Demographic Landscape (2025–2026)
- **Total Population:** 252.09 million (2025).
- **Youth Cohort (15–29 years):** 26.56% of the population.
- **Working-Age Population (15–64 years):** 56.9%.
- **Source:** Pakistan Economic Survey 2025–26.
- **Implication:** Pakistan possesses a significant "youth bulge" that represents a major potential driver for the digital economy if supported by education and opportunity.

## 2. Digital Access and Usage (2024–2025)
- **Internet Usage (Population):** 57%.
- **Household Internet Usage:** 70%.
- **Mobile/Smartphone Ownership:** 50%.
- **Source:** Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) HIES 2024–25.
- **Milestone:** 72,000 laptops distributed under the Prime Minister's Youth Laptop Scheme (July–March FY2026).

## 3. The Freelance Economy (FY2026)
- **Tech Freelancer Exports:** US$856.3 million (July–March FY2026).
- **Growth Rate:** 51% increase compared to the same period in the previous year.
- **Context:** Freelancing allows young professionals to participate in global markets from within Pakistan, provided they have skills and connectivity.

## 4. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai
- **Profile:** Technology entrepreneur, computer scientist, and systems architect.
- **Role:** Founder & Chairman of Orakzai Group and OkzByte Hub.
- **Focus Areas:** Blockchain infrastructure, artificial intelligence, fintech, and digital systems.
- **Significance:** Faisal's journey illustrates the transition from a learner/user to a builder/founder, connecting Orakzai identity with global technology.
- **Source:** LinkedIn, Crunchbase, CryptoSlate, and ResearchGate profiles (verified 2026).

## 5. Technology Pathways for Youth
- **Coding:** Languages like Python, JavaScript, and systems-oriented programming.
- **AI:** Generative AI, machine learning, and AI-assisted software development.
- **Digital Education:** e-Rozgaar centers (target 250), online libraries, and open-source learning.
- **Cultural Preservation:** Youth-led initiatives to digitize Pashto oral histories and tribal genealogies.

## 6. Evidence Boundaries
- **Framing:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as *one example* of a youth technology journey, not as a representative of the entire generation.
- **Statistical Discipline:** National statistics are clearly distinguished from individual or regional claims.
- **No Exaggeration:** Avoids promotional language; focuses on documented achievements and measurable economic contributions.
