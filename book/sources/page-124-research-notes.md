# Research Notes: Page 124 — AI & Education

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 124: AI & Education**.

## 1. National Context (Pakistan 2026)
- **HEC Mandate:** The Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan has mandated a compulsory three-credit-hour course on Artificial Intelligence (AI) for all undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs, starting from the Fall 2026 semester.
- **Digital Workforce Initiative:** The Ministry of IT and Telecommunication (MoITT) and the Ministry of Federal Education (MoFEPT) are launching 20,000 AI training programs (6–12 month certifications) to build a future-ready workforce.
- **KP Virtual School:** In April 2026, the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) government approved the establishment of the first public-sector virtual school system and the integration of AI into the provincial curriculum.
- **Digital Divide:** Research highlights that while AI offers personalization, it risks increasing inequality if access to devices, electricity, and high-speed internet is not addressed (31 educational institutions in ICT targeted for initial pilots).

## 2. Global Standards & Frameworks
- **UNESCO Guidance:** Following the "Guidance for generative AI in education and research" (2023/2026 update), which emphasizes ethical use, data privacy, and the central role of human teachers.
- **AI Literacy:** Frameworks are shifting from "prompt engineering" to "computational thinking," covering algorithms, data ethics, and verification of AI-generated content (hallucination awareness).

## 3. Language & Regional Education
- **Pashto NLP:** The emergence of 'Qehwa AI' and 'Katib' (developed by Peshawar students) provides a technical foundation for Pashto-language educational tutoring and translation.
- **Orakzai Connection:** AI is framed as a "Heritage Bridge," potentially digitizing oral traditions and providing a digital interface for tribal history archives, as discussed in Pages 103–110.

## 4. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — The AI Generation
- **Representation:** Faisal Orakzai is presented as a representative of the young Pakistani technological generation entering adulthood during the AI transition.
- **Focus:** His documented interests in software, digital infrastructure, and blockchain illustrate the "Young Pakistani Builder" model, where individual ambition meets global technology access.

## 5. Pedagogical Shift
- **Memorization to Understanding:** The shift from "Can you remember this?" to "Can you use this knowledge?" is a central theme.
- **Assessment Evolution:** Moving toward project-based learning, oral exams, and practical demonstrations to address the challenge of AI-generated assignments.
