# Research Notes: Page 120 — The Future of Computing

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 120: The Future of Computing**.

## 1. Computing Evolution & Architectures
- **Moore's Law:** Transitioning from simple transistor scaling to architectural innovation, 3D packaging, and chiplets.
- **Specialized Compute:** Rise of NPUs (Neural Processing Units), TPUs (Tensor Processing Units), and custom ASICs for AI training and inference.
- **Neuromorphic & Photonic:** Brain-inspired architectures and light-based computing are in active research phases to address energy and bandwidth constraints.

## 2. Artificial Intelligence & Agents
- **Generative AI:** Moving from large language models to multimodal systems (text, image, audio, video).
- **Agentic Systems:** Transition from passive tools to autonomous agents that can perceive, plan, and act.
- **Edge AI:** On-device processing for privacy, latency, and bandwidth efficiency.

## 3. Quantum Computing & Security
- **Current State:** Moving from experimental labs to early business impact; focus on quantum error correction and scaling qubits.
- **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC):** NIST finalized standards (FIPS 203, 204, 205) in 2024; 2026 is a pivotal year for enterprise adoption of quantum-safe encryption.

## 4. Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
- **Evolution:** From GUI (Keyboard/Mouse) to NUI (Touch/Voice/Gesture) and toward Spatial Computing (AR/VR) and Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI).
- **Multimodal Interaction:** Systems processing multiple inputs simultaneously to provide contextual assistance.

## 5. Pakistan Context (2025–2026)
- **NCAI (National Center of AI):** Operates labs in 6 universities; developed 220+ AI products for healthcare, agriculture, and education.
- **Education:** Launch of AI Research Apprenticeship Programs (2026) for youth at NUST and other institutions.
- **Robotics:** Research active in university labs focusing on industrial automation and assistive technologies.

## 6. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — Future Computing
- **Perspective:** Thinking beyond applications toward systems, blockchain, AI, and digital infrastructure.
- **Framework:** A conceptual model combining software, AI, blockchain, and advanced compute into integrated digital systems.

## 7. Risks & Sustainability
- **Computing Divide:** Infrastructure and skill gaps between urban centers and remote districts like Orakzai.
- **Green Computing:** Need for energy-efficient chips and sustainable data center cooling as compute demand surges.
- **Ethics:** Privacy, data ownership, and the responsible use of autonomous systems.
