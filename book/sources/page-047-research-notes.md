# Research Notes: Page 47 — Agriculture & Land

## Land Use & Geography
- **Total Area**: Approx. 1,538 sq km (153,800 hectares).
- **Cultivated Area**: Approx. 24,153 hectares (approx. 15.7% of total area).
- **Forest Cover**: Orakzai holds a significant portion of the forest area in the Merged Districts (approx. one-fourth of the total forest in Merged Areas).
- **Barren Land**: Estimated at 4.71% in some land-use plans.
- **Topographic Influence**: Agriculture is concentrated in the fertile valley floors (Mastura, Khanki, Khurmana) and on terraced slopes where feasible.

## Farming Systems
- **Rain-fed (Barani)**: Dominant system in many parts of the district.
- **Irrigated**: Concentrated near perennial streams (Toi) and using canals, wells, and tube wells.
- **Cropping Pattern**: 
  - **Mono-cropping**: Common in some areas (e.g., maize).
  - **Double-cropping**: Practiced where water availability and climate allow.

## Major Crops
1. **Wheat**: Primary winter (Rabi) crop; fundamental for food security.
2. **Maize**: Primary summer (Kharif) crop; widely cultivated.
3. **Rice**: Immense agricultural significance in specific watered tracts.
4. **Barley & Sorghum**: Documented historical and regional crops.
5. **Walnuts**: Abundant in Orakzai; a key non-cereal agricultural product.
6. **Vegetables & Fruits**: Seed potato, off-season vegetables, apricots, and walnuts are major contributors to the local agricultural economy.

## Livestock
- **Role**: Crucial component of the household economy and livelihood.
- **Categories**: Cattle, sheep, goats, poultry, buffalo, camels, and donkeys.
- **Income**: Dairy products generate significant income for rural families.
- **Population**: One recent count (2024 census metadata) indicates significant livestock holdings in the district.

## Challenges & Livelihood
- **Food Security**: High levels of food insecurity (estimated >60% in some assessments).
- **Infrastructure**: Limited access to modern agricultural machinery and certified seeds.
- **Climate Interaction**: Agriculture is highly sensitive to rainfall variability and temperature changes (Page 45).
