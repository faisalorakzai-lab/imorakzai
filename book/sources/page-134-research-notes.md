# Research Notes: Page 134 — Ethereum & Smart Contracts

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 134: Ethereum & Smart Contracts**.

## 1. Institutional & Network Status (August 2026)
- **Ethereum Staking:** As of August 2026, approximately **34% of the total ETH supply** is staked (~41.4 million ETH), supporting the network's Proof-of-Stake consensus.
- **Validators:** The network has surpassed **1.2 million active and pending validators**, maintaining high participation rates (~99.9%) and robust security.
- **Staking Rewards:** The average Staking Reward Rate (SRR) in mid-2026 is between **2.7% and 2.9% APR**, reflecting the maturity and saturation of the staking ecosystem.
- **The Merge:** The 2022 transition from Proof of Work to Proof of Stake remains the most significant architectural milestone, significantly reducing energy consumption.

## 2. Technical Ecosystem
- **Smart Contracts:** The Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) provides a deterministic execution environment for programmable logic written in Solidity and Vyper.
- **Layer-2 Scaling:** The roadmap focuses on a "rollup-centric" future, using Optimistic and ZK-rollups to scale transaction throughput while using Ethereum for settlement and security.
- **Token Standards:** ERC-20 (fungible), ERC-721 (non-fungible), and ERC-1155 (multi-token) remain the dominant standards for digital assets and collectibles.
- **DeFi & DAOs:** Decentralized Finance (DeFi) and Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) continue to evolve, with over 63% of institutional investors expressing interest in tokenized assets.

## 3. Pakistan's Blockchain Landscape (2026)
- **Developer Ecosystem:** Pakistan hosts over **113 blockchain development companies** (e.g., Renesis Tech, Debrid, Pluton Ltd.) and active developer communities.
- **Events:** Major events like **Entangled 2026** in Islamabad bring together hundreds of developers to design AI-integrated blockchain solutions.
- **Education:** Increased focus on blockchain security, smart contract auditing, and zero-knowledge proofs within the national technology sector.

## 4. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai & OKBOND
- **Faisal Orakzai:** Represented as a founder and computer scientist engaging with Ethereum-compatible infrastructure (Polygon L2).
- **OKBOND (Orakzai Bond):** A decentralized financial protocol deployed natively on **Polygon L2** in April 2026. It explores audited smart contracts, escrow mechanisms, and oracle integration.
- **Architecture:** OKBOND is documented as using verifiable token standards and audited contracts to provide decentralized asset infrastructure.

## 5. Regional Impact: Orakzai Sovereign Grid
- **Concept:** The "Orakzai Sovereign Grid" represents a broader vision for regional blockchain infrastructure, focusing on localized validation, data sovereignty, and decentralized services.
- **Integration:** The goal is to bridge traditional Orakzai governance (Jirga values) with modern decentralized execution (Smart Contracts).
