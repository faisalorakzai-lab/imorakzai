# Sources: Page 70 — Religion & Community Life

This document records the academic and ethnographic sources supporting **Page 70: Religion & Community Life** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Habib, M., Ullah, F., & Adil, M.** (2026). "Parental Perceptions of Disciplinary Practices in Deeni Madaris: A Qualitative Study from District Orakzai, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan". *Review of Crime, Peace and Society*.
   - **Details**: Research on the role of religious schools in the spiritual and moral development of children in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: The importance of Madrasa education in Orakzai community life.

2. **Alam, J., Ayaz, T. U., & Aziz, T.** (2024). "The role of local supporters in promoting social cohesion in District Orakzai: A qualitative study". *Human Nature Journal of Social Sciences*.
   - **Details**: Study identifying religious figures as key supporters of social stability in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: The role of religious leadership in community cohesion.

3. **Orakzai, S. B.** (2011). *Conflict in the Swat Valley of Pakistan: Pakhtun Culture and Peacebuilding*. (Related research on the role of faith in tribal peacebuilding).
   - **Details**: Academic work on the intersection of faith, culture, and conflict resolution in the region.
   - **Claim Supported**: The moral vocabulary of faith in social life.

4. **Brill / Academic Press**. (2023). "Editing Sufism: Contemporary Negotiations".
   - **Details**: Documents the presence and influence of Sufi lines of thought in the Orakzai region.
   - **Claim Supported**: Sectarian and spiritual diversity in Orakzai.

5. **Pashtoon, R.** (2020). "A Comprehensive Review on Zakāt's Role in Framing Social Security and Economic Sustainability".
   - **Details**: Analysis of how religious charity practices support community welfare in Pashtun contexts.
   - **Claim Supported**: The social function of Zakat and Sadaqah.
