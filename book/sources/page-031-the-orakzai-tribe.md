# Sources: Page 31 — The Orakzai Tribe

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 31: The Orakzai Tribe** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *Ōrakzī*.
   - **Publication**: Columbia University.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive scholarly entry on the Orakzai tribe, their geographic location, tribal sections, and Karlani classification.
   - **Claim Supported**: Tribal sections (Daulatzai, etc.), Karlani classification, and geographic heartland in Tirah.

2. **Frontier and Overseas Expeditions from India**: *Volume II: North-West Frontier Tribes*.
   - **Publication**: Intelligence Branch, Army Headquarters India, 1908.
   - **Details**: Detailed administrative and military record of the tribes between the Kabul and Gumal rivers, including Orakzai social organization and territory.
   - **Claim Supported**: Seven main sections (Ismailzai, Lashkarzai, Massuzai, Daulatzai, Muhammad Khel, Sturi Khel, Ali Khel), population estimates, and historical geography of Khanki/Mastura valleys.

3. **Gazetteer of the Kohat District, 1883-84**.
   - **Publication**: Punjab Government, 1884.
   - **Details**: Official colonial administrative record providing ethnographic descriptions, settlement patterns, and relations between the Orakzai and neighboring communities.
   - **Claim Supported**: Settlement patterns, agricultural practices, and relations with the Afridi and Bangash tribes.

4. **1911 Encyclopædia Britannica**: *Orakzai*.
   - **Publication**: Cambridge University Press.
   - **URL**: [https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/1911_Encyclop%C3%A6dia_Britannica/Orakzai](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/1911_Encyclop%C3%A6dia_Britannica/Orakzai)
   - **Details**: Historical overview of the tribe, their name's traditional meaning ("lost tribes"), and military history.
   - **Claim Supported**: Etymological interpretations and historical military expeditions (1855–1897).

5. **Karlani Pashtun Tribes**: *Utmankhel Tribal Confederacy Research*.
   - **URL**: [https://www.utmankhel.com/karlani/](https://www.utmankhel.com/karlani/)
   - **Details**: Modern scholarly framework for the Karlani confederacy and its constituent tribes.
   - **Claim Supported**: Inclusion of Orakzai within the Karlani tribal confederacy.
