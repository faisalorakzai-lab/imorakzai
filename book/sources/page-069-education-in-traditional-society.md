# Sources: Page 69 — Education in Traditional Society

This document records the academic and ethnographic sources supporting **Page 69: Education in Traditional Society** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Ahmad, K., et al.** (2026). "Tribal traditions and conservation perspectives of wild plants in Orakzai District, Pakistan". *Scientific Reports*.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive study on traditional ecological knowledge (TEK) and the intergenerational transmission of plant-based wisdom in Orakzai.
   - **Claim Supported**: Learning through observation and practice in agriculture and ecology.

2. **ResearchGate / Academic Study**. (2026). "Preservation and Dissemination of Indigenous Knowledge in Pashtuns' Culture: The Role of Hujra as a Learning Hub".
   - **Details**: Analysis of the Hujra as a multi-dimensional educational institution for social and political knowledge.
   - **Claim Supported**: The Hujra's role as a social academy for Pashtun youth.

3. **Ahmed, A. S.** (1980). *Pukhtun economy and society: Traditional structure and economic development in a tribal society*. Routledge.
   - **Details**: Ethnographic work by a former Political Agent in Orakzai, detailing tribal social structures and knowledge transmission.
   - **Claim Supported**: Traditional family structures and the role of elders in memory preservation.

4. **GSSR Journal / Human Nature Journal**. (2024-2025). "The Prominent Leftist Leaders of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa" / "Jirga System in FATA".
   - **Details**: Mentions of specific Orakzai madrasas (e.g., Anwar-ul-Islam) and the educational role of mosques.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical and contemporary religious education in Orakzai.

5. **Naseer, N.** (2026). "Early Warning by Women for Conflict Prevention". *Conflict Studies Quarterly*.
   - **Details**: Although focused on women, it documents the informal educational pathways for community safety and observation.
   - **Claim Supported**: Learning through observation and social participation.
