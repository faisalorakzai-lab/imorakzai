# Sources: Page 16 — Orakzai Within the Karlani System

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 16: Orakzai Within the Karlani System** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Encyclopaedia Iranica & Robert Nichols (*Oxford Research Encyclopedia*)**: *Karlani Genealogical Classification*.
   - **Authors**: Scholarly contributors to Encyclopaedia Iranica and Robert Nichols.
   - **Publisher**: Encyclopædia Iranica Foundation & Oxford University Press.
   - **Details**: Defines Karlani as a traditional genealogical framework rather than a centralized political entity or biological race.
   - **Claim Supported**: Karlani classification structure and distinction from tribal identity.

2. **Charles M. Kieffer (*Encyclopaedia Iranica*) & Daniel Balland (*Encyclopaedia Iranica*)**: *Associated Karlani Tribes*.
   - **Details**: Examination of major Karlani-associated communities (Afridi, Bangash, Orakzai, Khattak, Wazir, Mehsud) and their regional distribution.
   - **Claim Supported**: Multi-tribal association within the Karlani framework.

3. **Historical Ethnographies & Regional Geography**: *Orakzai Territory and Tirah Highlands*.
   - **Details**: Historical documentation of Orakzai settlement in the Tirah region, Kohat, Kurram, and neighboring frontier zones.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai historical homeland and regional geography.

4. **University of Malakand Research Journal of Pashto**: *Pashto Dialectology in the Northwest*.
   - **Publisher**: University of Malakand.
   - **Details**: Comparative lexical and phonetic studies of Pashto dialects (including Orakzai and Bangash varieties) within the Karlani dialect zone.
   - **Claim Supported**: Linguistic analysis and the distinction between dialect distribution and biological descent.
