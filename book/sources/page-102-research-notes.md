# Page 102 Research Notes — Tradition vs Modernity

## Scope and Methodology

Page 102 explores the complex relationship between tradition and modernity in Orakzai life, moving beyond binary "vs" framing toward a model of "Continuity through Change". The research focuses on how Orakzai communities adapt inherited practices to modern institutions, technology, and urban/global environments.

Key research areas:
1.  **Conceptual Framework**: Defining tradition and modernity not as opposites but as coexisting social processes.
2.  **Institutional Transformation**: The 2018 FATA merger as a catalyst for shifting from customary to constitutional governance.
3.  **Cultural Adaptation**: How language (Pashto), social spaces (Hujra), and mediation (Jirga) adapt to digital and urban contexts.
4.  **Generational Dynamics**: The role of youth as a bridge between ancestral memory and technological skills.
5.  **Evidence Boundaries**: Strictly distinguishing between Orakzai-specific, Pashtun-wide, and broader Pakistani social trends.

## Key Research Findings

### 1. Tradition and Modernity Interplay
- **Sociological Analysis**: Research indicates that modernization in tribal contexts is not uniform. It varies by socio-demographic factors and involves a "traditionalization" of certain aspects as a response to change (JCAS, 2026).
- **Adaptation over Erasure**: Modernity brings formal education and technology, but traditional values like *Melmastia* (hospitality) and *Hujra* (social complex) often persist in adapted forms (IHU, 2024).

### 2. The FATA Merger (2018) and Social Change
- **Constitutional Integration**: The 25th Amendment replaced the Frontier Crimes Regulation (FCR) with formal human rights and provincial institutions (JDSS, 2024).
- **Impact on Women**: The merger is expected to provide basic human rights to tribal women, though traditional norms continue to influence social advancement (JDSS, 2024; SSRN).
- **Social Cohesion**: Qualitative studies in Orakzai highlight the role of local supporters in promoting social cohesion during this transition (HNJSS, 2024).

### 3. Digital Transformation and Youth
- **Social Media Influence**: Digital communication significantly affects youth culture, changing how information is shared while also providing tools for cultural preservation (Frontiers, 2025; PJP-EU).
- **FM Radio in Orakzai**: Radio remains a critical tool for peacebuilding and cultural narrative in Orakzai, Kurram, and Waziristan (UWJSS, 2023).
- **Digital Literacy**: Community-based training for youth (ages 16-22) is emerging as a way to strengthen digital competencies while maintaining community ties (JACI, 2026).

### 4. Language and Identity
- **Linguistic Disruption**: Research on "Pakistanizing Pashtun" discusses how Pashto language and culture navigate national identity and modernization (ProQuest, 2023).
- **Proverbs and Values**: Globalization influences Pashtun cultural values, but proverbs remain a repository of intergenerational knowledge (PJSEL, 2023).

## Research Gaps and Limitations
- **Orakzai-Specific Longitudinal Data**: Limited data on specific village-level cultural shifts post-2018.
- **Digital Hujra Evidence**: While conceptualized, the actual social impact of digital groups replacing physical Hujras in Orakzai requires more fieldwork.
- **Gendered Modernity**: The diverse experiences of Orakzai women in modern urban vs. rural settings are under-documented.
