# Sources: Page 13 — Major Pashtun Tribes

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 13: Major Pashtun Tribes** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Encyclopaedia Iranica & Encyclopædia Britannica**: *Pashtun Tribal Confederations and Regional Groups*.
   - **Authors**: Historians and ethnographers specializing in Afghan and South Asian tribal studies.
   - **Publisher**: Encyclopædia Iranica Foundation, New York.
   - **Details**: Comprehensive documentation of major tribal groups (Durrani, Ghilzai, Yusufzai, Afridi, Khattak, Orakzai, Wazir, Mehsud, etc.) and their historical distributions.
   - **Claim Supported**: Diversity and historical presence of major Pashtun tribes.

2. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *Durrani Confederation and Tribal Structure*.
   - **Details**: Traces the political rise of the Durrani (Abdali) confederation and its constituent branches (Popalzai, Barakzai, Alikozai, Achakzai) under Ahmad Shah Durrani.
   - **Claim Supported**: Durrani confederation history and internal organization.

3. **Academic Scholarship on Khyber Pakhtunkhwa**: *Yusufzai Migration and Regional History*.
   - **Details**: Analyzes the historical expansion and settlement of the Yusufzai in Swat, Buner, and surrounding northwestern regions.
   - **Claim Supported**: Yusufzai historical geography and development.

4. **Encyclopaedia Iranica**: *Afridi, Khattak and Frontier Tribes*.
   - **Details**: Documents the strategic geopolitical position of the Afridi around the Khyber Pass and the literary-political legacy of the Khattak tribe, including Khushal Khan Khattak.
   - **Claim Supported**: Afridi and Khattak historical roles.

5. **Historical Ethnographies**: *Orakzai Tribal Organization and Tirah Geography*.
   - **Details**: Chronicles the traditional territorial boundaries, subdivisions, and historical role of the Orakzai tribe within the Karlani tribal lineage.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai tribal position within the wider Pashtun landscape.
