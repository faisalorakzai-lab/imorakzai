# Research Notes: Page 85 — Orakzai Agency

## Core Concept
- **Orakzai Agency**: A former administrative unit of FATA (1973–2018).
- **Landlocked Geography**: Unique among FATA agencies for not sharing a direct international border with Afghanistan.
- **Administrative Evolution**: Transition from Frontier Region (FR) Kohat to an independent Agency, and eventually to a District of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

## Establishment Date Comparison
The formal establishment of Orakzai Agency involves several key dates depending on the source:
- **November 3, 1973**: The President of Pakistan announced the creation of the Agency.
- **November 30, 1973**: Some administrative records cite this as the official establishment date.
- **December 1, 1973**: Formally inaugurated and began functioning as an independent administrative unit. Prime Minister Zulfikar Ali Bhutto announced it at a grand tribal jirga.

## Geography and Boundaries
- **Area**: Approximately 1,538 sq km.
- **North**: Khyber Agency.
- **West**: Kurram Agency.
- **South**: Kohat and Hangu Districts.
- **East**: FR Kohat (Peshawar/Kohat borderlands).
- **Status**: Landlocked highland territory. While near the frontier, it relies on Kurram and Khyber for access to the Durand Line.

## Administration and Governance
- **Political Agent (PA)**: Executive and judicial head under the FATA framework. Historically based in Hangu due to Orakzai's geography.
- **Assistant Political Agents (APAs)**: Subordinate officers managing sub-divisions (Upper and Lower Orakzai).
- **Frontier Crimes Regulation (FCR)**: The legal code governing the Agency, emphasizing collective responsibility and indirect rule.
- **Maliks and Elders**: Government-recognized intermediaries who facilitated communication between the PA and the tribal sections.

## 2017 Census Data (Orakzai Agency)
- **Total Population**: 254,303
- **Male**: 127,564 | **Female**: 126,728 | **Transgender**: 11
- **Urban Population**: 0 (Entirely rural)
- **Households**: 31,420
- **Source**: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS), 2017 Census.

## Transition to District (2018)
- **25th Amendment**: Repealed Article 247, merging FATA into KP.
- **Orakzai District**: The Agency was renamed and integrated into the provincial administrative and judicial system.
- **Institutional Shift**: Transition from Political Agent to Deputy Commissioner (DC) and the extension of regular courts and police.

## Author's Reflection Themes
- **Beyond the Name**: The Agency was a layer of state authority over a pre-existing tribal landscape.
- **Geography as Destiny**: Being landlocked shaped Orakzai's unique trade and political relationships.
- **Continuity**: While names change (Agency to District), the kinship networks and cultural identity of the Orakzai people remain the core of the region.
