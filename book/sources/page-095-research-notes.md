# Research Notes: Page 95 — Orakzai in Lahore

## Core Concept
- **The Historic Metropolis**: Lahore serves as Punjab's primary urban center, attracting Orakzai individuals for high-tier education, specialized healthcare, and commercial opportunities.
- **Multilingual Adaptation**: Migrant Pashto-speaking families in Lahore navigate a complex linguistic environment (Pashto, Punjabi, Urdu, English), negotiating their identity through language policy (Khalid & Khan, 2026).
- **Evidence Distinction**: High confidence in Lahore's urban expansion and general Pashtun migration patterns; specific Orakzai presence is verified through individual student and professional records.

## Lahore as an Urban Center
- **Roles**: Historic city, provincial capital, educational hub, commercial center, and healthcare destination.
- **Urban Expansion**:
  - **1990–2021**: Built-up area in Lahore increased from **455.45 km²** to **807.40 km²** (Ghafoor et al., 2023).
  - **1990–2020**: Built-up land relative change found to be **23.52%** at the cost of vegetation and barren land.
- **Migration Drivers**: Socioeconomic determinants include economic conditions, employment opportunities, and education (Ghafoor & Akbar, 2022).

## Orakzai Presence in Lahore
- **Education**: Verified Orakzai students and alumni in major institutions:
  - **University of the Punjab**: Documented students in Politics and International Relations, Computer Science, and Mass Communication.
  - **LUMS**: Fahad Orakzai (Student/Entrepreneur, candidate for Treasurer).
  - **GCU Lahore**: Muhammad Abdullah Orakzai (Intermediate), and other "Old Ravians."
  - **UMT**: Huzaifa Waqar Orakzai (Student).
- **Professional Class**: Documented presence in business, technology, and legal sectors.
- **Migration Patterns**: Staged migration (Homeland -> Regional Hub -> Lahore) and direct migration for specialized services.

## Language and Identity
- **Family Language Policy (2026 Study)**:
  - Pashto is the desired language at home for migrant families.
  - Urdu and English are used for utility, social mobility, and opportunity outside the home.
  - Multilingualism is a resource for constructing and negotiating identity.
- **Bilingual Identity (2022 Study)**: Young migrant Pashtuns use code-switching to construct identity in the urban Punjabi context.

## Research Gaps
- **Population Statistics**: No official census breakdown for the Orakzai sub-tribe within the Lahore District.
- **Neighborhood Concentration**: Orakzai presence is dispersed; no single "Orakzai neighborhood" is documented.
