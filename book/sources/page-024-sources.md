# Sources: Page 24 — Hujra — The Community Space

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 24: Hujra — The Community Space** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Akbar S. Ahmed**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society: Traditional Structure and Economic Development in a Tribal Society*.
   - **Author**: Akbar S. Ahmed.
   - **Publication**: Routledge, 1980 / republished 2011.
   - **Details**: Classic anthropological study of Pashtun social structures, examining the role of the Hujra as a central communal guesthouse and institution of tribal life.
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of the Hujra as a communal gathering and hospitality space.

2. **Fredrik Barth**: *Political Leadership Among Swat Pathans*.
   - **Author**: Fredrik Barth.
   - **Publication**: London School of Economics Monographs on Social Anthropology, No. 19, Athlone Press, 1959.
   - **Details**: Examines social organization, political alliances, and village life among Pashtuns in the Swat region.
   - **Claim Supported**: Village social organization and communal interaction.

3. **M. Jamil Hanifi**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica* entries on Pashtun institutions and social organization.
   - **Author**: M. Jamil Hanifi.
   - **Publication**: *Encyclopaedia Iranica*, Columbia University.
   - **Details**: Authoritative reference work on Iranian and Pashtun civilization, custom, and society.
   - **Claim Supported**: Historical context of Pashtun communal institutions and cultural memory.
