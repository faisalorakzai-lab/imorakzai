# Research Notes: Page 118 — Software & Digital Infrastructure

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 118: Software & Digital Infrastructure**.

## 1. Digital Infrastructure Framework
- **Physical Layer:** Submarine cables (AAE-1, PEACE, SMW-5, SMW-6 in progress), fiber-optic backbones, and cell towers.
- **Network Layer:** Internet Exchange Points (IXPs) and core network routing.
- **Compute & Storage:** Data centers (market growing to ~53MW by 2030) and cloud platforms (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS).
- **Platform Layer:** Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) including NADRA (Identity) and Raast (Payments).
- **Application Layer:** Software products, services, and AI-driven applications.

## 2. Connectivity & Networks (2025–2026)
- **Submarine Cables:** Pakistan is connected via systems like **AAE-1, PEACE, IMEWE, SMW-4, SMW-5**, and the newly launched **SMW-6** (2025/26).
- **Mobile Landscape:** Jazz leads with ~36% market share, followed by PTCL/Telenor (merger completion Jan 2026) and Zong.
- **Internet Exchange:** Local traffic is increasingly routed through domestic IXPs to reduce latency and international bandwidth costs.

## 3. Data Centers & Cloud
- **Market Size:** Valued at ~US$1.5 billion in 2025, with capacity projected to reach 53.30 MW by 2030.
- **Cloud Adoption:** Startups and enterprises are shifting toward cloud-first models, though energy resilience and cooling costs remain significant structural challenges.

## 4. Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)
- **NADRA:** Provides the foundational identity layer for verification and authentication across public and private sectors.
- **Raast:** The State Bank of Pakistan's instant payment system, active and facilitating real-time P2P and merchant transactions.
- **Government Digitization:** Target to make all federal and provincial payments fully digital by the end of 2026.

## 5. Software Industry & Exports
- **Service vs. Product:** Pakistan's IT exports (reaching US$4.6B in FY26) are primarily service-driven, though a growing SaaS ecosystem is emerging.
- **Developer Ecosystem:** Increasing use of open-source tools, cloud-native architectures (microservices, serverless), and AI integration.

## 6. Case Study: Faisal Orakzai — Building with Software
- **Perspective:** Entrepreneurial focus on digital infrastructure, blockchain, and software systems.
- **Projects:** Documented associations with **OkzByte Hub**, **Orakzai Group**, **Orakzai Bond**, and **Shamim Forever**.
- **Context:** Illustrates the bridge between local identity and global technical systems.

## 7. Structural Challenges
- **Infrastructure Gap:** Regional inequality in connectivity (urban vs. remote districts like Orakzai).
- **Resilience:** Power supply reliability and cybersecurity threats to critical infrastructure.
- **Brain Drain:** Retention of high-level technical talent in the software engineering sector.
