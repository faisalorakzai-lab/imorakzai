# Research Notes: Page 136 — Tokenization of Real-World Assets (RWA)

This document records the research findings and methodological decisions for **Page 136: Tokenization of Real-World Assets (RWA)**.

## 1. Global Market Status (August 2026)
- **Market Size:** The global RWA tokenization market has surpassed **$60 billion** across 7,000+ products as of July 2026, according to industry reports (Yahoo Finance, Brickken).
- **Institutional Ready:** Tokenized Treasuries have reached **$12.88 billion**, with private credit and short-term government securities leading institutional adoption.
- **Growth Trend:** Total value grew to over **$24 billion** by February 2026, representing a 266% growth since 2025.
- **Economic Outlook:** The IMF and BIS (2026) describe tokenization as a fundamental reconfiguration of financial architecture, moving beyond crypto hype into institutional rails.

## 2. Technical & Legal Framework
- **Core Concept:** Tokenization connects legal and economic rights in the physical world with programmable digital infrastructure.
- **The Stack:** Requires an integrated approach covering the Real-World Asset, Legal Structure, Custody/Registry, Identity/Compliance, Smart Contract, and Application layers.
- **Oracle Dependency:** RWAs rely heavily on oracles to bridge the gap between blockchain state and external data (property values, commodity existence).
- **Atomic Settlement:** The ability to coordinate asset delivery and payment (DvP) within a single transaction, reducing settlement risk.

## 3. Pakistan's RWA Landscape (2026)
- **Real Estate Shifting:** Pakistan's property market is transitioning from speculation to smart investing. Research from PIDE (Pakistan Institute of Development Economics) highlights the benefits of tokenization for inclusion and liquidity.
- **PropTech Convention 2026:** Major industry events in Pakistan are focusing on fractional ownership and blockchain-based property registries to address land record transparency.
- **Diaspora Capital:** Potential for tokenization to provide transparent mechanisms for overseas Pakistanis to invest in national infrastructure, agriculture, and real estate.
- **Agricultural RWAs:** Exploration of warehouse receipts and commodity financing through digital representations to improve financing efficiency for farmers.
