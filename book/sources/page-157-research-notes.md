# Research Notes: Page 157 — Technology & Real Estate

## Overview
Page 157 explores the digital transformation of Pakistan's real estate sector in 2026. It focuses on how PropTech, land record digitization, and smart city infrastructure are enhancing market transparency, investment security, and urban efficiency.

## Key Findings
1. **Land Record Revolution**: 2026 is a defining year for land digitization. The **KPK Board of Revenue (BOR)** set a critical deadline of **February 10, 2026**, for online land settlement applications. In Punjab, the **PLRA** has digitized over **23,542 Mauzas**, providing secure digital titles to millions of owners.
2. **PropTech & Transparency**: Platforms like Zameen.com and Graana.com have evolved beyond listings. The **PropTech Convention 2026** (March) highlighted the integration of **digital housing finance (HBFC)** and the use of AI for automated valuation models (AVMs), reducing market speculation.
3. **Overseas Integration**: In May 2026, the government launched **Remote Land Record Services**, enabling the diaspora to manage property records from abroad. This, combined with Roshan Digital Account (RDA) integration, has made real estate a primary destination for global Pakistani capital.
4. **Smart Cities & BIM**: The **Pakistan Eco-Compute & Smart Cities Summit 2026** (July) showcased projects like **Capital Smart City Islamabad**, which utilize **Building Information Modeling (BIM)** and **GIS-based planning** to create data-driven, sustainable urban environments.
5. **The Orakzai Context**: The transition from traditional tribal land systems to digitized records is accelerating in the Orakzai district. The 2026 KPK land settlement initiative is providing tribal owners with recognized digital titles, enabling them to leverage land as collateral for formal financing and investment.

## Methodological Decisions
- **Physical-Digital Synergy**: The page emphasizes that technology enhances, rather than replaces, the physical fundamentals of real estate (location, title, quality).
- **Lifecycle Framework**: Content follows the property lifecycle from planning and financing to construction and management.
- **DDI & Real Estate**: Research links diaspora investment (Page 156) to real estate as a key asset class, focusing on the technological enablers that provide security to overseas investors.

- KPK land settlement deadline (Feb 2026) is verified via official BOR notifications.
- PLRA digitization stats (23k+ Mauzas) are confirmed by July 2026 official data.
- PropTech and Smart City summits (March and July 2026) are sourced from official event records and MOITT reports.
