# Sources: Page 59 — Festivals & Celebrations

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 59: Festivals & Celebrations** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **KP Culture and Tourism Authority (KPCTA)**: *Orakzai Spring Festival Report*. (June 2024).
   - **Details**: Official documentation of the three-day Jashan-e-Baharan festival held in Orakzai District, featuring sports and cultural stalls.
   - **Claim Supported**: 2024 Spring Mela activities (paragliding, wrestling, stone lifting, jeep races).

2. **Ahmed, A. S.**: *Pukhtun Economy and Society*. (1980).
   - **Details**: Ethnographic study discussing the role of hospitality and communal gatherings in tribal society.
   - **Claim Supported**: Pashtun-wide hospitality and community gathering traditions.

3. **Hussain, K.**: *Consciousness, Gender and Identity: A Critical Study of Pashto Verbal Folkloric Arts*. (2025).
   - **Details**: Discusses the social role of the Hujra in Pashtun culture as a space for ritual and community celebration.
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of Hujra in festivals and Eid celebrations.

4. **Dawn / The Express Tribune**: *Reports on Orakzai Spring Festival*. (June 8, 2024).
   - **Details**: Media coverage of the 2024 festival, confirming specific sporting events and community participation.
   - **Claim Supported**: Modern organized festival in Orakzai; specific sports list.

5. **Ahmad, M. F.**: *Pashtun cultural revival in diaspora*. (2025).
   - **Details**: Discusses the use of traditional clothing for weddings and cultural festivals.
   - **Claim Supported**: Role of clothing in cultural festivals.
