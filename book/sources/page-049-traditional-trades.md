# Sources: Page 49 — Traditional Trades

This document records the academic and historical sources supporting **Page 49: Traditional Trades** in *I’M ORAKZAI*.

## Primary References & Academic Citations

1. **Pakistan Almanac**: *Khyber Pakhtunkhwa-Orakzai Profile*.
   - **Details**: Specifically identifies mazri palm handicrafts, embroidery on clothes, and silkworm raising as economic activities in Orakzai TD.
   - **Claim Supported**: Orakzai-specific handicraft evidence.

2. **SM Khan et al. (2020)**: *Mazri (Nannorrhops ritchiana (Griff) Aitch.): a remarkable source of manufacturing traditional handicrafts goods and utensils in Pakistan*.
   - **Details**: Academic study documenting the use of mazri leaves for mats, baskets, hand fans, and other household items in Orakzai and surrounding regions.
   - **Claim Supported**: Mazri palm craft techniques and products.

3. **UN Women Pakistan**: *Status of Women and Girls in Orakzai District (2020)*.
   - **Details**: Documents women's participation in the agricultural economy and handicrafts as a source of household income.
   - **Claim Supported**: Gender roles in traditional trades.

4. **Civil Secretariat FATA**: *FATA Sustainable Development Plan 2007-2015*.
   - **Details**: Discusses the broader economic infrastructure and the role of small-scale businesses and mining in the region.
   - **Claim Supported**: Economic context of traditional trades.

5. **Deputy Commissioner Orakzai**: *Official Communiqués (2024)*.
   - **Details**: Reports on the local preparation and distribution of mazri baskets as an eco-friendly initiative.
   - **Claim Supported**: Contemporary status of mazri palm crafts.

## Evidence Hierarchy & Categorization

- **Orakzai-Specific**: Mazri palm crafts (baskets, mats, fans), embroidery, silkworm raising, coal mining.
- **Regional FATA/Pashtun**: Wood-based products, furniture, blacksmithing (agricultural tools).
- **Conceptual Models**: Mixed-livelihood seasonal cycles; craft-to-market value chains.
